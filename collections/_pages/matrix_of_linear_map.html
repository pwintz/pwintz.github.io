Instructions

Let \(V\) be a vector space with a basis \(\mathcal{V} = \{\mathbf{v}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{v}_n\}\), and let \(W\) be a vector space with a basis \(\mathcal{W} = \{\mathbf{w}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{w}_m\}\). For a linear map \(T : V \to W\), the matrix of \(T\) relative to \(\mathcal{V}\) and \(\mathcal{W}\) is the \(m \times n\) matrix \[ A = \begin{bmatrix} [T(\mathbf{v}_1)]_{\mathcal{W}} & [T(\mathbf{v}_2)]_{\mathcal{W}} & \cdots & [T(\mathbf{v}_n)]_{\mathcal{W}} \end{bmatrix}. \] It transforms coordinates: \([T(\mathbf{x})]_{\mathcal{W}} = A[\mathbf{x}]_{\mathcal{V}}\) for every \(\mathbf{x}\) in \(V\).

Each problem has three parts, each worked in steps:

  1. Find the matrix \(A\) of \(T\).
    1. Apply \(T\) to each basis vector \(\mathbf{v}_j\) in \(\mathcal{V}\).
    2. Find the coordinates \([T(\mathbf{v}_j)]_{\mathcal{W}}\) of each result relative to \(\mathcal{W}\).
    3. Place the coordinate vectors into the columns of \(A\).
  2. Use \(A\) to find \([T(\mathbf{x})]_{\mathcal{W}}\) for a vector \(\mathbf{x}\).
    1. Find the coordinate vector \([\mathbf{x}]_{\mathcal{V}}\) of \(\mathbf{x}\) relative to \(\mathcal{V}\).
    2. Use \(A\) to find the coordinates \([T(\mathbf{x})]_{\mathcal{W}} = A[\mathbf{x}]_{\mathcal{V}}\).
  3. Determine whether \(T\) is onto and whether \(T\) is one-to-one.
    1. Find the rank and the nullity of \(A\).
    2. Use them to determine whether \(T\) is onto and whether \(T\) is one-to-one.

Part 3 uses two theorems from lecture (Module C, “Onto Functions and Rank” and “One-to-One Functions and Nullity”). For a linear map \(T : V \to W\) with matrix \(A_T\) relative to any bases, \[ T \text{ is onto} \iff \operatorname{rank} A_T = \dim W, \qquad T \text{ is one-to-one} \iff \operatorname{null} A_T = 0. \]
