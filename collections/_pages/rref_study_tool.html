Instructions

Type the entries of a matrix below to check whether it is in row echelon form (REF), reduced row echelon form (RREF), or neither. Every condition that fails is listed along with the entries that break it; point at a reason to highlight those entries in the matrix. For a matrix in REF, the page also gives the rank and nullity and the number of solutions of the system, with the reason.

To practice, switch to Quiz: the page shows a random matrix, and you decide which conditions it satisfies and which form it's in, then check your answers.

Entries can be integers, decimals, or fractions such as -2, 0.5, or 1/3. Blank entries count as 0. Use the arrow keys or Enter to move between entries (Shift+Enter moves up).

To practice row reducing a matrix step by step, try Gaussian Elimination Practice.
