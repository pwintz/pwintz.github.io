Project this page during an exam to show how much time is left. It shows whole minutes only, because a ticking seconds count distracts students. The minutes are rounded up, so “5 min” means that more than 4 minutes are left. The display turns amber with 15 minutes left and orange-red with 5 minutes left.

Extensions

No extensions.

    Settings and extensions are saved in this browser, so reloading the page keeps the timer. Extensions are cleared on the next day. Double-click the display to switch full screen on or off.
