The span of vectors $\mathbf{v}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{v}_k$ is the set of every linear combination $c_1 \mathbf{v}_1 + \cdots + c_k \mathbf{v}_k$. The shaded region below is the span. The black arrow is the linear combination chosen by the sliders, drawn tip to tail with dashed arrows.

The span of the vectors is the column space of the matrix $A$ whose columns are the vectors. Row reducing $A$ shows which columns are needed.
