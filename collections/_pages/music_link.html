Paste a link to a song, album, or artist from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, YouTube, Deezer, Qobuz, SoundCloud, or Bandcamp. Check that the right item was found, then share the link this page makes. Whoever opens it can play it in the service they use.

This is saved only in this browser. When a link opens your default service automatically, you can cancel it, or open the same link again within 10 minutes to choose a different service.

How it works

Details come from the public Apple Music (iTunes) and Deezer catalogs. Apple Music and Deezer links point straight to the matching item. Spotify, TIDAL, Qobuz, and the other services don't let websites look up their catalogs without an account, so those links open a search for the item, unless you pasted a link from that service or added one under "Add a link from another service". Qobuz is looked up through Jina Reader, a free service that fetches Qobuz's public search page, since Qobuz doesn't allow this page to read it directly; when that fails, you can add the Qobuz link yourself. TIDAL and Amazon Music links can't be pasted here because those services don't share song details publicly. The share link holds the item's details itself, so it keeps working without looking anything up again.
