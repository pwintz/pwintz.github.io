In mathematics, many terms are similar but not interchangeable. This dictionary groups easily confused terms, defines each one, and explains the distinction with examples to illustrate the difference. This work was inspired by the Dictionary of Fine Distinctions: Nuances, Niceties, and Subtle Shades of Meaning by Eli Burnstein.

  • Distinctions: groups of easily confused terms, with the differences between them.
  • Definitions: definitions of each term at several levels, from elementary to graduate.
  • Examples: examples and non-examples of the terms.
