A syllabus records dozens of decisions about how a course runs, and most are made in a hurry or copied from the last syllabus. This tool walks through those decisions for a STEM course, with the pros and cons of each option, and writes a syllabus from your answers as you go. Start from an example to see a finished syllabus, then change what doesn't fit your course. Your answers are saved in this browser.
