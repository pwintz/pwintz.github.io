# tagline: Mathematician. Teacher. Musician. Artist.

biography:
  Paul K. Wintz is a lecturer in Applied Mathematics at the University of California, Santa Cruz (UCSC).
  He received a B.S. degree in Engineering Physics with a minor in Music from Westmont College in 2016, an M.S. degree in Scientific Computing and Applied Mathematics from UCSC in 2020, and a PhD in Applied Mathematics from UCSC in 2025, where he researched hybrid control systems with Ricardo G. Sanfelice.
  Dr. Wintz was a finalist for the Best Student Paper award at the 2022 American Control Conference.
  He has previously taught undergraduate mathematics at Hartnell College.

research-interests:
  - Hybrid dynamical systems
  - Uniting feedback for exploiting uncertified controllers 
  - Formal methods for verification of dynamical systems and control systems
  - Computationally-constrained cyber-physical systems
  - Graph-based asymptotic and reachability analysis of hybrid systems
  - Model predictive control
