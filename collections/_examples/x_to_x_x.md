title: '$x \mapsto x \abs{x}$'
level: high-school
is: [differentiable, continuously_differentiable]
is_not: [smooth, analytic]
why:
  continuously_differentiable: >-
    $x \mapsto x \abs{x}$ is continuously differentiable, and so differentiable, because its derivative $2 \abs{x}$ is continuous.
  differentiable: >-
    $x \mapsto x \abs{x}$ is not smooth or analytic, because $2 \abs{x}$ is not differentiable at $0$.
---
