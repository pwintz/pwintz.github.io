---
published: false
title: '$\setdef{v \suchthat v_1 \leq 0}$, for the closed unit disk at $(1, 0)$'
level: graduate
is: [
  contingent_cone,
  clarke_tangent_cone
]
is_not: [tangent_space]
why:
  tangent_space: >-
    $\setdef{v \suchthat v_1 \leq 0}$ is not a tangent space, because it is not a vector space: $(1, 0)$ points out of the disk.
  clarke_tangent_cone: >-
    $\setdef{v \suchthat v_1 \leq 0}$ is the contingent cone and the Clarke tangent cone of the disk at $(1, 0)$, which agree because the disk is convex.
---
