title: 'The closed intervals $[a, b]$ in $\reals$, for the standard topology on $\reals$'
level: undergraduate
is_not: [topological_basis]
why:
  topological_basis: >-
    The closed intervals are not a basis for the standard topology on $\reals$, because a basis for a topology must consist of open sets, and $[a, b]$ is not open.
    Moreover, the topology generated by the closed intervals contains every one-point set $\setdef{a} = [a, a]$, so the generated topology is the discrete topology, not the standard topology.
---
