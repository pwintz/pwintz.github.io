title: 'The Pythagorean theorem'
detail: >-
  For the legs $a$ and $b$ and the hypotenuse $c$ of every right triangle,

  $$a^2 + b^2 = c^2.$$
level: high-school
is: [theorem]
is_not: [conjecture]
why:
  theorem: >-
    The Pythagorean theorem is a theorem, because it is a proved main result of plane geometry.
  conjecture: >-
    The Pythagorean theorem is not a conjecture, because it has a proof.
