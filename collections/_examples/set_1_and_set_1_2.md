title: '$\setdef{1}$ and $\setdef{1, 2}$'
level: elementary
is: [subset, proper_subset]
is_not: [element_of]
why:
  element_of: >-
    $\setdef{1}$ is not an element of $\setdef{1, 2}$, because the elements of $\setdef{1, 2}$ are $1$ and $2$.
  proper_subset: >-
    $\setdef{1}$ is a proper subset, and so a subset, of $\setdef{1, 2}$, because its only element, $1$, is in $\setdef{1, 2}$, and $2$ is not in $\setdef{1}$.
