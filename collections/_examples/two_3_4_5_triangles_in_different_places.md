---
title: 'Two $3$-$4$-$5$ triangles in different places'
level: high-school
is: [
  congruent,
  equivalent
]
is_not: [equal]
why:
  equal: >-
    The two triangles are not equal, because they are different sets of points.
  equivalent: >-
    The two triangles are congruent, and so equivalent, because a rotation and a translation move one onto the other.
---
