---
title: 'Cruise control holding 100 km/h on hilly roads'
level: elementary
is: [regulation]
is_not: [tracking]
why:
  regulation: >-
    Holding $100$ km/h is regulation because the set point is constant, and the hills act as disturbances.
  tracking: >-
    Holding $100$ km/h is not tracking, because the reference does not change.
---
