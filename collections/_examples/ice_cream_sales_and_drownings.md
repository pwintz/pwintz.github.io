title: 'The daily ice cream sales and the daily number of drownings in a beach town, over a year'
level: high-school
values:
  correlation: 'Positive'
  causation: 'Neither causes the other'
  statistical_independence: 'Dependent'
why:
  correlation: >-
    Ice cream sales and drownings in a beach town are positively correlated, because both are high in summer and low in winter.
  causation: >-
    Ice cream sales do not cause drownings, and drownings do not cause ice cream sales; hot weather causes both, so hot weather is a confounder.
  statistical_independence: >-
    Ice cream sales and drownings in a beach town are dependent, because knowing that ice cream sales are high makes it more probable that the day is hot, and so that drownings are high.
