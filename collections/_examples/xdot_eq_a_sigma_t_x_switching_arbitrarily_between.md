title: '$\dot{x} = A_{\sigma(t)} x$, switching arbitrarily between two matrices'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  switched_system,
  hybrid_system
]
why:
  switched_system: >-
    $\dot{x} = A_{\sigma(t)} x$ is a switched system because the switching signal $\sigma$ selects which matrix is active, and $x$ is continuous.
  hybrid_system: >-
    $\dot{x} = A_{\sigma(t)} x$ is a hybrid system, with $\sigma$ treated as a discrete state.
---
