title: "Latitude and longitude on the surface of the Earth, without the poles"
level: high-school
is: [coordinate_system]
is_not: [vector_basis]
why:
  coordinate_system: >-
    Latitude and longitude form a coordinate system on the surface of the Earth without the poles, because each such point has exactly one latitude between $-90°$ and $90°$ and one longitude in $(-180°, 180°]$.
    At a pole, every longitude names the same point.
  vector_basis: >-
    Latitude and longitude are not a basis, because the surface of the Earth is not a vector space, so there are no basis vectors to combine.
---
