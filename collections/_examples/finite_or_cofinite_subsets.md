---
title: 'The subsets of $\reals$ that are finite or have a finite complement'
level: graduate
is_not: [sigma_algebra, topology]
why:
  sigma_algebra: >-
    The finite and cofinite subsets of $\reals$ do not form a σ-algebra.
    They are closed under complements and finite unions, but not countable unions: the union of the one-point sets $\setdef{n}$, for $n \in \nats$, is $\nats$, which is neither finite nor cofinite.
  topology: >-
    The finite and cofinite subsets of $\reals$ do not form a topology, because they are not closed under arbitrary unions: the union of the one-point sets $\setdef{n}$, for $n \in \nats$, is $\nats$, which is neither finite nor cofinite.
---
