title: 'The subsets of $\reals$ that are finite or have a finite complement'
level: graduate
is_not: [sigma_algebra, topology]
why:
  sigma_algebra: >-
    It is closed under complements and finite unions, but not countable ones: the union of the one-point sets $\lbrace n \rbrace$, for $n \in \mathbb{N}$, is $\mathbb{N}$, which is neither finite nor cofinite.
  topology: >-
    The same union shows that it is not closed under arbitrary unions.
---
