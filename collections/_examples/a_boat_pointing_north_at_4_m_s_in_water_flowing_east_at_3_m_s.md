---
title: 'A boat pointing north at $4$ m/s in water flowing east at $3$ m/s'
level: high-school
description: >-
  A boat points north and moves north at $4$ m/s relative to the water, and the water flows east at $3$ m/s.
values:
  heading: '$0^\circ$'
  course: 'About $37^\circ$'
why:
  heading: >-
    The heading of the boat pointing north at $4$ m/s in water flowing east at $3$ m/s is $0^\circ$ because the nose of the boat points north.
  course: >-
    The boat pointing north at $4$ m/s in water flowing east at $3$ m/s moves $4$ m/s north and $3$ m/s east, so the course $\chi$ of the boat satisfies $\tan(\chi) = 3/4$.
    So the course is

    $$\chi = \arctan(3/4) \approx 37^\circ.$$
---
