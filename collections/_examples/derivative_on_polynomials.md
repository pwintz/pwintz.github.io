title: 'The derivative $p \mapsto p^{\prime}$ on polynomials with real coefficients'
level: undergraduate
is: [derivation]
why:
  derivation: >-
    The derivative $p \mapsto p^{\prime}$ on polynomials is a derivation, because it is linear and satisfies the product rule $(p q)^{\prime} = p^{\prime} q + p q^{\prime}$.
