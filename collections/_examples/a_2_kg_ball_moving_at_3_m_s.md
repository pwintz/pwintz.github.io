title: 'A $2$ kg ball moving east at $3$ m/s'
level: high-school
values:
  momentum: '$p = 6$ kg·m/s, east'
  energy: '$K = 9$ J'
why:
  momentum: >-
    The momentum of a $2$ kg ball moving east at $3$ m/s is the mass times the velocity, $p = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$ kg·m/s, pointing east.
  energy: >-
    The kinetic energy of a $2$ kg ball moving at $3$ m/s is $K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 3^2 = 9$ J, which has no direction.
