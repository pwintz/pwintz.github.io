title: '$\nabla f(x, y) = (2x, 3)$, for $f(x, y) = x^2 + 3y$'
level: undergraduate
is: [gradient]
is_not: [directional_derivative]
why:
  directional_derivative: >-
    The gradient is a vector. A directional derivative is a number, $\nabla f \cdot v$, for a given direction $v$.
