published: false
title: 'Prepending $4$ to $(1, 2, 3)$ produces $(4, 1, 2, 3)$'
level: elementary
is: [
  prepend,
  insert
]
is_not: [append]
why:
  append: >-
    The $4$ is added at the beginning, not the end.
  insert: >-
    Prepending $4$ inserts a single element at the beginning of the list.
    It is not appending, which adds at the end.
---
