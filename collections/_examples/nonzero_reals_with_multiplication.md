---
title: 'The nonzero real numbers, with multiplication'
level: high-school
is: [group]
is_not: [ring, field]
why:
  group: >-
    The nonzero real numbers form a group under multiplication, because $1$ is an identity and $1/x$ is the inverse of $x$.
  ring: >-
    The nonzero real numbers, with multiplication, do not form a ring, because a ring needs an addition too, and the nonzero real numbers are not closed under addition: $1 + (-1) = 0$.
  field: >- 
    The nonzero real numbers are not a field without $0$ because a field requires there is an additive identity.
---
