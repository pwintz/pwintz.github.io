title: A $440$ Hz tuning fork and a $442$ Hz tuning fork sounding at the same time
level: high-school
is: [
  interference,
  beats,
  constructive_interference,
  destructive_interference
]
values:
  beats: '$f_{\text{beat}} = 2$ Hz'
why:
  interference: >-
    The sounds of a $440$ Hz tuning fork and a $442$ Hz tuning fork interfere, because the air pressure at the ear is the sum of the pressures of the two sounds.
  beats: >-
    A $440$ Hz tuning fork and a $442$ Hz tuning fork make beats with frequency $\abs{442 - 440} = 2$ Hz, so the loudness rises and falls twice each second.
  constructive_interference: >-
    The sounds of a $440$ Hz tuning fork and a $442$ Hz tuning fork have constructive interference twice each second, at the moments when the two sounds drift into phase and the sound is loudest.
  destructive_interference: >-
    The sounds of a $440$ Hz tuning fork and a $442$ Hz tuning fork have destructive interference twice each second, at the moments when the two sounds drift half a cycle out of phase and the sound is quietest.
