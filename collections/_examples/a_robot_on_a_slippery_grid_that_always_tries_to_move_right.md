title: 'A robot on a slippery grid that tries to move right at every step'
level: high-school
description: >-
  When the robot tries to move right, the robot moves right with probability $0.8$ and slips up or down with probability $0.1$ each.
is: [markov_chain]
why:
  markov_chain: >-
    A robot on a slippery grid that tries to move right at every step is a Markov chain, because the robot follows a fixed rule, so the probabilities of the next square depend only on the current square.
