title: 'The unit circle, for the closed unit disk in $\reals^2$'
level: high-school
is: [boundary]
is_not: [edge_polygon, edge_graph]
why:
  boundary: >-
    The unit circle is the boundary of the closed unit disk because every ball around a point of the circle contains points inside and outside the disk.
  edge_graph: >-
    The unit circle, for the closed unit disk in $\reals^2$, is not an edge of a polygon, since the disk has no straight sides, and it is not an edge of a graph, since it is not a link between two vertices.
