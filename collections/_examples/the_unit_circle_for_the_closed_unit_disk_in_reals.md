---
published: false
title: 'The unit circle, for the closed unit disk in $\reals^2$'
level: high-school
is: [boundary]
is_not: [
  edge_polygon,
  edge_graph
]
why:
  boundary: >-
    The unit circle is the boundary of the closed unit disk because every ball around a point of the circle contains points inside and outside the disk.
  edge_graph: >-
    The unit circle is not an edge of a graph, because it is not a link between two vertices.
  edge_polygon: >-
    The unit circle is not an edge of a polygon, because the closed unit disk has no straight sides.
---
