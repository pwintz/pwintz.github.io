title: 'The events "the first flip is heads" and "the second flip is heads," for two flips of a fair coin'
level: high-school
is: [statistical_independence]
is_not: [mutually_exclusive]
why:
  statistical_independence: >-
    The events "the first flip is heads" and "the second flip is heads" are independent, because the probability that both flips are heads is $1/4 = 1/2 \cdot 1/2$.
  mutually_exclusive: >-
    The events "the first flip is heads" and "the second flip is heads" are not mutually exclusive, because both flips can be heads.
