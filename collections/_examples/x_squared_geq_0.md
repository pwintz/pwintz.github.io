title: '$x^2 \geq 0$'
level: elementary
is: [inequality]
is_not: [equation, expression]
why:
  equation: >-
    $x^2 \geq 0$ is not an equation, because it has no equal sign, even though it is true for every real $x$.
  expression: >-
    $x^2 \geq 0$ is an inequality because it compares two expressions with $\geq$.
