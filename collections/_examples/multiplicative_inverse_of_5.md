---
title: 'The number $5$ under multiplication of real numbers'
level: elementary
values:
  inverse_element: '$\frac{1}{5}$, because $5 \times \frac{1}{5} = 1$'
why:
  inverse_element: >-
    For multiplication of real numbers, the identity element is $1$, so the inverse of $5$ is the number $\frac{1}{5}$ whose product with $5$ is $1$; the number $0$ has no inverse for multiplication, because $0$ times every number is $0$.
