published: false
title: 'A self-driving car staying in the center of its lane on a winding road'
level: high-school
is: [tracking]
is_not: [rendezvous]
why:
  tracking: >-
    A self-driving car staying in the center of its lane is a tracking problem, because the car's position across the lane must follow the lane's center, which changes along the road, at every time.
  rendezvous: >-
    A self-driving car staying in the center of its lane is not a rendezvous problem, because the goal is a small error at every time, not meeting a target at one final time.
---
