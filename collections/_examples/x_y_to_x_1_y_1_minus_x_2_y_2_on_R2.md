---
title: '$(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 - x_2 y_2$ on $\reals^2$'
level: undergraduate
is: [bilinear_form]
is_not: [inner_product]
why:
  bilinear_form: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 - x_2 y_2$ is a bilinear form.
  inner_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 - x_2 y_2$ is not an inner product, because it is not positive definite: $(0, 1)$ paired with itself gives $-1$.
---
