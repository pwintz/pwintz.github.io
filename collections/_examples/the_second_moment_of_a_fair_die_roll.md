title: 'The second moment $\mathbb{E}[X^2] = 91/6$ of the number $X$ shown by a fair six-sided die'
level: high-school
is: [statistical_moment]
why:
  statistical_moment: >-
    The second moment of a fair die roll $X$ is $\mathbb{E}[X^2] = (1 + 4 + 9 + 16 + 25 + 36) / 6 = 91/6$, which is the average of the squares of the six equally likely outcomes.
---
The first moment is $\mathbb{E}[X] = 7/2$, so the variance, which is the second central moment, is

$$\operatorname{Var}(X) = \mathbb{E}[X^2] - (\mathbb{E}[X])^2 = \frac{91}{6} - \frac{49}{4} = \frac{35}{12}.$$
