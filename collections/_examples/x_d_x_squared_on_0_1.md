  riemann_stieltjes_integral: '$\int_0^1 x \cdot 2x \mathrm{d}x = \frac{2}{3}$'
why:
  riemann_stieltjes_integral: >-
    The Riemann–Stieltjes integral of $x$ with respect to $x^2$ on $[0, 1]$ is $\frac{2}{3}$, because $g(x) = x^2$ is differentiable, so $\mathrm{d}g = 2x \mathrm{d}x$.
