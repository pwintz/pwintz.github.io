published: false
title: A drop of food coloring spreading slowly through a glass of still water
level: elementary
is: [
  diffusion,
  particle_diffusion
]
is_not: [
  diffraction,
  convection
]
why:
  diffusion: >-
    A drop of food coloring spreading through still water is diffusion, because the random motion of the molecules carries the coloring from where the coloring is concentrated to where there is less coloring.
  particle_diffusion: >-
    A drop of food coloring spreading through still water is particle diffusion, because the molecules of the dye themselves wander through the water, from where the dye is concentrated to where there is less dye.
  diffraction: >-
    A drop of food coloring spreading through still water is not diffraction, because nothing is bending around an obstacle: the coloring is a substance, not a wave.
  convection: >-
    A drop of food coloring spreading through still water is not convection, because the water does not flow.
    Stirring the water would mix the coloring much faster, by convection.
---
