---
title: 'A thermostat setting of 20 degrees'
level: high-school
is: [set_point]
is_not: [equilibrium, operating_point]
why:
  set_point: >-
    A thermostat setting of $20$ degrees is a set point because it is a target chosen for the room.
  operating_point: >-
    A thermostat setting of 20 degrees is not an operating point, because on a very cold day, the room may not reach it.
  equilibrium: >-
    A thermostat setting of 20 degrees is not an equilibrium, because on a very cold day, the room may not reach it.
---
