---
title: '$\dot{x} = -x + \sin t$'
level: high-school
is_not: [
  time_invariant,
  unforced
]
why:
  time_invariant: >-
    $\dot{x} = -x + \sin t$ is not time-invariant, because the forcing term $\sin t$ depends explicitly on $t$.
  unforced: >-
    $\dot{x} = -x + \sin t$ is not unforced, because the forcing term $\sin t$ depends explicitly on $t$.
---
