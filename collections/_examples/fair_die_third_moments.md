title: 'The roll $X$ of a fair six-sided die, for $k = 3$'
level: high-school
values:
  statistical_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[X^3] = 147/2$'
  central_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[(X - 7/2)^3] = 0$'
why:
  statistical_moment: >-
    The third moment of a fair die roll $X$ is the average of the cubes of the outcomes, $\mathbb{E}[X^3] = (1 + 8 + 27 + 64 + 125 + 216) / 6 = 441/6 = 147/2$.
  central_moment: >-
    The third central moment of a fair die roll $X$ is $0$, because the distribution is symmetric about the mean $7/2$: the cubed deviations $(\pm 5/2)^3$, $(\pm 3/2)^3$, and $(\pm 1/2)^3$ cancel in pairs.
