    The matrix $$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & 5 \\ 0 & 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$$ has nullity $1$, because its third column has no leading entry, so $x_3$ is a free variable, and its kernel is the line spanned by $(1, -2, 1)$.
