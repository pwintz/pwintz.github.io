title: 'A $2 \times 3 \times 4$ box'
level: elementary
values:
  volume: '$2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4 = 24$ cubic units'
  area: '$2 (2 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 4) = 52$ square units'
  diameter: '$\sqrt{2^2 + 3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{29}$'
  cardinality: '$\mathfrak{c}$'
  lebesgue_measure: '$24$'
  hausdorff_dimension: '$3$'
why:
  area: >-
    The area of a $2 \times 3 \times 4$ box is its surface area: the sum of the areas of its six faces, two of each size.
  diameter: >-
    The diameter of a solid $2 \times 3 \times 4$ box is the length of its long diagonal, $\sqrt{2^2 + 3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{29}$.
  cardinality: >-
    The cardinality of a solid box is $\mathfrak{c}$, the same as that of $\reals$.
  lebesgue_measure: >-
    The three-dimensional Lebesgue measure of a solid $2 \times 3 \times 4$ box is its volume, $24$.
  hausdorff_dimension: >-
    The Hausdorff dimension of a solid box is $3$.
