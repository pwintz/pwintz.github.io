---
published: false
title: 'The open disk $\setdef{(x, y) \suchthat x^2 + y^2 < 1}$, as the interior of the circle $\setdef{(x, y) \suchthat x^2 + y^2 = 1}$'
level: high-school
is_not: [interior]
why:
  interior: >-
    The circle is a curve, which contains no disk at all, so its interior is empty.
    The open disk is the region the circle encloses: the interior of the closed disk, not of the circle.
---
