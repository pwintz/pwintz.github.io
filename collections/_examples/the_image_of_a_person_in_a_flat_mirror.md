title: The image of a person in a flat mirror
level: elementary
is: [
  reflection_wave,
  reflection_geometry
]
why:
  reflection_wave: >-
    A person sees their image in a flat mirror because of the reflection of a wave: light from the person bounces off of the mirror and back to their eyes.
  reflection_geometry: >-
    The image of a person in a flat mirror is the geometric reflection of the person across the plane of the mirror, because each point of the image is as far behind the mirror as the matching point of the person is in front of the mirror.
---
The geometric reflection reverses orientation, so the image of a right hand looks like a left hand.
