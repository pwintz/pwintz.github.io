---
published: false
title: 'Independent fair coin flips $X_1, X_2, \dots$ (each $0$ or $1$), compared with another independent fair coin flip $X$'
level: high-school
is: [convergence_in_distribution]
is_not: [convergence_in_probability]
why:
  convergence_in_distribution: >-
    Every $X_n$ has the same distribution as $X$.
  convergence_in_probability: >-
    $X_n$ differs from $X$ with probability $1/2$, for every $n$.
---
