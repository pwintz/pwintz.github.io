title: 'A $2$ kg ball whirled on a string in a horizontal circle at a constant speed of $3$ m/s'
level: high-school
values:
  momentum: '$\norm{p} = 6$ kg·m/s, in a changing direction'
  energy: '$K = 9$ J, constant'
why:
  momentum: >-
    The momentum of a $2$ kg ball whirled in a circle at $3$ m/s has the constant size $6$ kg·m/s, but its direction turns with the velocity, so the momentum changes; the string supplies the force that changes it.
  energy: >-
    The kinetic energy of a $2$ kg ball whirled in a circle at $3$ m/s is constantly $9$ J, because the kinetic energy depends only on the speed, which does not change.
