title: 'An A ($4.0$) in a $3$-credit course and a C ($2.0$) in a $1$-credit course'
level: high-school
is: [weighted_average]
values:
  weighted_average: '$\frac{3 \cdot 4.0 + 1 \cdot 2.0}{3 + 1} = 3.5$'
  arithmetic_mean: '$\frac{4.0 + 2.0}{2} = 3.0$'
why:
  weighted_average: >-
    The credit-weighted grade point average is $(3 \cdot 4 + 1 \cdot 2) / 4 = 3.5$.
  arithmetic_mean: >-
    The credit-weighted grade point average is $3.5$, because the A counts three times as much as the C.
    The unweighted mean of the two grades is $3.0$.
