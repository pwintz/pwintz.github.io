title: 'The polynomials $x^3$ and $x$, with coefficients in $\setdef{0, 1, 2}$ and arithmetic modulo $3$'
level: undergraduate
values:
  polynomial: '$x^3 \neq x$'
  polynomial_function: '$a^3 = a$ for every $a \in \setdef{0, 1, 2}$'
why:
  polynomial: >-
    With arithmetic modulo $3$, the polynomials $x^3$ and $x$ are different, because the coefficient of $x^3$ is $1$ in $x^3$ and $0$ in $x$.
  polynomial_function: >-
    With arithmetic modulo $3$, the polynomials $x^3$ and $x$ give the same function on $\setdef{0, 1, 2}$, because $0^3 = 0$, $1^3 = 1$, and $2^3 = 8 = 2$ modulo $3$.
