title: '$1$, for $S = [1, 2]$'
level: high-school
is: [
  lower_bound,
  infimum,
  minimum,
  minimal_element
]
why:
  minimal_element: >-
    $1$ is a minimal element of $[1, 2]$, because no element of $[1, 2]$ is smaller than $1$.
  lower_bound: >-
    $1$ is the infimum of $[1, 2]$, and so a lower bound, and it belongs to the set, so it is the minimum.
