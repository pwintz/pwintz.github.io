title: 'The "23" on a jersey'
level: elementary
is: [nominal_number]
is_not: [
  cardinal_number,
  ordinal_number
]
why:
  nominal_number: >-
    The "23" on a jersey is a nominal number because it is only a label.
  ordinal_number: >-
    The "23" on a jersey is not a cardinal or ordinal number: it counts nothing, and player 23 does not come after player 7.
---
