title: 'The axiom of choice'
detail: 'Every collection of nonempty sets has a function that picks one element from each set.'
level: undergraduate
is: [axiom]
is_not: [assumption]
why:
  axiom: >-
    The axiom of choice is an axiom of set theory, because it cannot be proved from the other axioms (ZF) and is added to them.
  assumption: >-
    The axiom of choice is not an assumption of one result, though some authors state the results that need it separately.
---
