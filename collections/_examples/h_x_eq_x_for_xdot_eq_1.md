title: '$h(x) = x$, for $\dot{x} = 1$'
level: graduate
is: [barrier_function]
is_not: [lyapunov_function]
why:
  barrier_function: >-
    $h(x) = x$ is a barrier function for $\dot{x} = 1$ because $\nabla h(x) \cdot f(x) = 1 \geq 0$, so $[0, \infty)$ is forward invariant.
  lyapunov_function: >-
    $h(x) = x$ is not a Lyapunov function, because the system has no equilibrium at all.
---
