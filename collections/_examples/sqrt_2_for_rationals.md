published: false
title: 'The point $\sqrt{2}$, for the set of rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}$'
level: high-school
is: [
  limit_point,
  adherent_point
]
is_not: [isolated_point]
why:
  limit_point: >-
    The point $\sqrt{2}$ is a limit point of $\mathbb{Q}$, because the decimal approximations $1.4, 1.41, 1.414, \dots$ are rational numbers that come arbitrarily close to $\sqrt{2}$.
  adherent_point: >-
    The point $\sqrt{2}$ is an adherent point of $\mathbb{Q}$, because rational numbers come arbitrarily close to $\sqrt{2}$, even though $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.
  isolated_point: >-
    The point $\sqrt{2}$ is not an isolated point of $\mathbb{Q}$, because $\sqrt{2}$ does not belong to $\mathbb{Q}$.
---
