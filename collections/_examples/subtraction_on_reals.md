---
title: 'Subtraction $a - b$ on $\reals$'
level: elementary
is_not: [
  associative,
  commutative
]
why:
  associative: >-
    Subtraction on $\reals$ is not associative, because $(5 - 3) - 1 = 1$, but $5 - (3 - 1) = 3$.
  commutative: >-
    Subtraction on $\reals$ is not commutative, because $5 - 3 = 2$, but $3 - 5 = -2$.
---
