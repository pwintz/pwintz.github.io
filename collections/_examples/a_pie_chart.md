---
title: 'A pie chart'
level: elementary
is: [chart]
is_not: [
  plot,
  diagram
]
why:
  chart: >-
    A pie chart is a chart because it displays data.
  diagram: >-
    A pie chart is not a diagram, because it shows data, not structure.
  plot: >-
    A pie chart is not a plot, because it has no coordinates.
---
