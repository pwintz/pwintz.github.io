title: 'A level drone pointing north'
level: high-school
description: >-
  A drone hovers with its rotors level and its nose pointing north, and the reference directions are north, east, and down.
values:
  orientation_rigid_body: 'Aligned with the reference directions'
  attitude: 'Yaw $0^\circ$, pitch $0^\circ$, roll $0^\circ$'
why:
  orientation_rigid_body: >-
    The orientation of a level drone pointing north is aligned with the reference directions, because its nose points north, its right side points east, and its bottom points down.
    So the rotation matrix that describes its orientation is the identity matrix.
  attitude: >-
    The attitude of a level drone pointing north has yaw, pitch, and roll all $0^\circ$, because the drone is not turned from the reference directions.
