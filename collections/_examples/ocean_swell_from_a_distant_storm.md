title: Long ocean swells from a distant storm reaching a beach before the shorter waves from the same storm
level: high-school
is: [dispersion_wave]
why:
  dispersion_wave: >-
    Long ocean swells from a distant storm arriving before the shorter waves is dispersion, because on deep water, the speed of a wave grows with the wavelength of the wave, so waves of different wavelengths that leave the storm together separate as they travel.
