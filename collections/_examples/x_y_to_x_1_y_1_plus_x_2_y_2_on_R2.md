---
title: '$(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ on $\reals^2$'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  bilinear_form,
  inner_product,
  dot_product
]
why:
  dot_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ is the dot product on $\reals^2$.
  inner_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ is an inner product, because it is bilinear, symmetric, and positive definite: $x_1^2 + x_2^2 > 0$ for $x \neq 0$.
  bilinear_form: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ on $\reals^2$ is a bilinear form, because it is linear in $x$ for each fixed $y$, and linear in $y$ for each fixed $x$.
---
