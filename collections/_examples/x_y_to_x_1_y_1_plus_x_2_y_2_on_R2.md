---
title: '$(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ on $\reals^2$'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  bilinear_form,
  inner_product,
  dot_product
]
why:
  dot_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ is the dot product on $\reals^2$.
  inner_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ is an inner product: the dot product on $\reals^2$.
  norm: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ gives the Euclidean norm, as $\norm{x} = \sqrt{x \cdot x}$.
  bilinear_form: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ on $\reals^2$ is an inner product and a bilinear form, because it is the dot product.
---
