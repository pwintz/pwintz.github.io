title: 'A $1$ kg ball held at rest $2$ m above the floor'
level: high-school
values:
  momentum: '$p = 0$ kg·m/s'
  energy: 'Potential energy $V \approx 19.6$ J, relative to the floor'
why:
  momentum: >-
    The momentum of a ball held at rest is $0$, because its velocity is $0$.
  energy: >-
    The potential energy of a $1$ kg ball held $2$ m above the floor is $V = m g h \approx 1 \cdot 9.8 \cdot 2 = 19.6$ J relative to the floor, because gravity can do that much work on the ball as it falls to the floor.
