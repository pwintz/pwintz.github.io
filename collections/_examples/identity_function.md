---
title: '$x \mapsto x$, for composition of functions'
level: high-school
is: [idempotent, identity_element]
is_not: [constant_function]
why:
  identity_element: >-
    $x \mapsto x$ is the identity element for composition, and it is idempotent, because composing it with itself gives itself.
  fixed_point: >-
    $x \mapsto x$, for composition of functions, is not constant, and every point is a fixed point.
---
