---
title: 'A heated rod with insulated ends'
level: high-school
is: [neumann_condition]
is_not: [dirichlet_condition, robin_condition]
why:
  neumann_condition: >-
    Insulated ends have zero heat flux.
  dirichlet_condition: >-
    A heated rod with insulated ends is not a Dirichlet, because it prescribes the flux, not the value.
  robin_condition: >-
    A heated rod with insulated ends is not Robin condition, because it prescribes the flux, not the value.
---
