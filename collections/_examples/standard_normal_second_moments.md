title: 'A standard normal random variable $X$, for $k = 2$'
level: undergraduate
values:
  statistical_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[X^2] = 1$'
  central_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[(X - 0)^2] = 1$'
why:
  statistical_moment: >-
    The second moment of a standard normal random variable $X$ is $\mathbb{E}[X^2] = \operatorname{Var}(X) + (\mathbb{E}[X])^2 = 1 + 0 = 1$.
  central_moment: >-
    The second central moment of a standard normal random variable $X$ is its variance, $1$, which equals its second moment because its mean is $0$.
