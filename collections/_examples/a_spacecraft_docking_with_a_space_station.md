title: 'A spacecraft docking with a space station'
level: high-school
is: [rendezvous]
is_not: [tracking]
why:
  tracking: >-
    A spacecraft docking with a space station is not a tracking problem, because the spacecraft does not need to follow a reference at every time before docking; the spacecraft's route matters only through the fuel it uses.
  rendezvous: >-
    A spacecraft docking with a space station is a rendezvous problem, because the spacecraft must reach the station's position with the station's velocity at the moment of docking.
---
