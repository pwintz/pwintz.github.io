---
title: 'A lighthouse due east of a ship pointing north'
level: high-school
description: >-
  A ship points north and moves north in still water, and a lighthouse is due east of the ship.
values:
  heading: '$0^\circ$'
  course: '$0^\circ$'
  bearing: '$90^\circ$'
why:
  heading: >-
    The heading of the ship is $0^\circ$ because the nose of the ship points north.
  course: >-
    The course of the ship is $0^\circ$ because the ship moves north.
  bearing: >-
    The bearing of the lighthouse from the ship is $90^\circ$ because the lighthouse is due east of the ship.
---
