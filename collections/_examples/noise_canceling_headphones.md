title: Noise-canceling headphones playing a flipped copy of the outside noise
level: high-school
is: [
  interference,
  destructive_interference
]
why:
  interference: >-
    In noise-canceling headphones, the outside noise and the sound from the headphones interfere, because the air pressure at the ear is the sum of the pressures of the two sounds.
  destructive_interference: >-
    Noise-canceling headphones make destructive interference, because a flipped copy of the noise is half a cycle out of phase with the noise at every frequency, so the two sounds cancel at the ear.
