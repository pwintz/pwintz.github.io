---
title: 'The vectors $(1, 0)$ and $(2, 0)$ in $\reals^2$'
level: high-school
is_not: [vector_basis, linearly_independent]
why:
  linearly_independent: >-
    The vectors $(1, 0)$ and $(2, 0)$ are not linearly independent, because $(2, 0) = 2 (1, 0)$.
  vector_basis: >-
    Both point along the $x$-axis, so no combination of them gives $(0, 1)$.
---
