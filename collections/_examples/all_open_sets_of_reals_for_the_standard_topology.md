title: 'The collection of all open sets of $\reals$, for the standard topology on $\reals$'
level: undergraduate
is: [topological_basis]
why:
  topological_basis: >-
    The collection of all open sets of $\reals$ is a basis for the standard topology, because every open set $U$ is the union of the one-set collection $\setdef{U}$.
