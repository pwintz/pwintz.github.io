---
title: 'The map from the unit circle $S^1$ to $\reals^2$ that sends each point of $S^1$ to itself'
level: undergraduate
is: [inclusion_map]
is_not: [identity_function]
why:
  inclusion_map: >-
    The map from the unit circle $S^1$ to $\reals^2$ that sends each point to itself is the inclusion map of $S^1$ into $\reals^2$, because $S^1$ is a subset of $\reals^2$.
    With the subspace topology on $S^1$, this inclusion map is a topological embedding.
  identity_function: >-
    The map from the unit circle $S^1$ to $\reals^2$ that sends each point to itself is not an identity function, because its codomain $\reals^2$ is larger than its domain $S^1$.
---
