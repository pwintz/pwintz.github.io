title: '$f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$, at the origin'
level: undergraduate
is: [frechet_derivative, gateaux_derivative, directional_derivative]
why:
  directional_derivative: >-
    $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$ has every directional derivative at the origin, and all of them are $0$, because $f$ is Fréchet differentiable there with derivative $0$.
