title: '$i$, for the polynomial $x^2 + 1$ with real coefficients'
level: high-school
description: >-
  The imaginary unit $i$ is the complex number such that $i^2 = -1$.
is: [root_of_polynomial]
why:
  root_of_polynomial: >-
    $i$ is a root of $x^2 + 1$, because $i^2 + 1 = -1 + 1 = 0$.
    The coefficients of $x^2 + 1$ are real, but its roots, $i$ and $-i$, are complex.
