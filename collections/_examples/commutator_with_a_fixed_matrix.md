title: 'The map $Y \mapsto A Y - Y A$ on $n \times n$ real matrices, for a fixed $n \times n$ matrix $A$'
level: graduate
is: [derivation]
why:
  derivation: >-
    For a fixed matrix $A$, the map $Y \mapsto A Y - Y A$ is a derivation of the algebra of $n \times n$ matrices, because it is linear and $A (Y Z) - (Y Z) A = (A Y - Y A) Z + Y (A Z - Z A)$.
