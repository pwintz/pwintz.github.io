---
title: 'Energy $H = \frac{1}{2}(p^2 + q^2)$, for the oscillator $\dot{q} = p$, $\dot{p} = -q$'
level: undergraduate
is: [invariant]
is_not: [constant_function]
why:
  constant_function: >-
    The energy $H$ is not a constant function, because different solutions have different energies.
  invariant: >-
    The energy $H$ is an invariant, because it is constant along each solution.
---
