---
title: 'The surface of a donut (a torus)'
level: high-school
is: [
  connected,
  path_connected,
  manifold
]
is_not: [
  sphere,
  simply_connected,
  sphere_topological
]
why:
  connected: >-
    The surface of a donut is connected because it is path-connected.
  path_connected: >-
    The surface of a donut is path-connected because every two points on it are joined by a path that first goes around the tube and then goes around the hole.
  manifold: >-
    The surface of a donut is a manifold of dimension $2$, because each point has a neighborhood on the surface that is homeomorphic to an open disk in $\reals^2$.
  sphere: >-
    The surface of a donut is not a sphere, because its points are not all at one distance from a single center.
  simply_connected: >-
    The surface of a donut is not simply connected, because a loop that goes once around the hole cannot slide along the surface to a point.
  sphere_topological: >-
    The surface of a donut is not a topological sphere.
    A surface can be homeomorphic only to the sphere $S^2$, on which every loop can slide to a point, but a loop around the hole of the donut cannot.
---
