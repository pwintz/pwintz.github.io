---
title: 'The ordered list $(A, B)$ of $2$ of the letters $A$, $B$, and $C$'
level: high-school
is: [permutation]
is_not: [combination]
why:
  permutation: >-
    The ordered list $(A, B)$ is a permutation of $2$ of the letters $A$, $B$, and $C$, because it lists $2$ different letters in order.
    The list $(B, A)$ is a different permutation.
  combination: >-
    The ordered list $(A, B)$ is not a combination, because a combination has no order; the combination $\setdef{A, B}$ corresponds to both $(A, B)$ and $(B, A)$.
---
