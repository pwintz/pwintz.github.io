title: '$\int_0^1 x \mathrm{d}g(x)$, where $g$ is $0$ before $\frac{1}{2}$ and $1$ from $\frac{1}{2}$ on'
level: undergraduate
values:
  riemann_stieltjes_integral: '$\frac{1}{2}$'
  riemann_integral: 'None'
why:
  riemann_stieltjes_integral: >-
    The Riemann–Stieltjes integral of $x$ with respect to a unit step at $\frac{1}{2}$ is $\frac{1}{2}$, the value of $x$ at the jump, because $g$ changes only there.
  riemann_integral: >-
    The integral with respect to a step function $g$ is not a Riemann integral, because $g$ has no derivative at the jump to turn $\mathrm{d}g$ into $g^{\prime}(x) \mathrm{d}x$.
