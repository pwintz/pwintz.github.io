title: 'Heat equation $u_t = u_{xx}$'
level: undergraduate
is: [parabolic_pde]
is_not: [
  elliptic_pde,
  hyperbolic_pde
]
why:
  parabolic_pde: >-
    The heat equation is parabolic because, in the variables $(t, x)$, its only second-order term is $u_{xx}$, so $B^2 - AC = 0$.
