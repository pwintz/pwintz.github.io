title: A $100$ Ω resistor in a $60$ Hz circuit
level: high-school
values:
  resistance: '$R = 100$ Ω'
  reactance: '$X = 0$ Ω'
  impedance: '$Z = 100$ Ω'
why:
  resistance: >-
    The resistance of a $100$ Ω resistor is $100$ Ω at every frequency.
  reactance: >-
    The reactance of a $100$ Ω resistor is $0$, because a resistor stores no energy, so the current is in phase with the voltage.
  impedance: >-
    The impedance of a $100$ Ω resistor is the real number $100$ Ω, because the impedance is $R + i X$ with $R = 100$ Ω and $X = 0$.
