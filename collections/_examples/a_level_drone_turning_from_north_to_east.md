---
title: 'A quadcopter turning from pointing north to pointing east'
level: high-school
description: >-
  A quadcopter drone hovers with its rotors level and turns its nose from north to east and the reference directions are north, east, and down.
values:
  rotation: '$90^\circ$ clockwise about the vertical axis, seen from above'
  orientation_rigid_body: 'Level, pointing east: yaw $90^\circ$, pitch $0^\circ$, roll $0^\circ$'
why:
  rotation: >-
    The rotation of a level drone turning from north to east is a quarter turn clockwise about the vertical axis, seen from above, because east is a quarter turn clockwise from north.
  orientation_rigid_body: >-
    The orientation of the drone after turning from north to east is level and pointing east, which is the final state, not the change.
    Its yaw is $90^\circ$ because its heading is east, and its pitch and roll are $0^\circ$ because its rotors stay level.
