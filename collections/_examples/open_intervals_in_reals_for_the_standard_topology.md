title: 'The open intervals $(a, b)$ in $\reals$, for the standard topology on $\reals$'
level: undergraduate
is: [topological_basis]
why:
  topological_basis: >-
    The open intervals form a basis for the standard topology on $\reals$, because for every open set $U \subseteq \reals$ and every point $x \in U$, there is an $\varepsilon > 0$ such that $x \in (x - \varepsilon, x + \varepsilon) \subseteq U$.
    So every open set of $\reals$ is a union of open intervals.
