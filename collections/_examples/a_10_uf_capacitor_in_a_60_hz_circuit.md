title: A $10$ μF capacitor in a $60$ Hz circuit
level: undergraduate
values:
  resistance: '$R = 0$ Ω'
  reactance: '$X \approx -265.3$ Ω'
  impedance: '$Z \approx -265.3 i$ Ω'
why:
  resistance: >-
    The resistance of an ideal $10$ μF capacitor is $0$, because an ideal capacitor converts no energy into heat.
  reactance: |-
    The reactance of a $10$ μF capacitor at $60$ Hz is

    $$X = -\frac{1}{2 \pi f C} = -\frac{1}{2 \pi \cdot 60 \cdot 10^{-5}} \approx -265.3 \text{ Ω}.$$
  impedance: >-
    The impedance of a $10$ μF capacitor at $60$ Hz is $Z = 1 / (i \omega C) \approx -265.3 i$ Ω, which is purely imaginary, so the current leads the voltage by a quarter cycle.
