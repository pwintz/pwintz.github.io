title: 'Estimating the temperature at noon from the temperatures measured at 9 a.m. and 3 p.m.'
level: high-school
is: [interpolation]
is_not: [extrapolation]
why:
  interpolation: >-
    Estimating the temperature at noon from measurements at 9 a.m. and 3 p.m. is interpolation, because noon is between the two measurement times.
  extrapolation: >-
    Estimating the temperature at noon from measurements at 9 a.m. and 3 p.m. is not extrapolation, because noon is inside the range of the measurement times.
