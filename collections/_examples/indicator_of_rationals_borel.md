title: 'The indicator function of $\mathbb{Q}$, from $\reals$ to $\reals$, with the Borel σ-algebras'
level: graduate
is: [
  measurable_map,
  indicator_function
]
is_not: [continuous]
why:
  measurable_map: >-
    The indicator function of $\mathbb{Q}$ is measurable, because $\mathbb{Q}$ is a countable union of points, so $\mathbb{Q}$ and its complement are Borel sets, and the preimage of every set is $\emptyset$, $\mathbb{Q}$, $\reals \setminus \mathbb{Q}$, or $\reals$.
  continuous: >-
    The indicator function of $\mathbb{Q}$ is not continuous at any point, because every interval contains rational and irrational numbers, where it takes the values $1$ and $0$.
  indicator_function: >-
    The indicator function of $\mathbb{Q}$ is $1$ at every rational number and $0$ at every irrational number.
---
