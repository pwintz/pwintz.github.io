---
title: 'The pairs of integers $\mathbb{Z}^2$, with addition and multiplication by integers'
level: undergraduate
is: [module]
is_not: [
  vector_space,
  algebra_over_a_field
]
why:
  module: >-
    The pairs of integers $\mathbb{Z}^2$, with addition and multiplication by integers, form a module over the ring $\mathbb{Z}$, because the rules of a vector space hold with integer scalars.
  vector_space: >-
    The pairs of integers $\mathbb{Z}^2$, with multiplication by integers, do not form a vector space, because the integers do not form a field.
  algebra_over_a_field: >-
    The pairs of integers $\mathbb{Z}^2$, with addition and multiplication by integers, do not form an algebra over a field, because the scalars do not form a field and no multiplication of two pairs is given.
---
