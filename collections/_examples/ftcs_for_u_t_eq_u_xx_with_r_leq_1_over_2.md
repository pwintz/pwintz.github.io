level: undergraduate
is: [consistent_scheme, stable_scheme, convergent_scheme]
why:
  convergent_scheme: >-
    FTCS for $u_t = u_{xx}$ with $r \leq \frac{1}{2}$ is convergent, by the Lax equivalence theorem, because it is consistent and stable.
  consistent_scheme: >-
    FTCS for the heat equation is consistent, and with $r \leq \frac{1}{2}$ it is stable.
