title: A sheet of paper scattering light in all directions (diffuse reflection)
level: high-school
is: [
  reflection_wave,
  scattering
]
is_not: [
  diffusion,
  diffraction
]
why:
  reflection_wave: >-
    Light bouncing off of a sheet of paper is a reflection of a wave, because the light turns back from the surface of the paper.
  scattering: >-
    A sheet of paper scatters light, because the rough fibers of the paper face in many directions, so each fiber reflects light in a different direction.
  diffusion: >-
    Light scattered by a sheet of paper is not diffusion, even though the reflection is called "diffuse," because the light is not spreading from a high concentration to a low concentration by random molecular motion.
  diffraction: >-
    Light scattered by a sheet of paper is not diffraction, because the fibers of the paper are much larger than the wavelength of light, so the light spreads because the fibers are tilted in many directions, not because the light bends around their edges.
---
