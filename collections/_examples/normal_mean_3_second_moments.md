title: 'A normal random variable $X$ with mean $3$ and variance $1$, for $k = 2$'
level: undergraduate
values:
  statistical_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[X^2] = 10$'
  central_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[(X - 3)^2] = 1$'
why:
  statistical_moment: >-
    The second moment of a normal random variable $X$ with mean $3$ and variance $1$ is $\mathbb{E}[X^2] = \operatorname{Var}(X) + (\mathbb{E}[X])^2 = 1 + 9 = 10$.
  central_moment: >-
    The second central moment of a normal random variable $X$ with mean $3$ and variance $1$ is its variance, $1$.
