title: The blue color of the daytime sky
level: high-school
is: [scattering]
is_not: [dispersion_wave]
why:
  scattering: >-
    The daytime sky is blue because of scattering: air molecules redirect sunlight in all directions, and the molecules scatter blue light much more strongly than red light, so the light that reaches us from the sky away from the sun is mostly blue.
  dispersion_wave: >-
    The blue color of the sky is not caused by dispersion, even though the colors of sunlight are separated, because the blue light is separated by being scattered more strongly than red light, not by traveling at a different speed.
