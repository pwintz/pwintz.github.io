---
title: '$(3, -1)$ is the coordinate vector of $3 + 2x$, relative to the basis $(1 + x, x)$'
level: undergraduate
is: [coordinate_vector]
why:
  coordinate_vector: >-
    $3 + 2x$ is the linear combination of the basis vectors $1 + x$ and $x$ using the coefficients $3$ and $-1$:

    $$3 + 2x = 3(1 + x) + (-1)(x)$$
---
