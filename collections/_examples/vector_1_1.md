---
title: 'The vector $x = (1, 1) \in \reals^2$'
level: high-school
values:
  euclidean_norm: '$\norm{x}_2 = \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}$'
  one_norm: '$\norm{x}_1 = \abs{1} + \abs{1} = 2$'
  infinity_norm: '$\norm{x}_{\infty} = \max(\abs{1}, \abs{1}) = 1$'
why:
  euclidean_norm: >-
    The three norms of $(1, 1)$ are as far apart as possible for a vector in $\reals^2$: the 1-norm is $\sqrt{2}$ times the Euclidean norm, which is $\sqrt{2}$ times the infinity norm.
