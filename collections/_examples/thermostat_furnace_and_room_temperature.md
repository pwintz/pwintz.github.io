title: 'The heat output of a furnace and the temperature of a room, when a thermostat holds the room at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ through a winter'
level: high-school
values:
  correlation: 'Approximately uncorrelated'
  causation: 'The furnace causes the room to warm'
  statistical_independence: 'Approximately independent'
why:
  correlation: >-
    When a thermostat holds a room at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, the furnace output and the room temperature are approximately uncorrelated, because the room temperature stays approximately constant while the furnace output varies with the weather outside.
  causation: >-
    The furnace causes the room to warm: if the furnace were switched off, then the room temperature would drop.
  statistical_independence: >-
    When a thermostat holds a room at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, the furnace output and the room temperature are approximately independent, because the room temperature is approximately the same whatever the furnace output is.
