title: '$x \mapsto \sin(x)$ on $\reals$'
level: high-school
is: [
  continuous,
  uniformly_continuous
]
why:
  uniformly_continuous: >-
    $\sin$ is uniformly continuous, and so continuous, because $\abs{\sin(x) - \sin(y)} \leq \abs{x - y}$, so the choice $\delta = \varepsilon$ works everywhere.
