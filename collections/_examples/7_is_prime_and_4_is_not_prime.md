title: '"$7$ is prime and $4$ is not prime"'
level: undergraduate
detail: >-
  This statement is the result of substituting "$7$ is prime" for $p$ and "$4$ is prime" for $q$ in the propositional formula $p \land \neg q$.
is: [proposition]
is_not: [propositional_formula]
why:
  proposition: >-
    "$7$ is prime and $4$ is not prime" is a proposition, because the statement is true: $7$ is prime, and $4$ is not prime.
  propositional_formula: >-
    "$7$ is prime and $4$ is not prime" is not a propositional formula, because the statement contains no propositional variables; the statement is one instance of the formula $p \land \neg q$.
