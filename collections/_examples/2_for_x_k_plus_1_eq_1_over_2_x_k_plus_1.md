title: '$2$, for $x_{k+1} = \frac{1}{2} x_k + 1$'
level: high-school
is: [equilibrium, fixed_point, steady_state]
why:
  steady_state: >-
    $2$ is the steady state because every solution converges to it.
  equilibrium: >-
    $2$ is a fixed point of the update map because $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 + 1 = 2$, so it is an equilibrium, since in discrete time the equilibria are exactly the fixed points of the update map.
