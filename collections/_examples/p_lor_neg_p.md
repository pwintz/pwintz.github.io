title: 'The tautology $p \lor \neg p$'
level: undergraduate
is: [propositional_formula]
is_not: [proposition]
why:
  propositional_formula: >-
    $p \lor \neg p$ is a propositional formula, because $p \lor \neg p$ is built from the propositional variable $p$ with the connectives $\lor$ and $\neg$.
  proposition: >-
    $p \lor \neg p$ is not a proposition, although $p \lor \neg p$ is true for every truth value of $p$: the formula is a pattern, and each substitution of a proposition for $p$, such as "$7$ is prime or $7$ is not prime," gives a different proposition.
