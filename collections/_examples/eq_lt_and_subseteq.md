title: '$=$, $<$, and $\subseteq$'
level: high-school
is: [relation]
is_not: [binary_operation]
why:
  relation: >-
    $=$, $<$, and $\subseteq$ are relations because they make statements that are true or false: "$2 < 3$" is true and "$4 < 4$" is false.
  binary_operation: >-
    $=$, $<$, and $\subseteq$ are not binary operations, because they do not produce a new number or set.
---
