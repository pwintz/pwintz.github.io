title: 'The events "the roll is at most $2$" and "the roll is even," for one roll of a fair six-sided die'
level: high-school
is: [statistical_independence]
is_not: [mutually_exclusive]
why:
  statistical_independence: >-
    The events "the roll is at most $2$" and "the roll is even" are independent, because their only common outcome is $2$, so $P(\text{both}) = 1/6$, which equals $P(\text{at most } 2) P(\text{even}) = 1/3 \cdot 1/2$.
  mutually_exclusive: >-
    The events "the roll is at most $2$" and "the roll is even" are not mutually exclusive, because a roll of $2$ belongs to both events.
