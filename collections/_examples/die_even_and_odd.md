title: 'The events "the roll is even" and "the roll is odd," for one roll of a fair six-sided die'
level: high-school
is: [mutually_exclusive]
is_not: [statistical_independence]
why:
  mutually_exclusive: >-
    The events "the roll is even" and "the roll is odd" are mutually exclusive, because no number is both even and odd.
  statistical_independence: >-
    The events "the roll is even" and "the roll is odd" are not independent, because $P(\text{even and odd}) = 0$, but $P(\text{even}) P(\text{odd}) = 1/2 \cdot 1/2 = 1/4$.
    Knowing that the roll is even makes the probability that the roll is odd drop from $1/2$ to $0$.
