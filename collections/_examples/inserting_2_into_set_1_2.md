title: 'Inserting $2$ into $\setdef{1, 2}$ produces $\setdef{1, 2}$'
level: elementary
is: [insert]
is_not: [append]
why:
  insert: >-
    Inserting $2$ into $\setdef{1, 2}$ is insertion, and it does not change the set, because $2$ is already in it.
  append: >-
    Inserting $2$ into $\setdef{1, 2}$ is not appending, because sets have no "end."
---
