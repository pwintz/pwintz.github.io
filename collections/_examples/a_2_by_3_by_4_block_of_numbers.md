published: false
title: 'A $2 \times 3 \times 4$ block of numbers'
level: elementary
is: [array]
is_not: [
  matrix,
  tensor
]
why:
  matrix: >-
    A $2 \times 3 \times 4$ block of numbers has three axes, so it is not a matrix (it is sometimes called a tensor).
  tensor: >-
    A $2 \times 3 \times 4$ block of numbers is not a tensor by itself, because it is not a multilinear function.
    It becomes the array of components of a tensor only after a vector space, a basis, and the type of each index are chosen.
---
