title: "Predicting a town's population in 2040 from censuses in 2000, 2010, and 2020"
level: high-school
is: [extrapolation]
is_not: [interpolation]
why:
  interpolation: >-
    Predicting a population in 2040 from censuses in 2000, 2010, and 2020 is not interpolation, because 2040 is outside the range of the census years.
  extrapolation: >-
    Predicting a population in 2040 from censuses in 2000, 2010, and 2020 is extrapolation, because 2040 is after the last census year, so the prediction assumes the trend of the data continues for twenty more years.
