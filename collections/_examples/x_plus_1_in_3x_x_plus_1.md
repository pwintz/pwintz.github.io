title: '$x + 1$, in $3x(x + 1)$'
level: elementary
is: [factor, expression]
is_not: [term_of_sum]
why:
  term_of_sum: >-
    $x + 1$ is not a term of $3x(x + 1)$, because $3x(x + 1)$ is not a sum.
  expression: >-
    $x + 1$ is an expression, because it names a number once $x$ is given.
  factor: >-
    $x + 1$ is a factor of $3x(x + 1)$, because it is one of the parts being multiplied.
