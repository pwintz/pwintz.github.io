title: 'Resistor $y = u$'
level: high-school
is: [passive, input_strictly_passive, output_strictly_passive]
is_not: [lossless]
why:
  lossless: >-
    The resistor is not lossless, because all of the supplied energy is dissipated.
  output_strictly_passive: >-
    The resistor is passive, input strictly passive, and output strictly passive because $u y = u^2 = y^2$, with $V = 0$.
