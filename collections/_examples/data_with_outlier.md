level: elementary
values:
  arithmetic_mean: '$\frac{1 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 100}{5} = 21.4$'
  median: '$\operatorname{median}(1, 1, 2, 3, 100) = 2$'
  mode: '$\operatorname{mode}(1, 1, 2, 3, 100) = 1$'
  range_statistics: '$100 - 1 = 99$'
  geometric_mean: '$\sqrt[5]{1 \cdot 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 100} = \sqrt[5]{600} \approx 3.59$'
why:
  arithmetic_mean: >-
    The mean of the data $1, 1, 2, 3, 100$ is $21.4$, because the outlier $100$ pulls the mean far above most of the data.
  median: >-
    The median of the data $1, 1, 2, 3, 100$ is $2$, the middle value in sorted order, which the outlier $100$ does not affect.
  mode: >-
    The mode of the data $1, 1, 2, 3, 100$ is $1$, because $1$ is the only value that appears twice.
  geometric_mean: >-
    The geometric mean of the data $1, 1, 2, 3, 100$ is about $3.59$, which the outlier $100$ pulls up much less than the arithmetic mean, because the geometric mean averages the logarithms of the data.
