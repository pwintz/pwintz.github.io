---
title: '$0$, for the set $S = (0, 1]$'
level: high-school
is: [
  lower_bound,
  infimum
]
is_not: [minimum]
why:
  minimum: >-
    $0$ is not the minimum, because $0 \notin S$, and $S$ has no minimum.
  infimum: >-
    $0$ is the infimum of $S$ because it is the greatest lower bound, so it is also a lower bound.
---
