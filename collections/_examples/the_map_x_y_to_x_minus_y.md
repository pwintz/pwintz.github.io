---
title: 'The map $f(x, y) = (x, -y)$ on $\reals^2$'
level: high-school
is: [reflection_geometry]
is_not: [reflection_wave]
why:
  reflection_geometry: >-
    The map $f(x, y) = (x, -y)$ is the reflection across the $x$-axis, because $f$ sends each point on the $x$-axis to itself and sends each other point to the point at the same distance on the other side of the $x$-axis.
