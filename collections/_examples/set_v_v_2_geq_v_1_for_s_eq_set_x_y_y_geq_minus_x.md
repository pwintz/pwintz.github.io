published: false
title: '$\setdef{v \suchthat v_2 \geq \abs{v_1}}$, for $S = \setdef{(x, y) \suchthat y \geq -\abs{x}}$ at the origin'
level: graduate
is: [clarke_tangent_cone]
is_not: [contingent_cone, tangent_space]
why:
  clarke_tangent_cone: >-
    $\setdef{v \suchthat v_2 \geq \abs{v_1}}$ is the Clarke tangent cone of $S$ at the origin, because only these directions point into $S$ from every nearby boundary point.
  contingent_cone: >-
    The cone $\setdef{v \suchthat v_2 \geq \abs{v_1}}$ is not the contingent cone of $S$, which is larger, and it is not a tangent space.
---
