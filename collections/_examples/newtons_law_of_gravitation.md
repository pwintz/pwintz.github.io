---
title: 'Newton''s law of universal gravitation, $F = G m_1 m_2 / r^2$'
level: high-school
is: [scientific_law]
is_not: [
  theory_science,
  hypothesis_science
]
why:
  scientific_law: >-
    Newton's law of gravitation is a law because it is an equation that describes how strongly masses attract.
  theory_science: >-
    Newton's law of gravitation is not a theory, because it does not explain why masses attract; general relativity, a theory, does.
---
