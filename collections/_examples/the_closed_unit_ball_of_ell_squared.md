title: 'The closed unit ball of $\ell^2$'
level: graduate
is: [paracompact]
is_not: [compact, sequentially_compact, locally_compact]
why:
  paracompact: >-
    The closed unit ball of $\ell^2$ is paracompact because it is a metric space.
  compact: >-
    The closed unit ball of $\ell^2$ is closed and bounded, but not compact or sequentially compact, because the standard basis vectors have no convergent subsequence, and it is not locally compact.
---
