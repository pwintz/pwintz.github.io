published: false
title: 'Splitting $$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$$ into its diagonal and the part above the diagonal'
level: undergraduate
is_not: [jordan_chevalley_decomposition]
why:
  jordan_chevalley_decomposition: >-
    The two parts do not commute.
    The matrix has distinct eigenvalues, so it is diagonalizable, and its Jordan–Chevalley decomposition is $D = A$, $N = 0$.
