---
title: 'The surface of a basketball'
level: high-school
is: [
  sphere,
  sphere_topological,
  connected,
  path_connected,
  simply_connected,
  manifold
]
is_not: [
  ball,
  open_ball,
  closed_ball
]
why:
  simply_connected: >-
    Although it is hollow, every loop on it can slide off to a point.
  ball: >-
    The surface of a basketball is not a ball, because it does not include the inside.
  path_connected: >-
    The surface of a basketball is path-connected because every two points are joined by an arc of a great circle.
  connected: >-
    The surface of a basketball is connected because it is path-connected.
  sphere_topological: >-
    The surface of a basketball is a sphere, and so a topological sphere, because it consists of the points at one distance from the center.
  manifold: >-
    The surface of a basketball is a manifold of dimension $2$, because each point has a neighborhood on the surface that is homeomorphic to an open disk in $\reals^2$.
---
