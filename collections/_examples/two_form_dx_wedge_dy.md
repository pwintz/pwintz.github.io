title: 'The $2$-form $\mathrm{d}x \wedge \mathrm{d}y$ on $\reals^2$'
level: undergraduate
is: [differential_form]
why:
  differential_form: >-
    The $2$-form $\mathrm{d}x \wedge \mathrm{d}y$ on $\reals^2$ is a differential form, because it assigns to each point the alternating bilinear function $(u, v) \mapsto u_1 v_2 - u_2 v_1$, the signed area of the parallelogram spanned by $u$ and $v$.
    Swapping $u$ and $v$ changes the sign of the area.
