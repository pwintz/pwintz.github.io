published: false
title: '$[-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}]$, for $\dot{x} = -x + x^2$ and its equilibrium $0$'
level: high-school
is: [forward_invariant]
is_not: [
  region_of_attraction,
  basin_of_attraction
]
why:
  forward_invariant: >-
    $[-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}]$ is forward invariant because $\dot{x} = x(x - 1)$ points inward at both ends.
  region_of_attraction: >-
    $[-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}]$ is not the region of attraction, because it is only part of it, the kind of estimate that a sublevel set of a Lyapunov function provides.
---
