published: false
title: 'The standardized average $$\sqrt{n} (\overline{X}_n - 3.5) / \sigma$$ of $n$ fair die rolls'
level: undergraduate
is: [convergence_in_distribution]
is_not: [convergence_in_probability]
why:
  convergence_in_distribution: >-
    The standardized average of $n$ fair die rolls converges in distribution to the standard normal distribution, by the central limit theorem.
  convergence_in_probability: >-
    The standardized average of $n$ fair die rolls does not converge in probability to any random variable, because its values keep fluctuating as $n$ grows.
---
