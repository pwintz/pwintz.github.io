title: 'The events "the roll is $7$" and "the roll is $1$," for one roll of a fair six-sided die'
level: high-school
is: [
  mutually_exclusive,
  statistical_independence
]
why:
  mutually_exclusive: >-
    The events "the roll is $7$" and "the roll is $1$" are mutually exclusive, because no roll is both $7$ and $1$.
  statistical_independence: >-
    The events "the roll is $7$" and "the roll is $1$" are independent, because "the roll is $7$" is the empty event, so $P(\text{both}) = 0 = 0 \cdot 1/6$.
