title: '$r \mapsto r^2$ on $[0, \infty)$'
level: graduate
is: [class_K, class_K_infinity, positive_definite_function]
is_not: [class_L]
why:
  positive_definite_function: >-
    $r \mapsto r^2$ is positive definite, and it is not of class $\mathcal{L}$, because it does not decrease to $0$.
  class_K: >-
    $r \mapsto r^2$ is of class $\mathcal{K}$ because it is continuous, increasing, and zero at $0$, and of class $\mathcal{K}_{\infty}$ because it is unbounded.
---
