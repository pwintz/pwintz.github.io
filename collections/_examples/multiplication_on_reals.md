---
title: 'Multiplication $\times$ on $\reals$'
level: elementary
is: [binary_operation, associative, commutative]
is_not: [relation]
why:
  binary_operation: >-
    Multiplication on $\reals$ is a binary operation, because multiplication combines two real numbers into a new one: "$2 \times 4$" produces $8$.
  relation: >-
    Multiplication on $\reals$ is not a relation, because "$2 \times 4$" does not have a true or false value.
  associative: >-
    Multiplication on $\reals$ is associative: for example, $(2 \times 3) \times 4 = 2 \times (3 \times 4) = 24$.
  commutative: >-
    Multiplication on $\reals$ is commutative: for example, $2 \times 3 = 3 \times 2 = 6$.
---
