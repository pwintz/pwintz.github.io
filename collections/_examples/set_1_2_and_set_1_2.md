title: '$\setdef{1, 2}$ and $\setdef{1, 2}$'
level: elementary
is: [subset]
is_not: [
  proper_subset,
  element_of
]
why:
  subset: >-
    $\setdef{1, 2}$ is a subset of itself, as is every set.
  proper_subset: >-
    $\setdef{1, 2}$ is not a proper subset of itself, because a proper subset must leave out some element.
  element_of: >-
    $\setdef{1, 2}$ is not an element of itself, because its elements are $1$ and $2$.
---
