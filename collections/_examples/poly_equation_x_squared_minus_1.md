---
title: '$x^2 - 1 = 0$'
level: high-school
is: [polynomial_equation, equation]
is_not: [polynomial]
why:
  polynomial_equation: >-
    $x^2 - 1 = 0$ is a polynomial equation.
    Its solutions are $x = 1$ and $x = -1$, the roots of $x^2 - 1$.
  polynomial: >-
    $x^2 - 1 = 0$ is not a polynomial, because it is an equation (it includes an equal sign "=").
---
