---
title: 'The continuous functions on $[0, 1]$, with pointwise addition, multiplication by real numbers, and pointwise multiplication'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  vector_space,
  module,
  algebra_over_a_field
]
why:
  vector_space: >-
    The continuous functions on $[0, 1]$ form a real vector space, because sums and real multiples of continuous functions are continuous.
  module: >-
    The continuous functions on $[0, 1]$ form a module over $\reals$, because they form a vector space over $\reals$.
  algebra_over_a_field: >-
    The continuous functions on $[0, 1]$ form an algebra over $\reals$, because the pointwise product $(f g)(x) = f(x) g(x)$ of continuous functions is continuous and bilinear.
---
