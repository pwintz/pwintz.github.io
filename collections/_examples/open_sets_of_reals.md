---
title: 'The open sets of $\reals$ (the unions of open intervals)'
level: undergraduate
is: [topology]
is_not: [sigma_algebra]
why:
  sigma_algebra: >-
    The complement of $(0, 1)$ is not open.
  topology: >-
    The open sets of $\reals$ form a topology because unions and finite intersections of open sets are open.
