published: false
title: Ocean waves spreading out into a harbor after passing through a narrow gap in a breakwater
level: elementary
description: >-
  A breakwater is a wall built in the sea to protect a harbor from waves.
  The water has the same depth on both sides of the gap.
is: [diffraction]
is_not: [
  refraction,
  diffusion
]
why:
  diffraction: >-
    Ocean waves spreading out after passing through a narrow gap in a breakwater is diffraction, because the waves bend around the edges of the gap into the parts of the harbor that are hidden from the open sea.
  refraction: >-
    Ocean waves spreading out after passing through a gap in a breakwater is not refraction, because the water has the same depth on both sides of the gap, so the waves do not change speed.
  diffusion: >-
    Ocean waves spreading out after passing through a gap in a breakwater is not diffusion, because the waves spread by bending around the edges of the gap, not by the random motion of molecules, and the water itself is not carried into the harbor.
---
