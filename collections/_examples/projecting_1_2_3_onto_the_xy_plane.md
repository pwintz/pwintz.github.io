title: 'Projecting $\vect{x} = (1, 2, 3)$ onto the $xy$-plane in $\reals^3$'
level: high-school
values:
  orthogonal_projection: '$\operatorname{proj}_W \vect{x} = (1, 2, 0)$'
why:
  orthogonal_projection: >-
    The orthogonal projection of $(1, 2, 3)$ onto the $xy$-plane $W$ is $(1, 2, 0)$, because the difference $(1, 2, 3) - (1, 2, 0) = (0, 0, 3)$ points along the $z$-axis, which is orthogonal to every vector in the $xy$-plane.
