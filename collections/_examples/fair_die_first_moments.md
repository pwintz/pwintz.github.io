title: 'The roll $X$ of a fair six-sided die, for $k = 1$'
level: high-school
values:
  statistical_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[X] = 7/2$'
  central_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[X - 7/2] = 0$'
why:
  statistical_moment: >-
    The first moment of a fair die roll $X$ is its mean, $\mathbb{E}[X] = (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6) / 6 = 7/2$.
  central_moment: >-
    The first central moment of a fair die roll $X$ is $\mathbb{E}[X - 7/2] = 7/2 - 7/2 = 0$, because the deviations from the mean average to zero.
