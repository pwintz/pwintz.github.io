title: 'Two $1$ kg carts moving toward each other at $2$ m/s each, which collide and stick together'
level: high-school
values:
  momentum: '$p = 0$ kg·m/s, before and after the collision'
  energy: 'Kinetic energy $K = 4$ J before the collision and $K = 0$ J after'
why:
  momentum: >-
    The total momentum of two $1$ kg carts moving toward each other at $2$ m/s is $p = 1 \cdot 2 + 1 \cdot (-2) = 0$ kg·m/s, because the momenta of the carts point in opposite directions and cancel.
    The total momentum stays $0$ in the collision, so the carts stop after they stick together.
  energy: >-
    The total kinetic energy of two $1$ kg carts moving toward each other at $2$ m/s is $K = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1 \cdot 2^2 = 4$ J, because kinetic energies are positive numbers that add, whatever the directions.
    When the carts stick together and stop, the $4$ J of kinetic energy becomes heat and sound, so the total energy is still conserved.
