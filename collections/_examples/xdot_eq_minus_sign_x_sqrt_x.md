title: '$\dot{x} = -\operatorname{sign}(x) \sqrt{\abs{x}}$'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  lyapunov_stable,
  attractive,
  asymptotically_stable,
  globally_asymptotically_stable,
  finite_time_stable
]
why:
  finite_time_stable: >-
    The origin of $\dot{x} = -\operatorname{sign}(x) \sqrt{\abs{x}}$ is finite-time stable because a solution starting at $x_0 > 0$ reaches $0$ at time $2 \sqrt{x_0}$.
  globally_asymptotically_stable: >-
    The origin of $\dot{x} = -\operatorname{sign}(x) \sqrt{\abs{x}}$ is Lyapunov stable, attractive, and globally asymptotically stable, because it is finite-time stable.
