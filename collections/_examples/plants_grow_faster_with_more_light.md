---
title: 'Before an experiment: "Bean plants grow taller with more hours of light each day"'
level: elementary
is: [hypothesis_science]
is_not: [
  theory_science,
  scientific_law,
  conjecture
]
why:
  hypothesis_science: >-
    "Bean plants grow taller with more hours of light" is a hypothesis, because it is a prediction that an experiment can test and could show to be false.
  conjecture: >-
    The hypothesis "Bean plants grow taller with more hours of light each day" is not a conjecture, because experiments, not proofs, decide it.
  theory_science: >-
    The hypothesis "Bean plants grow taller with more hours of light each day" is not a theory, because no body of evidence supports it yet.
  scientific_law: >-
    The hypothesis "Bean plants grow taller with more hours of light each day" is not a law, because it has not been observed to hold in every tested case.
---
