title: 'One side of a square'
level: elementary
is: [edge_polygon]
is_not: [boundary]
why:
  edge_polygon: >-
    One side of a square is an edge of the square, viewed as a polygon.
  boundary: >-
    One side of a square is not the boundary of the square, because the boundary consists of all four sides.
