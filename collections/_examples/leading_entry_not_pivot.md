title: 'The $2$ in row $1$ of $$\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & 4 \\ 1 & 3 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$$'
level: high-school
is: [leading_entry]
is_not: [pivot]
why:
  pivot: >-
    The leading $2$ in row $1$ is not a pivot, because the matrix is not in echelon form, and the $2$'s position, $(1, 2)$, is not a pivot position.
  leading_entry: >-
    The $2$ is the leading entry of row $1$, because it is the first nonzero entry of that row.
