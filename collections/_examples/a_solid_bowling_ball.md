---
title: 'A solid bowling ball'
level: high-school
is: [
  ball,
  closed_ball
]
is_not: [sphere]
why:
  sphere: >-
    A solid bowling ball is not a sphere, which would be only the surface.
  closed_ball: >-
    A solid bowling ball is a closed ball, and so a ball, because it includes the inside and the surface.
---
