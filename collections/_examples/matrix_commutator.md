title: 'The commutator $(A, B) \mapsto A B - B A$ of $n \times n$ real matrices'
level: graduate
is: [lie_bracket]
why:
  lie_bracket: >-
    The commutator $[A, B] = A B - B A$ of $n \times n$ matrices is a Lie bracket, because it is bilinear, $[A, B] = -[B, A]$, and expanding the products shows that the Jacobi identity holds.
