title: A rainbow, formed by sunlight in raindrops
level: high-school
is: [
  reflection_wave,
  refraction,
  dispersion_wave
]
why:
  reflection_wave: >-
    A rainbow involves reflection, because the sunlight in each raindrop reflects off of the back of the drop before the light leaves the drop.
  refraction: >-
    A rainbow involves refraction, because sunlight bends as the light enters a raindrop and again as the light leaves the drop.
  dispersion_wave: >-
    A rainbow involves dispersion, because the speed of light in water depends on the frequency of the light, so each color is refracted by a different angle, which spreads the colors apart.
