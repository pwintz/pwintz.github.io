published: false
title: Two speakers playing the same $340$ Hz tone in phase, heard at a point $2$ m from one speaker and $2.5$ m from the other
level: high-school
description: >-
  The speed of sound in the air is $340$ m/s, so the wavelength of the tone is $1$ m.
is: [
  interference,
  destructive_interference
]
is_not: [constructive_interference]
why:
  interference: >-
    The sounds from two speakers that reach the same point at the same time interfere, because the air pressure at the point is the sum of the pressures of the two sounds.
  destructive_interference: >-
    Two in-phase speakers playing a $340$ Hz tone, heard $2$ m from one speaker and $2.5$ m from the other, have destructive interference, because the difference of $0.5$ m between the distances is half a wavelength, so the peaks of each sound arrive with the troughs of the other.
  constructive_interference: >-
    Two in-phase speakers playing a $340$ Hz tone, heard $2$ m from one speaker and $2.5$ m from the other, do not have constructive interference, because the difference of $0.5$ m between the distances is half a wavelength, not a whole number of wavelengths.
---
If the two speakers are equally loud at the point, then the tone is nearly silent there.
In a real room, reflections from the walls also reach the point, so the cancellation is incomplete.
