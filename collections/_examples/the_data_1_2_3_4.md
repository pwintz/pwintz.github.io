---
title: 'The data $1, 2, 3, 4$'
level: high-school
values:
  variance: '$\frac{(1.5)^2 + (0.5)^2 + (0.5)^2 + (1.5)^2}{4} = \frac{5}{4} = 1.25$'
  sample_variance: '$\frac{(1.5)^2 + (0.5)^2 + (0.5)^2 + (1.5)^2}{3} = \frac{5}{3} \approx 1.67$'
why:
  variance: >-
    The variance of the data $1, 2, 3, 4$ divides the sum $5$ of the squared distances from the mean $2.5$ by the number of data points, $4$.
  sample_variance: >-
    The sample variance of the data $1, 2, 3, 4$ divides the sum $5$ of the squared distances from the mean $2.5$ by $4 - 1 = 3$.
---
