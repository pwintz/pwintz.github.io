---
title: '$P \implies Q$ and $\neg Q \implies \neg P$'
level: high-school
is: [equivalent]
is_not: [equal]
why:
  equivalent: >-
    $P \implies Q$ and its contrapositive $\neg Q \implies \neg P$ are logically equivalent, because each is true exactly when the other is.
  equal: >-
    $P \implies Q$ and its contrapositive $\neg Q \implies \neg P$ are not equal, because they are different statements.
---
