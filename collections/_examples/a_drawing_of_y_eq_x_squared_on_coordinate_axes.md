---
title: 'A drawing of $y = x^2$ on coordinate axes'
level: elementary
is: [
  graph_of_function,
  graph_chart,
  plot
]
is_not: [
  graph_network,
  diagram
]
why:
  graph_chart: >-
    The drawing of $y = x^2$ is a chart, because it is a picture that displays information.
  diagram: >-
    The drawing is not a diagram, because its positions encode numbers.
  graph_of_function: >-
    The drawing of $y = x^2$ is a picture of the graph of $x \mapsto x^2$.
  graph_network: >-
    The drawing of $y = x^2$ is not a network, because it has no vertices or edges.
---
