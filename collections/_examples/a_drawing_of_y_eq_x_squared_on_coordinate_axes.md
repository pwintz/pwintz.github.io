---
title: 'A drawing of $y = x^2$ on coordinate axes'
level: elementary
is: [graph_of_function, graph_chart, plot]
is_not: [graph_network, diagram]
why:
  graph_chart: >-
    The drawing is a chart, and it is a picture of the graph of $x \mapsto x^2$.
    It is not a network, because it has no vertices or edges.
  diagram: >-
    The drawing is not a diagram, because its positions encode numbers.
  graph_of_function: >-
    The drawing is a plot of the graph of $x \mapsto x^2$.
---
