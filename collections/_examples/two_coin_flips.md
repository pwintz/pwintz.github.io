title: 'The results of two separate flips of a fair coin'
level: high-school
values:
  correlation: 'Uncorrelated'
  causation: 'Neither causes the other'
  statistical_independence: 'Independent'
why:
  correlation: >-
    The results of two separate coin flips are uncorrelated, because the results are independent and independent random variables with finite variances are uncorrelated.
  causation: >-
    The result of one coin flip does not cause the result of a separate coin flip, because the flips have no physical connection.
  statistical_independence: >-
    The results of two separate flips of a fair coin are independent, because each of the $4$ pairs of results has probability $\frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}$.
