---
title: 'The vertex sequence $a, b, c$ in the triangle graph with vertices $a$, $b$, $c$ and edges $\setdef{a, b}$, $\setdef{b, c}$, $\setdef{c, a}$'
level: undergraduate
is: [path_graph]
is_not: [path_continuous]
why:
  path_graph: >-
    The vertex sequence $a, b, c$ is a path from $a$ to $c$ in the triangle graph, because the vertices $a$, $b$, $c$ are distinct and the edges $\setdef{a, b}$ and $\setdef{b, c}$ join consecutive vertices.
  path_continuous: >-
    The vertex sequence $a, b, c$ is not a continuous path, because it is a finite list of vertices, not a continuous function from $[0, 1]$ to a topological space.
---
