---
title: 'The theory of evolution by natural selection'
level: high-school
is: [theory_science]
is_not: [
  hypothesis_science,
  scientific_law
]
why:
  theory_science: >-
    Evolution by natural selection is a theory, because it explains a broad range of observations (fossils, genetics, the distribution of species) and is supported by a large body of evidence.
  scientific_law: >-
    Evolution by natural selection is not a law because it explains why species change rather than describing one pattern.
  hypothesis_science: >-
    Evolution by natural selection is not a hypothesis because it has be demonstrated via experiments.
---
