title: Two speakers playing the same $340$ Hz tone in phase, heard at a point $2$ m from one speaker and $3$ m from the other
level: high-school
description: >-
  The speed of sound in the air is $340$ m/s, so the wavelength of the tone is $1$ m.
is: [
  interference,
  constructive_interference
]
is_not: [destructive_interference]
why:
  interference: >-
    The sounds from two speakers that reach the same point at the same time interfere, because the air pressure at the point is the sum of the pressures of the two sounds.
  constructive_interference: >-
    Two in-phase speakers playing a $340$ Hz tone, heard $2$ m from one speaker and $3$ m from the other, have constructive interference, because the difference of $1$ m between the distances is a whole wavelength, so the peaks of the two sounds arrive together.
  destructive_interference: >-
    Two in-phase speakers playing a $340$ Hz tone, heard $2$ m from one speaker and $3$ m from the other, do not have destructive interference, because the difference of $1$ m between the distances is a whole wavelength, not an odd number of half wavelengths.
