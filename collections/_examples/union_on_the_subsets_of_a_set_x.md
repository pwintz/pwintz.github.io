---
published: false
title: '$\cup$ on the subsets of a set $X$'
level: high-school
is: [binary_operation]
is_not: [relation]
why:
  binary_operation: >-
    $\cup$ is a binary operation on the subsets of $X$ because the union of two subsets of $X$ is a subset of $X$.
  relation: >-
    $\cup$ is not a relation, because "$A \cup B$" is not true or false.
---
