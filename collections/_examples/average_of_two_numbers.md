title: 'The average $a \ast b = \frac{a + b}{2}$ of two real numbers'
level: high-school
is: [commutative]
is_not: [associative]
why:
  commutative: >-
    The average of two numbers is commutative, because $\frac{a + b}{2} = \frac{b + a}{2}$.
  associative: >-
    The average of two numbers is not associative, because $(0 \ast 0) \ast 4 = 0 \ast 4 = 2$, but $0 \ast (0 \ast 4) = 0 \ast 2 = 1$.
