title: 'The circle $r = 1$, for $\dot{r} = r (r - 1)$, $\dot{\theta} = 1$ in polar coordinates'
level: undergraduate
is: [limit_cycle, omega_limit_set]
is_not: [attractive, attractor]
why:
  limit_cycle: >-
    The circle $r = 1$ is an isolated periodic orbit, so it is a limit cycle, and it is the ω-limit set of each of its points.
  attractive: >-
    The circle $r = 1$, for $\dot{r} = r (r - 1)$, $\dot{\theta} = 1$ in polar coordinates, is not attractive or an attractor, because solutions starting inside it spiral toward the origin, and solutions starting outside it go to infinity.
