title: 'Modus ponens: from $p$ and $p \implies q$, infer $q$'
level: high-school
is: [rule_of_inference]
why:
  rule_of_inference: >-
    Modus ponens is a rule of inference, because modus ponens says that every statement of the form $q$ may be concluded from statements of the forms $p$ and $p \implies q$.
