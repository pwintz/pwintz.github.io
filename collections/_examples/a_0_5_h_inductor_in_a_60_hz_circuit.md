title: A $0.5$ H inductor in a $60$ Hz circuit
level: undergraduate
values:
  resistance: '$R = 0$ Ω'
  reactance: '$X \approx 188.5$ Ω'
  impedance: '$Z \approx 188.5 i$ Ω'
why:
  resistance: >-
    The resistance of an ideal $0.5$ H inductor is $0$, because an ideal inductor converts no energy into heat.
  reactance: >-
    The reactance of a $0.5$ H inductor at $60$ Hz is

    $$X = 2 \pi f L = 2 \pi \cdot 60 \cdot 0.5 \approx 188.5 \text{ Ω}.$$
  impedance: >-
    The impedance of a $0.5$ H inductor at $60$ Hz is $Z = i \omega L \approx 188.5 i$ Ω, which is purely imaginary, so the voltage leads the current by a quarter cycle.
