---
title: 'Tricomi equation $y u_{xx} + u_{yy} = 0$'
level: undergraduate
values:
  elliptic_pde: 'For $y > 0$'
  parabolic_pde: 'On the line $y = 0$'
  hyperbolic_pde: 'For $y < 0$'
why:
  hyperbolic_pde: >-
    The Tricomi equation is hyperbolic for $y < 0$, because its coefficients $A = y$, $B = 0$, and $C = 1$ give $B^2 - AC = -y > 0$ there.
  elliptic_pde: >-
    The Tricomi equation is elliptic for $y > 0$, because its coefficients $A = y$, $B = 0$, and $C = 1$ give $B^2 - AC = -y < 0$ there.
  parabolic_pde: >-
    The Tricomi equation is parabolic on the line $y = 0$, because its coefficients $A = y$, $B = 0$, and $C = 1$ give $B^2 - AC = -y = 0$ there.
---
