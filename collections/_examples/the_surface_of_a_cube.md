---
title: 'The surface of a cube'
level: high-school
is: [sphere_topological]
is_not: [sphere]
why:
  sphere: >-
    The surface of a cube is not a (geometric) sphere, because its points are not all at the same distance from the center.
  sphere_topological: >-
    The surface of a cube is a topological sphere, because it can be inflated into a sphere.
---
