---
title: 'Choosing a committee of $2$ members from a club of $10$ members'
level: high-school
is: [combination]
is_not: [permutation]
values:
  binomial_coefficient: '$\binom{10}{2} = 45$'
why:
  combination: >-
    Each committee of $2$ members chosen from $10$ members is a combination of $2$ of the members, because a committee is a set of members, with no order.
  permutation: >-
    A committee of $2$ members chosen from $10$ members is not a permutation, because the order does not matter: Ana and Ben is the same committee as Ben and Ana.
  binomial_coefficient: >-
    Choosing a committee of $2$ from $10$ members has $\binom{10}{2} = (10 \cdot 9) / 2 = 45$ outcomes, which is half the $90$ ordered choices, because each committee can be ordered in $2$ ways.
---
