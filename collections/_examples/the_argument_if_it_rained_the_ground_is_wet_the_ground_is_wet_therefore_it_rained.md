title: 'The argument "If it rained, then the ground is wet; the ground is wet; therefore, it rained"'
level: high-school
is: [argument_logic]
is_not: [
  valid_argument,
  sound_argument
]
why:
  argument_logic: >-
    The rain argument is an argument, with the premises "If it rained, then the ground is wet" and "The ground is wet" and the conclusion "It rained."
  valid_argument: >-
    The rain argument is not valid, because the premises can be true while the conclusion is false: a sprinkler can wet the ground when it did not rain.
    This invalid form is called _affirming the consequent_.
  sound_argument: >-
    The rain argument is not sound, because every sound argument is valid, and the rain argument is not valid.
---
