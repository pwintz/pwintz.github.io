title: 'Turning from $(1, 0)$ to $(0, -1)$ in the plane'
level: high-school
description: >-
  The vector $(1, 0)$ points east, and the vector $(0, -1)$ points south.
values:
  angle: '$\theta = 90^\circ$'
  signed_angle: '$\theta = -90^\circ$'
why:
  angle: >-
    The angle between $(1, 0)$ and $(0, -1)$ is $90^\circ$, because their dot product is $0$.
  signed_angle: >-
    The signed angle from $(1, 0)$ to $(0, -1)$ is $-90^\circ$, because turning east toward south the short way is clockwise.
