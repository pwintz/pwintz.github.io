---
title: '$A = \pi r^2$'
level: high-school
is: [
  equation,
  formula
]
is_not: [identity_equation]
why:
  identity_equation: >-
    The equation $A = \pi r^2$ is not an identity because is not true for every choice of variables $A$ and $r$, such as for $A = 0$ and $r = 1$.
  equation: >-
    $A = \pi r^2$ is an equation, because it says that two expressions are equal.
  formula: >-
    $A = \pi r^2$ is a formula, because it gives the area $A$ in terms of the radius $r$.
---
