---
title: 'A function with $f^{\prime}(x) = 0$ for every $x$ in an interval is constant on the interval, as a consequence of the mean value theorem'
level: undergraduate
is: [corollary]
is_not: [conjecture]
why:
  conjecture: >-
    "A function with zero derivative on an interval is not a conjecture, because it is proved.
  corollary: >-
    "A function with zero derivative on an interval is constant" is a corollary of the mean value theorem, because it follows by applying that theorem to two points of the interval.
