title: 'The indicator functions of the interval $[0, 1]$, as a subset of $\reals$, at the point $x = 2$'
level: undergraduate
values:
  indicator_function: '$\mathbf{1}_{[0, 1]}(2) = 0$'
  indicator_function_convex: '$\delta_{[0, 1]}(2) = \infty$'
why:
  indicator_function: >-
    The 0–1 indicator function of $[0, 1]$ is $0$ at $x = 2$, because $2$ is not in $[0, 1]$.
  indicator_function_convex: >-
    The convex-analysis indicator function of $[0, 1]$ is $\infty$ at $x = 2$, because $2$ is not in $[0, 1]$.
    Adding $\delta_{[0, 1]}$ to an objective function makes every point outside $[0, 1]$ infinitely costly.
