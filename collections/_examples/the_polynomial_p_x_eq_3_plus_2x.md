---
title: 'The polynomial $p(x) = 3 + 2x$'
level: high-school
is: [vector]
is_not: [coordinate_vector]
why:
  vector: >-
    $p(x) = 3 + 2x$ is a vector in the space of polynomials of degree 1 or less.
  coordinate_vector: >-
    $3 + 2x$ is not a coordinate vector, because it is not a list of numbers.
---
