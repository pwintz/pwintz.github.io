---
title: '$(x, y) \mapsto x y + 1$ on $\reals^2$'
level: undergraduate
is_not: [
  bilinear_form,
  inner_product
]
why:
  bilinear_form: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x y + 1$ is not a bilinear form, because it is not linear in $x$: at $x = 0$, the output is $1$, not $0$.
  inner_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto x y + 1$ is not a inner_product because it is not a bilinear form.
---
