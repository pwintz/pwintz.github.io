title: '$A = \setdef{1, 2, 3}$ and $B = \setdef{2, 3, 4}$'
level: elementary
values:
  set_union: '$A \cup B = \setdef{1, 2, 3, 4}$'
  set_intersection: '$A \cap B = \setdef{2, 3}$'
  set_difference: '$A \setminus B = \setdef{1}$'
why:
  set_difference: >-
    In the other order, $B \setminus A = \setdef{4}$.
  set_intersection: >-
    The union has everything in $A$ or $B$ (or both), and the intersection has everything in both.
