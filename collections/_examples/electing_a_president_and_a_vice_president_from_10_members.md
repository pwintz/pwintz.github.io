---
title: 'Electing a president and a vice president from a club of $10$ members'
level: high-school
is: [permutation]
is_not: [combination]
values:
  number_of_permutations: '$P(10, 2) = 90$'
why:
  permutation: >-
    Each outcome of electing a president and a vice president from $10$ members is a permutation of $2$ of the members, because the outcome lists who is president first and who is vice president second.
  combination: >-
    An outcome of electing a president and a vice president from $10$ members is not a combination, because the order matters: Ana as president and Ben as vice president is a different outcome from Ben as president and Ana as vice president.
  number_of_permutations: >-
    Electing a president and a vice president from $10$ members has $P(10, 2) = 10 \cdot 9 = 90$ outcomes, because there are $10$ choices for president and then $9$ for vice president.
---
