title: 'The vectors $(1, 0)$, $(0, 1)$, and $(1, 1)$ in $\reals^2$'
level: high-school
is: [span]
is_not: [vector_basis, linearly_independent]
why:
  linearly_independent: >-
    The vectors $(1, 0)$, $(0, 1)$, and $(1, 1)$ are not linearly independent, because $(1, 1) = (1, 0) + (0, 1)$.
  vector_basis: >-
    The vectors $(1, 0)$, $(0, 1)$, and $(1, 1)$ are not a basis of $\reals^2$.
    They span $\reals^2$, but they are not independent: $(1, 1) = (1, 0) + (0, 1)$, so $(1, 1)$ can be built in more than one way.
