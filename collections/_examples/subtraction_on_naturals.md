---
title: 'Subtraction on $\nats$'
level: high-school
is_not: [binary_operation, relation]
why:
  binary_operation: >-
    Subtraction is not a binary operation on $\nats$, because $3 - 5$ is not in $\nats$ (it is one on $\mathbb{Z}$).
  relation: >-
    Subtraction is not a relation, because "$5 - 3$" is not true or false.
---
