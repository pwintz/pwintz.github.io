title: '$\reals \setdiff \rationals$, as a subset of $\reals$'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  dense_set,
  almost_everywhere,
  full_measure
]
why:
  dense_set: >-
    $\reals \setdiff \rationals$ is dense because every interval contains an irrational number.
    It contains almost every real number because its complement $\rationals$ has measure zero.
  full_measure: >-
    $\reals \setdiff \rationals$ has full measure in $\reals$, because its complement $\rationals$ is countable and so has measure zero.
---
