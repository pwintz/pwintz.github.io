---
title: '$1$'
level: elementary
is: [
  natural_numbers_from_one,
  natural_numbers_from_zero,
  whole_numbers
]
why:
  natural_numbers_from_one: >-
    $1$ is a natural number under both conventions: it is the first natural number when the natural numbers start at $1$, and the second when they start at $0$.
