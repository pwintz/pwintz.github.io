title: 'The events "the roll is at least $4$" and "the roll is even," for one roll of a fair six-sided die'
level: high-school
is_not: [
  mutually_exclusive,
  statistical_independence
]
why:
  mutually_exclusive: >-
    The events "the roll is at least $4$" and "the roll is even" are not mutually exclusive, because rolls of $4$ and $6$ belong to both events.
  statistical_independence: >-
    The events "the roll is at least $4$" and "the roll is even" are not independent, because $P(\text{both}) = P(\setdef{4, 6}) = 1/3$, but $P(\text{at least } 4) P(\text{even}) = 1/2 \cdot 1/2 = 1/4$.
