title: An echo of a shout off of a cliff
level: elementary
is: [reflection_wave]
why:
  reflection_wave: >-
    An echo of a shout off of a cliff is a reflection of a wave, because the sound wave of the shout bounces off of the cliff and travels back to the person who shouted.
---
The echo arrives after the time that the sound takes to travel to the cliff and back.
Sound travels about $343$ m/s in air, so the echo from a cliff $170$ m away arrives about $1$ s after the shout.
