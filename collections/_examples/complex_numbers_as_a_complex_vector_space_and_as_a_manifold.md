title: '$\complexes$, as a complex vector space and as a manifold'
level: undergraduate
values:
  dimension_vector_space: '$\dim_{\complexes} \complexes = 1$'
  dimension_manifold: '$2$'
why:
  dimension_vector_space: >-
    The dimension of $\complexes$ as a complex vector space is $1$, because the single vector $1$ is a basis of $\complexes$ over $\complexes$.
  dimension_manifold: >-
    The dimension of $\complexes$ as a manifold is $2$, because the map $a + b i \mapsto (a, b)$ is a chart from $\complexes$ onto $\reals^2$.
