---
title: '$[0, 1]$, for $\dot{x} = u$ with $\abs{u} \leq 1$'
level: graduate
is: [controlled_invariant]
is_not: [forward_invariant]
why:
  controlled_invariant: >-
    $[0, 1]$ is controlled invariant because the input $u = 0$ keeps solutions in place.
  forward_invariant: >-
    $[0, 1]$ is not forward invariant for every input, because $u = 1$ pushes solutions out through $1$.
---
