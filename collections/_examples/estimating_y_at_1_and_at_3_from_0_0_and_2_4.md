values:
  interpolation: '$y(1) = 2$'
  extrapolation: '$y(3) = 6$'
why:
  interpolation: >-
    Estimating $y(1) = 2$ from the data points $(0, 0)$ and $(2, 4)$ is interpolation, because $x = 1$ lies between the data points $x = 0$ and $x = 2$.
  extrapolation: >-
    Estimating $y(3) = 6$ from the data points $(0, 0)$ and $(2, 4)$ is extrapolation, because $x = 3$ lies beyond the largest data point $x = 2$.
