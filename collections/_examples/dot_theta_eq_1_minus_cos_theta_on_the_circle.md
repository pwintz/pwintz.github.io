---
published: false
title: '$\dot{\theta} = 1 - \cos \theta$, on the circle'
level: undergraduate
is: [attractive]
is_not: [lyapunov_stable, asymptotically_stable]
why:
  attractive: >-
    The equilibrium $\theta = 0$ is attractive because every solution converges to it.
  asymptotically_stable: >-
    $\dot{\theta} = 1 - \cos \theta$, on the circle, is not Lyapunov stable, and so not asymptotically stable, because solutions starting just after $0$ first travel all the way around the circle.
---
