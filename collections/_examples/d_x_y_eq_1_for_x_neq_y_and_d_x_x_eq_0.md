---
title: '$d(x, y) = 1$ for $x \neq y$, and $d(x, x) = 0$'
level: undergraduate
definition: discrete_metric
is: [
  metric,
  discrete_metric
]
is_not: [norm]
why:
  metric: >-
    The discrete metric is a metric.
  norm: >-
    The discrete metric does not come from a norm, because it does not scale: $d(2x, 0) = 1 = d(x, 0)$ for every $x \neq 0$.
---
