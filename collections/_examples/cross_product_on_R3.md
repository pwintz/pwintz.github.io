title: 'The cross product $(u, v) \mapsto u \times v$ on $\reals^3$'
level: graduate
is: [lie_bracket]
why:
  lie_bracket: >-
    The cross product on $\reals^3$ is a Lie bracket, because it is bilinear, $u \times v = -v \times u$, and $u \times (v \times w) + v \times (w \times u) + w \times (u \times v) = 0$.
    It is the Lie bracket of the Lie algebra of rotations of $\reals^3$, written in coordinates.
