published: false
title: '$x$, in $x + 1$'
level: elementary
is: [term_of_sum]
is_not: [factor]
why:
  term_of_sum: >-
    $x$ is a term of $x + 1$ because it is one of the parts being added.
  factor: >-
    $x$ is not a factor, because inside $x + 1$, the variable $x$ is added, not multiplied.
---
