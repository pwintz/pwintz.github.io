---
title: '$(x, y) \mapsto 2 x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ on $\reals^2$'
level: undergraduate
is: [bilinear_form, inner_product]
is_not: [dot_product]
why:
  dot_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto 2 x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ is not the dot product, because of the factor $2$.
  inner_product: >-
    $(x, y) \mapsto 2 x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2$ is a bilinear form, and it is an inner product because it is symmetric and positive definite.
---
