title: '$3x^2$, in $3x^2 - 5x + 2$'
level: elementary
is: [term_of_sum, expression]
is_not: [factor]
why:
  factor: >-
    $3x^2$ is not a factor, because $3x^2 - 5x + 2$ is not a product with $3x^2$ as one of its parts.
  expression: >-
    $3x^2$ is a term of $3x^2 - 5x + 2$ because it is one of the parts being added, and it is an expression.
