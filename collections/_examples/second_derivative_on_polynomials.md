title: 'The second derivative $p \mapsto p^{\prime\prime}$ on polynomials with real coefficients'
level: undergraduate
is_not: [derivation]
why:
  derivation: >-
    The second derivative $p \mapsto p^{\prime\prime}$ on polynomials is not a derivation, because it fails the product rule: for $p(x) = q(x) = x$, $(p q)^{\prime\prime} = (x^2)^{\prime\prime} = 2$, but $p^{\prime\prime} q + p q^{\prime\prime} = 0$.
