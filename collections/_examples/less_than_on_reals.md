---
title: 'The relation $<$ on $\reals$'
level: undergraduate
is: [relation]
is_not: [
  function,
  operator,
  binary_operation
]
values:
  relation: '$\setdef{(x, y) \in \reals \times \reals \suchthat x \text{ is less than } y}$'
why:
  relation: >-
    The relation $<$ on $\reals$ is the set of pairs $(x, y)$ such that $x$ is less than $y$, so $2 < 3$ is true because $(2, 3)$ is in the set, and $3 < 2$ is false because $(3, 2)$ is not in the set.
  function: >-
    The relation $<$ on $\reals$ is not a function, because it relates $0$ to every positive number, not to exactly one number.
  operator: >-
    The relation $<$ on $\reals$ is not an operator, because it is not a function.
---
