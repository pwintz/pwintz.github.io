---
title: '$\setdef{\emptyset, \setdef{1}, \setdef{1, 2}}$, on $X = \setdef{1, 2}$'
level: undergraduate
is: [topology]
is_not: [sigma_algebra]
why:
  sigma_algebra: >-
    The collection $\setdef{\emptyset, \setdef{1}, \setdef{1, 2}}$ is not a σ-algebra on $\setdef{1, 2}$, because it does not contain $\setdef{2}$, the complement of $\setdef{1}$.
  topology: >-
    The collection $\setdef{\emptyset, \setdef{1}, \setdef{1, 2}}$ is a topology on $\setdef{1, 2}$, because it contains $\emptyset$ and $\setdef{1, 2}$ and is closed under unions and intersections.
---
