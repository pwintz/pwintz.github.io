---
published: false
title: '$\lbrace \emptyset, \lbrace 1 \rbrace, \lbrace 1, 2 \rbrace \rbrace$, on $X = \lbrace 1, 2 \rbrace$'
level: undergraduate
is: [topology]
is_not: [sigma_algebra]
why:
  sigma_algebra: >-
    The collection $\lbrace \emptyset, \lbrace 1 \rbrace, \lbrace 1, 2 \rbrace \rbrace$ is not a σ-algebra on $\lbrace 1, 2 \rbrace$, because it does not contain $\lbrace 2 \rbrace$, the complement of $\lbrace 1 \rbrace$.
