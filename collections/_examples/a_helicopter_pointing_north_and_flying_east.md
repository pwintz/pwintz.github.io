title: 'A helicopter pointing north and flying east'
level: high-school
description: >-
  A helicopter points north and flies sideways toward the east, with no wind.
values:
  heading: '$0^\circ$'
  course: '$90^\circ$'
why:
  heading: >-
    The heading of the helicopter pointing north and flying east is $0^\circ$ because the nose of the helicopter points north.
  course: >-
    The course of the helicopter pointing north and flying east is $90^\circ$ because the helicopter moves east.
