title: 'An eigendecomposition $A = P D P^{-1}$, with $D$ diagonal'
level: undergraduate
is_not: [singular_value_decomposition]
why:
  singular_value_decomposition: >-
    The columns of $P$ need not be orthonormal, and the eigenvalues in $D$ can be negative or complex.
    For a symmetric positive semidefinite matrix, the two decompositions coincide.
