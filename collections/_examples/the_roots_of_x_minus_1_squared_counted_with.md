---
title: 'The roots of $(x - 1)^2$ counted with multiplicity'
level: high-school
is: [multiset]
is_not: [set]
why:
  multiset: >-
    The roots of $(x - 1)^2$ counted with multiplicity form a multiset, in which the root $1$ appears twice.
  set: >-
    The roots of $(x - 1)^2$ counted with multiplicity does not form a set, which cannot count an element twice.
---
