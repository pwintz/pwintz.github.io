---
title: 'The imaginary unit $i$'
definition: imaginary_unit
level: high-school
description: >-
  The number $i$ such that $i^2 = -1$.
is_not: [real_number]
why:
  real_number: >-
    The imaginary unit $i$ is not a real number, because $i^2 = -1$ is negative and the square of every real number is nonnegative.
---
