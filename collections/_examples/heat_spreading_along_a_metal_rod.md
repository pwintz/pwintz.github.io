title: Heat spreading along a metal rod from an end that is held in a flame
level: high-school
is: [
  conduction,
  diffusion,
  heat_diffusion
]
is_not: [
  convection,
  particle_diffusion
]
why:
  conduction: >-
    Heat spreading along a metal rod from an end held in a flame is conduction, because the heat flows through the rod from the hot end toward the cold end without the metal moving.
  diffusion: >-
    Heat spreading along a metal rod from an end held in a flame is the diffusion of heat, because the temperature in the rod satisfies the diffusion equation, which is called the heat equation.
  heat_diffusion: >-
    Heat spreading along a metal rod from an end held in a flame is heat diffusion, because the temperature of the rod evens out as heat is passed between neighboring atoms and electrons, following the heat equation.
  particle_diffusion: >-
    Heat spreading along a metal rod is not particle diffusion, because no substance travels along the rod: the atoms of the metal stay in place, and only energy moves.
  convection: >-
    Heat spreading along a metal rod is not convection, because the metal does not flow.
---
