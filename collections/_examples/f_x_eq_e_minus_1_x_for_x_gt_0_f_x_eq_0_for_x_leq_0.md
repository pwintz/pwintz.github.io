title: '$f(x) = e^{-1/x}$ for $x > 0$, $f(x) = 0$ for $x \leq 0$'
level: undergraduate
is: [differentiable, continuously_differentiable, smooth]
is_not: [analytic]
why:
  smooth: >-
    The function $f$, with $f(x) = e^{-1/x}$ for $x > 0$ and $f(x) = 0$ for $x \leq 0$, is smooth because it has derivatives of every order, so it is also continuously differentiable and differentiable.
  analytic: >-
    The function $f$, with $f(x) = e^{-1/x}$ for $x > 0$ and $f(x) = 0$ for $x \leq 0$, is not analytic, because every derivative at $0$ is $0$, so its Taylor series at $0$ is $0$, but $f(x) > 0$ for $x > 0$.
