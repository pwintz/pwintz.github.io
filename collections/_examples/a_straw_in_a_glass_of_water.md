title: A straw in a glass of water looks bent at the surface of the water
level: elementary
is: [refraction]
is_not: [diffraction]
why:
  refraction: >-
    A straw in a glass of water looks bent because of refraction: the light from the part of the straw under the water bends as the light passes from the water into the air, where light travels faster.
  diffraction: >-
    The bent look of a straw in a glass of water is not caused by diffraction, because the light bends where the light changes speed at the surface of the water, not where the light passes the edge of an obstacle.
