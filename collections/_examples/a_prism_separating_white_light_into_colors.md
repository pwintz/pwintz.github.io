title: A glass prism separating white light into the colors of the rainbow
level: high-school
is: [
  refraction,
  dispersion_wave
]
is_not: [diffraction]
why:
  refraction: >-
    A glass prism refracts white light, because the light bends as the light enters the glass and again as the light leaves the glass, where the speed of light changes.
  dispersion_wave: >-
    A glass prism separating white light into colors is dispersion, because the speed of light in glass depends on the frequency of the light, so each color is refracted by a different angle.
  diffraction: >-
    A glass prism separating white light into colors is not diffraction, even though a diffraction grating also separates colors, because the prism separates the colors by bending light where the speed of light changes, not by the light bending around edges.
---
Violet light is bent the most and red light is bent the least.
A diffraction grating separates colors in the opposite order: red light spreads the most, because red light has the longest wavelength.
