---
title: 'The $x$-axis and the line through $(0, 0, 1)$ parallel to the $y$-axis in $\reals^3$'
level: high-school
is: [orthogonal_vectors]
is_not: [perpendicular, right_angle]
why:
  orthogonal_vectors: >-
    The $x$-axis and the line through $(0, 0, 1)$ parallel to the $y$-axis have orthogonal direction vectors $(1, 0, 0)$ and $(0, 1, 0)$, so the lines are orthogonal.
  perpendicular: >-
    The $x$-axis and the line through $(0, 0, 1)$ parallel to the $y$-axis are not perpendicular, because the lines never intersect: the first line has $z = 0$ and the second line has $z = 1$.
  right_angle: >-
    The $x$-axis and the line through $(0, 0, 1)$ parallel to the $y$-axis do not form a right angle, because the lines have no common point at which to form an angle.
---
