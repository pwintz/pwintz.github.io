---
title: 'The integers modulo $5$, $\setdef{0, 1, 2, 3, 4}$, with addition and multiplication modulo $5$'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  group,
  ring,
  field
]
why:
  group: >-
    The integers modulo $5$ form a group under addition modulo $5$, because $0$ is an identity and $5 - n$ is the inverse of $n$.
  ring: >-
    The integers modulo $5$, with addition and multiplication modulo $5$, form a ring, because the rules of integer arithmetic still hold after taking remainders.
  field: >-
    The integers modulo $5$ form a field, because every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse: $1 \cdot 1 = 1$, $2 \cdot 3 = 6 = 1$, and $4 \cdot 4 = 16 = 1$ modulo $5$.
---
