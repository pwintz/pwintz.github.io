published: false
title: '$\setdef{v \suchthat v_2 \geq -\abs{v_1}}$, for $S = \setdef{(x, y) \suchthat y \geq -\abs{x}}$ at the origin'
level: graduate
is: [contingent_cone]
is_not: [
  clarke_tangent_cone,
  tangent_space
]
why:
  contingent_cone: >-
    $\setdef{v \suchthat v_2 \geq -\abs{v_1}}$ is the contingent cone of $S$ at the origin, because $S$ is a cone, so its contingent cone there is $S$ itself.
  clarke_tangent_cone: >-
    The cone $\setdef{v \suchthat v_2 \geq -\abs{v_1}}$ is not the Clarke tangent cone of $S$, because $S$ is not convex, and the Clarke tangent cone is smaller.
  tangent_space: >-
    The cone $\setdef{v \suchthat v_2 \geq -\abs{v_1}}$ is not a tangent space, because it is not a vector space.
---
