title: 'The roll $X$ of a fair six-sided die, for $k = 2$'
level: high-school
values:
  statistical_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[X^2] = 91/6$'
  central_moment: '$\mathbb{E}[(X - 7/2)^2] = 35/12$'
why:
  statistical_moment: >-
    The second moment of a fair die roll $X$ is the average of the squares of the outcomes, $\mathbb{E}[X^2] = (1 + 4 + 9 + 16 + 25 + 36) / 6 = 91/6$.
  central_moment: >-
    The second central moment of a fair die roll $X$ is its variance:

    $$\mathbb{E}\bigl[(X - 7/2)^2\bigr] = \mathbb{E}[X^2] - \left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{91}{6} - \frac{49}{4} = \frac{35}{12}.$$
