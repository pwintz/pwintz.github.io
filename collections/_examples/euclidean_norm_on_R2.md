---
title: 'The Euclidean norm $x \mapsto \sqrt{x_1^2 + x_2^2}$ on $\reals^2$'
level: high-school
description: >-
  The Euclidean norm, which gives the length of a vector in the plane.
is: [norm]
is_not: [covector]
why:
  covector: >-
    The Euclidean norm on $\reals^2$ is not a covector, because it is not linear: the length of $-v$ is the length of $v$, not its negative.
  norm: >-
    $x \mapsto \sqrt{x_1^2 + x_2^2}$ is a norm: the Euclidean norm, which comes from the dot product.
---
