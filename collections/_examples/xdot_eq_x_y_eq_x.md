---
title: '$\dot{x} = x$, $y = x$'
level: undergraduate
is: [
  observable,
  reconstructible,
  detectable
]
why:
  observable: >-
    $\dot{x} = x$, $y = x$ is observable because the output is the state.
  reconstructible: >-
    $\dot{x} = x$, $y = x$ is reconstructible and detectable, because it is observable.
---
