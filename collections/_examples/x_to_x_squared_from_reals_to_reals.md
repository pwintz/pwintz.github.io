title: '$x \mapsto x^2$, from $\reals$ to $\reals$'
level: undergraduate
is: [continuous, closed_map]
is_not: [open_map]
why:
  open_map: >-
    $x \mapsto x^2$ is not an open map, because the image of $(-1, 1)$ is $[0, 1)$, which is not open.
  continuous: >-
    $x \mapsto x^2$ is continuous, and it is a closed map.
---
