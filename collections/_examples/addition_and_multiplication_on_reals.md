---
title: '$+$ and $\times$ on $\reals$'
level: high-school
is: [binary_operation]
is_not: [relation]
why:
  binary_operation: >-
    $+$ and $\times$ are binary operations on $\reals$ because they combine two real numbers into a new one: "$2 + 4$" produces $6$.
  relation: >-
    $+$ and $\times$ are not relations, because "$2 + 4$" does not have a true or false value.
---
