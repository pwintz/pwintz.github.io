published: false
title: 'Changing the cut points $0, 0.5, 1$ of $[0, 1]$ to $0, 0.4, 1$'
level: high-school
is_not: [refinement]
why:
  refinement: >-
    Changing the cut points $0, 0.5, 1$ to $0, 0.4, 1$ is not a refinement, because it removes the cut point $0.5$.
    A refinement keeps every old cut point and adds new ones.
---
