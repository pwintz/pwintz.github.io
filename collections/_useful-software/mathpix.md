title: MathPix
categories: latex research
homepage: https://mathpix.com/
excerpt: Optical Character Recognition (OCR) tool for generating LaTeX code based on handwritten and printed mathematics. 
summary: MathPix uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to convert photos, screenshots, and PDFs of mathematics into LaTeX code.
pricing: There is an extremely limited free plan, which only allows a small number of conversions per month. A subscription costs $5 per month (with a discount if paid annually).
alternatives:
  - Nowadays, you could likely use an LLM to convert a scan of mathematics in to LaTeX, but with lower reliability and higher cost.
