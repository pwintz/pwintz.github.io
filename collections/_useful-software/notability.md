Noteful does have a few downsides:
- No handwriting recognition, so it can be hard to search for content in handwritten notes.
- No audio transcriptions.

- Notability provides more AI features than Noteful, including handwriting-to-text conversion, speech transcription, and automatic generation of study notes.
