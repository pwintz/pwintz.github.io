summary: >
  A function from $\reals^n$ to $\reals^m$ has several derivatives in vector calculus, each combining its partial derivatives differently.
  The [Jacobian](/dictionary/definitions/jacobian) of a [vector field](/dictionary/definitions/vector-field) contains all of its first [partial derivatives](/dictionary/definitions/partial-derivative).
  The [divergence](/dictionary/definitions/divergence) (a scalar) and [curl](/dictionary/definitions/curl) (a vector) summarize parts of it.
  The [Laplacian](/dictionary/definitions/laplacian) is a second-order [operator](/dictionary/definitions/operator) on scalar functions.

## Notes

- The [divergence](/dictionary/definitions/divergence) is the trace of the [Jacobian](/dictionary/definitions/jacobian), and the [curl](/dictionary/definitions/curl) comes from its antisymmetric part.
- The divergence of a [vector field](/dictionary/definitions/vector-field) is unrelated to a [divergent](/dictionary/definitions/divergent) sequence.
