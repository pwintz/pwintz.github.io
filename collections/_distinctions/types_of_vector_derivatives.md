summary: >
  A function from $\reals^n$ to $\reals^m$ has several derivatives in vector calculus, each combining its partial derivatives differently.
  The [Jacobian](/definitions/jacobian) of a vector field contains all of its first [partial derivatives](/definitions/partial-derivative).
  The [divergence](/definitions/divergence) (a scalar) and [curl](/definitions/curl) (a vector) summarize parts of it.
  The [Laplacian](/definitions/laplacian) is a second-order [operator](/definitions/operator) on scalar functions.

## Notes

- The [divergence](/definitions/divergence) is the trace of the [Jacobian](/definitions/jacobian), and the [curl](/definitions/curl) comes from its antisymmetric part.
- The divergence of a vector field is unrelated to a [divergent](/definitions/divergent) sequence.
