## "In" is not "inside"

In everyday speech, we say that water is "in" a bottle.
In mathematics, "$x$ is in $A$" means $x \in A$, which is independent of geometry.
For example, the center of a [sphere](/definitions/sphere) is not in the sphere, but it is in the [ball](/definitions/ball) that the sphere surrounds.

Similarly, a polygon _inscribed_ in a [circle](/definitions/circle) has its vertices on the circle, so the vertices are elements of the circle, but the rest of the polygon is not.
