summary: >
  In mathematical writing, proved statements are labeled by their role.
  A theorem, a proposition, a lemma, and a [corollary](/dictionary/definitions/corollary) are all proved, and they differ only in importance and role: a theorem is a main result, a proposition is a lesser one, a lemma helps prove something else, and a corollary follows quickly from another result.
  A claim is a step inside a proof.
  A conjecture has not been proved.

## Notes

- The ranking of theorem, proposition, and lemma varies between authors; some papers have no propositions at all.
- "Proposition" as a label for a result is unrelated to a [proposition in logic](/dictionary/definitions/proposition), a statement that is true or false.
