## Notes

- Between [Hausdorff](/definitions/hausdorff-space) and normal are the _regular_ spaces (T₃), in which a point and a [closed set](/definitions/closed-set) not containing it have disjoint [neighborhoods](/definitions/neighborhood).
- The Urysohn metrization theorem says that a second-countable regular Hausdorff space is [metrizable](/definitions/metrizable).
