summary: >
  The mean, median, and mode are different ways to summarize the middle of data, and they can be far apart.
  "Average" usually means the mean. The expected value is the mean of a probability distribution rather than of data.
terms:
  - arithmetic_mean
  - average
  - median
  - mode
  - weighted_average
  - expected_value
examples_note: >-
  Each cell is the ___ of the data or distribution in that row.
examples:
  - example: data_with_outlier
    why: >-
      The outlier $100$ pulls the mean far above most of the data, but does not affect the median.
  - example: fair_die
    why: >-
      The expected value is not a possible outcome. Every outcome is equally likely, so every outcome is a mode.
  - example: credit_weighted_grades
    why: >-
      The credit-weighted grade point average is $(3 \cdot 4 + 1 \cdot 2) / 4 = 3.5$, while the unweighted mean is $3.0$.
