## Specific chart types that are often confused

- **Histogram vs. bar chart.** A histogram shows how many values fall in each range of a numerical variable, so its bars touch and their order is fixed. A bar chart compares separate categories, so its bars are separated and can be reordered.
- **Venn diagram vs. Euler diagram.** A Venn diagram shows every possible intersection of the sets, even empty ones. An Euler diagram shows only the relationships that actually occur.
- **Line chart vs. graph of a function.** A line chart connects data points with segments. The segments between points are not data.

"Graph" has several conflicting meanings (the graph of a function, a network, or any chart). See [the meanings of "graph"](/distinctions/meanings-of-graph).
