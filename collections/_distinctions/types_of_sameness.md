title: Types of sameness
kind: family
areas: [sets]
summary: >
  Mathematics has many ways to say that two objects are "the same."
  Equal objects are the same object.
  The other words describe objects that are different but "the same in some respect," and each one depends on what respect is meant.
terms:
  - equal
  - congruent
  - equivalent
  - isomorphic
  - identified
examples:
  - example: one_half_and_two_quarters
    why: >-
      In school, $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{2}{4}$ are called "equivalent fractions," because the fractions are written differently.
  - example: 3_and_10
  - example: two_3_4_5_triangles_in_different_places

  - example: p_rightarrow_q_and_neg_q_rightarrow_neg_p

## Notes

<!-- TODO: Add similar as a definition. -->
- "Similar" is another word in this family.
  Similar triangles have the same shape but possibly different sizes, and similar matrices $A$ and $B = P^{-1} A P$ represent the same [linear map](/definitions/linear-map) in different bases.
  
- Equality, congruence, isomorphism, and similarity are all [equivalence relations](/definitions/equivalence-relation), so each one splits objects into classes of "same in that respect."
