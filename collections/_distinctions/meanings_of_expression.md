summary: >
  "Expression" names a piece of mathematical writing in both school algebra and logic, but the two meanings disagree about equations and inequalities.
  In school algebra, an [expression](/dictionary/definitions/expression), such as $x^2 + 1$, has a value and contains no equals sign or inequality sign, so $x^2 \geq 0$ is an inequality, not an expression.
  In logic, an [expression](/dictionary/definitions/expression-logic) is any well-formed string of symbols, so $x^2 + 1$ is an expression (a term) and $x^2 \geq 0$ is an expression too (a formula).
