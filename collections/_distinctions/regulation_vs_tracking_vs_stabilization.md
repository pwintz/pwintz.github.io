## Notes

- Tracking can be posed as stabilization: the goal is to make the origin of the error dynamics, in $e = r - y$, asymptotically stable. For a time-varying reference, the error dynamics are time-varying.
- In output regulation theory (Francis and Wonham), "regulation" includes tracking references and rejecting disturbances generated by a known exosystem. The internal model principle says the controller must contain a model of that exosystem, such as an integrator for constant references.
