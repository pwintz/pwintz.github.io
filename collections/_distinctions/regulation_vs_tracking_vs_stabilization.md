## Notes

- [Tracking](/dictionary/definitions/tracking) can be posed as [stabilization](/dictionary/definitions/stabilization): the goal is to make the origin of the error dynamics, in $e = r - y$, [asymptotically stable](/dictionary/definitions/asymptotically-stable).
  For a time-varying reference, the error dynamics are time-varying.
- In output [regulation](/dictionary/definitions/regulation) theory (Francis and Wonham), "regulation" includes tracking references and rejecting [disturbances](/dictionary/definitions/disturbance) generated by a known exosystem.
  The internal model principle says the controller must contain a model of that exosystem, such as an integrator for constant references.
