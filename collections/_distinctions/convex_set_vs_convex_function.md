title: Convex set vs. convex function
kind: siblings
areas: [analysis, geometry]
summary: >
  "Convex" describes both sets and functions, and the two meanings are linked.
  A [convex set](/definitions/convex-set) contains the segment between any two of its points, and a [convex function](/definitions/convex-function) lies on or below the segment between any two points on its graph.
  A function is convex exactly when the region on and above its graph is a convex set.
