## Notes

- A set is unbounded if it does not fit in any [ball](/dictionary/definitions/ball): for every $M > 0$, it has two points farther than $M$ apart.
  This requires a notion of distance, whereas being infinite does not.
- "Infinitely many" and "arbitrarily large" are different.
  The set $\setdef{1/n \suchthat n \in \nats}$ has [infinitely many elements](/dictionary/definitions/infinite-set), none larger than $1$.
