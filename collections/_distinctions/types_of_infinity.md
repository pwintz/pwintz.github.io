## Notes

- A set is unbounded if it does not fit in any ball: for every $M > 0$, it has two points farther than $M$ apart. This requires a notion of distance, whereas being infinite does not.
- "Infinitely many" and "arbitrarily large" are different. The set $\lbrace 1/n : n \in \mathbb{N} \rbrace$ has infinitely many elements, none larger than $1$.
