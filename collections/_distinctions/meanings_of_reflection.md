  "Reflection" names both a physical event and a geometric transformation.
  In physics, the [reflection of a wave](/dictionary/definitions/reflection-wave) is the bouncing back of a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave), such as light or sound, from a boundary.
  In geometry, a [reflection](/dictionary/definitions/reflection-geometry) is a transformation that flips space across a line or a plane, keeping each point of the line or the plane in place.

## Notes

- The two meanings are linked by the law of reflection: the direction of a ray that is reflected by a flat boundary is the geometric reflection of the direction of the incoming ray across the plane of the boundary.
- The [image method](/dictionary/definitions/reflection-wave) uses the geometric reflection of a source across a flat boundary to describe the [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) that the boundary reflects.
