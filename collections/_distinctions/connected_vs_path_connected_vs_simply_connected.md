title: Connected vs. path-connected vs. simply connected
kind: hierarchy
areas: [topology, analysis]
summary: >
  [Topology](/definitions/topology) has several ways to say that a space is "in one piece," and some also rule out holes.
  A [connected](/definitions/connected) space is in one piece.
  A [path-connected](/definitions/path-connected) space is in one piece that you can travel across along a path.
  A [simply connected](/definitions/simply-connected) space is path-connected and has no holes that a loop can wrap around.
relation: >
  simply connected ⊂ path-connected ⊂ connected
