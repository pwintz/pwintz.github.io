  Diffusion, scattering, diffraction, and dispersion are all kinds of spreading, and the words sound alike, but each spreads something different for a different reason.
  [Diffusion](/dictionary/definitions/diffusion) is the spreading of a substance or of heat from high concentration to low concentration, caused by the random motion of molecules.
  [Scattering](/dictionary/definitions/scattering) is the redirection of parts of a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) in many directions by many small obstacles or irregularities.
  [Diffraction](/dictionary/definitions/diffraction) is the spreading of a wave around the edges of an obstacle or an opening.
  [Dispersion](/dictionary/definitions/dispersion-wave) is the separation of a wave into its frequencies, because the different frequencies travel at different speeds causing them to bend or [_refract_](/dictionary/definitions/refraction) at different angles when moving between different materials.

## Notes

- [Diffusion](/dictionary/definitions/diffusion) carries matter or heat.
  [Scattering](/dictionary/definitions/scattering), [diffraction](/dictionary/definitions/diffraction), and [dispersion](/dictionary/definitions/dispersion-wave) change where the [energy](/dictionary/definitions/energy) of a wave goes, without carrying the medium along.
- Diffusion is described by the diffusion equation $\partial u / \partial t = D \nabla^2 u$, which smooths out differences and does not oscillate.
  Scattering, diffraction, and dispersion are described by wave equations, whose solutions oscillate and travel at a finite speed.
- Scattering and diffraction spread a wave in direction, and dispersion spreads a wave by [frequency](/dictionary/definitions/frequency-oscillation), in direction (as in a prism) or in time (as a pulse stretches out).
- Scattering needs many small obstacles, diffraction needs an edge or an opening about the size of the [wavelength](/dictionary/definitions/wavelength), and dispersion needs a medium in which the [wave speed](/dictionary/definitions/wave-speed) depends on the frequency.
- In statistics, "dispersion" means how spread out data are, which is unrelated to [waves](/dictionary/definitions/wave).
