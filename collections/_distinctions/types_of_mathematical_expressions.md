## Factor vs. expand vs. distribute vs. FOIL

- To _factor_ is to rewrite a sum as a product: $x^2 + 3x + 2 = (x + 1)(x + 2)$.
- To _expand_ is the reverse: to rewrite a product as a sum.
- The _distributive law_ $a(b + c) = ab + ac$ justifies both.
- _FOIL_ (first, outer, inner, last) is a mnemonic for expanding a product of two binomials.
  It does not apply to longer sums, which is a reason to learn the distributive law instead.

## Subscripts and superscripts

A superscript is often an exponent ($x^2$), but it can also be an index ($x^{(2)}$ for the second iterate), a derivative ($f^{(2)}$), or a label.
Use parentheses or a different notation when the meaning could be ambiguous.
