published: false
title: Reflection Coefficient
term: reflection coefficient
applies_to: boundaries between media
synonyms: ['$R$', '$\Gamma$']
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a [plane wave](/dictionary/definitions/plane-wave) that reaches a flat boundary between two media at normal incidence.
      The _reflection coefficient_ of the boundary is the ratio of the [amplitude](/dictionary/definitions/amplitude) $A_r$ of the [reflected](/dictionary/definitions/reflection-wave) [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) to the amplitude $A_i$ of the incident wave, at the boundary:

      $$R = \frac{A_r}{A_i}.$$
properties:
  - >-
    For the pressure of a sound [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave), the reflection coefficient is

    $$R = \frac{Z_2 - Z_1}{Z_2 + Z_1},$$

    where $Z_1$ and $Z_2$ are the [characteristic acoustic impedances](/dictionary/definitions/characteristic-acoustic-impedance) of the first medium and the second medium.
  - >-
    The reflection coefficient is between $-1$ and $1$ when the [impedances](/dictionary/definitions/impedance) are real and positive.
    A negative reflection coefficient means that the reflected [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) is flipped upside down.
  - >-
    The fraction of the incident [energy](/dictionary/definitions/energy) that is reflected is $R^2$.
  - >-
    The reflection coefficient and the [transmission coefficient](/dictionary/definitions/transmission-coefficient) $T$ of the pressure satisfy $T = 1 + R$.
---
The sign of the reflection coefficient depends on which quantity is measured.
For a sound [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave), the reflection coefficient of the particle velocity is $-R$, the negative of the reflection coefficient of the pressure.
