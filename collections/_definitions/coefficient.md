title: Coefficient
term: coefficient
applies_to: terms of a polynomial or linear combination
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a term in algebra, made of a number times a variable.
      The _coefficient_ of the term is the number that multiplies its variable.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a term of a polynomial, made of a constant factor times a variable part.
      The _coefficient_ of the term is the constant factor that multiplies its variable part.
---
