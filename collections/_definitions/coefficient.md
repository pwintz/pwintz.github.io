title: Coefficient
term: coefficient
applies_to: terms of a polynomial or linear combination
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _coefficient_ is a constant that is multiplied with a variable, like "5" in $5x$.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a term of a [polynomial](/dictionary/definitions/polynomial), made of a constant factor times a variable part.
      The _coefficient_ of the term is the constant factor that multiplies its variable part.
  undergraduate:
    context: vector spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a [linear combination](/dictionary/definitions/linear-combination)

      $$c_1 \vect{v}_1 + c_2 \vect{v}_2 + \cdots + c_n \vect{v}_n$$

      of vectors $\vect{v}_1, \dots, \vect{v}_n$ in a [vector space](/dictionary/definitions/vector-space) $V$ over a [field](/dictionary/definitions/field) $\mathbb{F}$, where $c_1, \dots, c_n \in \mathbb{F}$.
      The _coefficient_ of $\vect{v}_i$ in the linear combination is the scalar $c_i$.
