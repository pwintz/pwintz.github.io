title: Median
term: median
applies_to: data and distributions
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _median_ of a list of numbers is the middle value when the numbers are sorted. With an even number of values, it is the mean of the two middle values.
  undergraduate:
    context: probability
    text: |-
      A _median_ of a random variable $X$ is a number $m$ with $P(X \leq m) \geq \frac{1}{2}$ and $P(X \geq m) \geq \frac{1}{2}$.
