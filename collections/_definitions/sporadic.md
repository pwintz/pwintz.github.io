title: Sporadic
term: sporadic
applies_to: events
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of something that happens from time to time, like rain in the desert.
      It is _sporadic_ if it happens now and then, at irregular times.
  undergraduate:
    context: real-time systems
    text: |-
      Consider a stream of [events](/definitions/event) (or tasks) in a real-time system, arriving one after another.
      The events are _sporadic_ if the events can occur at any time, but consecutive occurrences are separated by at least a known minimum inter-arrival time.
