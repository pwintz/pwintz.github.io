title: Intersection of sets
term: intersection
applies_to: sets
synonyms: [$\cap$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _intersection_ of two sets is the set of things that are in both of them.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _intersection_ $A \cap B$ is the set of everything that is in both $A$ and $B$.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _intersection_ of $A$ and $B$ is $A \cap B = \lbrace x : x \in A \text{ and } x \in B \rbrace$. Sets $A$ and $B$ are _disjoint_ if $A \cap B = \emptyset$.
