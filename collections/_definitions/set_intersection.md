---
title: Intersection of sets
term: intersection
applies_to: sets
synonyms: [$\cap$]
levels:
  # elementary:
  #   text: |-
  #     Think of two sets, like the all the students who play soccer and all the students who play piano.
  #     The _intersection_ of the two sets is the set of things that are in both of them.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider two sets $A$ and $B$.
      The _intersection_ of $A$ and $B$, written $A \cap B$, is the set of every element that is in both $A$ and $B$.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider two sets $A$ and $B$.
      The _intersection_ of sets $A$ and $B$ is 
      
      $$A \cap B = \setdef{x \suchthat x \in A \text{ and } x \in B}.$$
      
---

Sets $A$ and $B$ are _disjoint_ if $A \cap B = \emptyset$.
