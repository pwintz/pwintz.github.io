---
title: Equivalent
term: equivalent
applies_to: any objects
synonyms: [$\sim$, $\equiv$]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider two objects and some way of comparing them that has been chosen.
      The objects are _equivalent_ if the objects are the same in that particular sense.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider an [equivalence relation](/dictionary/definitions/equivalence-relation) $\sim$ on a set: a relation that is reflexive ($a \sim a$), symmetric ($a \sim b$ implies $b \sim a$), and transitive ($a \sim b$ and $b \sim c$ imply $a \sim c$).
      Two objects $a$ and $b$ are _equivalent_ if $a \sim b$.
      
---

Two statements $P$ and $Q$ are _logically equivalent_ ($P \Leftrightarrow Q$) if each implies the other.

Whether two objects are equivalent depends on which [equivalence relation](/dictionary/definitions/equivalence-relation) is meant.
