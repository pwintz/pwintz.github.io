title: Equivalent
term: equivalent
applies_to: any objects
synonyms: [$\sim$, $\equiv$]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Two objects are _equivalent_ if they are the same in a particular sense that has been chosen.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Two objects are _equivalent_ if they are related by an equivalence [relation](/definitions/relation): a relation $\sim$ that is reflexive ($a \sim a$), symmetric ($a \sim b$ implies $b \sim a$), and transitive ($a \sim b$ and $b \sim c$ imply $a \sim c$). Whether two objects are equivalent depends on which equivalence relation is meant.
---
Two statements $P$ and $Q$ are _logically equivalent_ ($P \Leftrightarrow Q$) if each implies the other.
