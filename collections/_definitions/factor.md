title: Factor
term: factor
applies_to: products
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a whole number, like $12$, and the numbers that divide it.
      A _factor_ of the number is a number that divides it evenly.
      $3$ is a factor of $12$ because $12 = 3 \times 4$.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a product, made of parts that are multiplied together.
      A _factor_ of the product is one of those parts.
