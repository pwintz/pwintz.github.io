title: Factor
term: factor
applies_to: products
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _factor_ of a number is a number that divides it evenly. $3$ is a factor of $12$ because $12 = 3 \times 4$.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _factor_ of a product is one of the parts that are multiplied together.
