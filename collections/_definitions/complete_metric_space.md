title: Complete metric space
term: complete
applies_to: metric spaces
levels:
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a [metric space](/dictionary/definitions/metric-space) $X$.
      The space $X$ is _complete_ if every [Cauchy sequence](/dictionary/definitions/cauchy-sequence) in $X$ converges to a point of $X$.
properties:
  - >-
    $\reals$ is complete, but $\mathbb{Q}$ is not: $\reals$ fills in the gaps of $\mathbb{Q}$.
  - >-
    A complete [normed space](/dictionary/definitions/normed-space) is a [Banach space](/dictionary/definitions/banach-space), and a complete [inner product space](/dictionary/definitions/inner-product-space) is a [Hilbert space](/dictionary/definitions/hilbert-space).
