---
title: Perpendicular
term: perpendicular
synonyms: [⟂]
applies_to: lines, segments, and planes
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Two lines are _perpendicular_ if the lines cross and make square corners where they cross.
  high-school:
    context: Euclidean geometry
    text: |-
      Two lines in a plane or in space are _perpendicular_ if the lines intersect and form a [right angle](/dictionary/definitions/right-angle) at their intersection.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Shapes are _perpendicular_ at at a point $P$ if they intersect at $P$ at a [right angle](/dictionary/definitions/right-angle).
properties:
  - >-
    Two lines in the plane with slopes $m_1$ and $m_2$ are perpendicular exactly when $m_1 m_2 = -1$, or when one line is horizontal and the other is vertical.
  - >-
    Lines in space whose directions are at [right angles](/dictionary/definitions/right-angle) but that do not intersect are usually called _orthogonal_ rather than perpendicular.
---
