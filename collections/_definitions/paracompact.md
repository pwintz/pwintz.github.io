title: Paracompact
term: paracompact
applies_to: topological spaces
levels:
  graduate:
    context: topological spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a ([Hausdorff](/dictionary/definitions/hausdorff-space)) topological space.
      The space is _paracompact_ if every open cover has a locally finite open [refinement](/dictionary/definitions/refinement): a cover by smaller [open sets](/dictionary/definitions/open-set), such that each point has a [neighborhood](/dictionary/definitions/neighborhood) meeting only finitely many of them.
properties:
  - >-
    A [Hausdorff](/dictionary/definitions/hausdorff-space) space is paracompact if and only if every open cover has a subordinate partition of unity.
  - >-
    Every [metric space](/dictionary/definitions/metric-space) is paracompact (Stone's theorem), and so is every [compact](/dictionary/definitions/compact) [Hausdorff](/dictionary/definitions/hausdorff-space) space.
