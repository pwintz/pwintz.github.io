title: Paracompact
term: paracompact
applies_to: topological spaces
levels:
  graduate:
    context: topological spaces
    text: |-
      A ([Hausdorff](/definitions/hausdorff-space)) space is _paracompact_ if every open cover has a locally finite open refinement: a cover by smaller open sets, such that each point has a neighborhood meeting only finitely many of them.
properties:
  - >-
    A [Hausdorff](/definitions/hausdorff-space) space is paracompact if and only if every open cover has a subordinate partition of unity.
  - >-
    Every [metric space](/definitions/metric-space) is paracompact (Stone's theorem), and so is every [compact](/definitions/compact) [Hausdorff](/definitions/hausdorff-space) space.
