published: false
title: Momentum
term: momentum
applies_to: moving objects
synonyms: [linear momentum, '$p$']
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider an object of mass $m$ moving with velocity $v$.
      The _momentum_ of the object is the product of its mass and its velocity:

      $$p = m v.$$

      Momentum is a [vector](/dictionary/definitions/vector), with the direction of the velocity, and is measured in kilogram meters per second (kg·m/s).
properties:
  - >-
    Force is the rate of change of momentum: $F = \frac{\mathrm{d}p}{\mathrm{d}t}$.
    A change of momentum is called an _impulse_.
  - >-
    The total momentum of an isolated system is constant, so momentum is conserved in every collision, elastic or inelastic.
  - >-
    Momentum is the conserved quantity that comes from symmetry under shifts in space (Noether's theorem).
---
The _angular momentum_ $r \times p$ of an object about a point is a different quantity.
In [Lagrangian](/dictionary/definitions/lagrangian) mechanics, the [conjugate momentum](/dictionary/definitions/conjugate-momentum) $\partial L / \partial \dot{q}$ generalizes momentum to other coordinates, such as angles.
