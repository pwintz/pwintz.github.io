title: Minkowski sum
term: Minkowski sum
applies_to: subsets of vector spaces
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _Minkowski sum_ of two sets of points is the set of all sums $a + b$, with $a$ from the first set and $b$ from the second.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      For [subsets](/definitions/subset) $A$ and $B$ of a [vector](/definitions/vector) space, the _Minkowski sum_ is $A + B = \lbrace a + b : a \in A, b \in B \rbrace$.
