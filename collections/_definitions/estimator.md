---
title: Estimator
term: estimator
applies_to: estimation problems
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider an unknown quantity and some data related to it.
      An _estimator_ is a rule for guessing the unknown quantity from the data.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider an unknown quantity, such as a state or a parameter, and the data available about it.
      An _estimator_ is any rule for computing an estimate of the quantity from the data.
properties:
  - >-
    [Observers](/definitions/observer), filters, and [predictors](/definitions/predictor) are all estimators of a state.
---
