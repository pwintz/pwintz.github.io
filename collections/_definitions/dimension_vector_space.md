title: Dimension (of a vector space)
term: dimension (of a vector space)
applies_to: vector spaces
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The dimension of an object is the number of directions you can move within that 
      Think of the directions you can move in a space: along a line, you can only go back and forth.
      The _dimension_ of a space is the number of independent directions you can move in it.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a space, such as a line, a plane, or the space around us, and coordinates that describe each point in it.
      The _dimension_ of the space is the number of coordinates needed to describe a point.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a [vector](/definitions/vector) space and a [basis](/definitions/vector-basis) of it.
      The _dimension_ of the vector space is the number of vectors in the basis.
---
Other notions of dimension include the dimension of a manifold, the Hausdorff dimension of a fractal, and the dimension of a physical quantity.
