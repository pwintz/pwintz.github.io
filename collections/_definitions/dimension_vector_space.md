title: Dimension (of a vector space)
term: dimension (of a vector space)
applies_to: vector spaces
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _dimension_ of a space is the number of independent directions you can move in it.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _dimension_ of a space is the number of coordinates needed to describe a point.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _dimension_ of a [vector](/definitions/vector) space is the number of vectors in a basis of it.
---
Other notions of dimension include the dimension of a manifold, the Hausdorff dimension of a fractal, and the dimension of a physical quantity.
