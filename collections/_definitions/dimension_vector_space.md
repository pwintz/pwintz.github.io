---
title: Dimension (of a vector space)
term: dimension (of a vector space)
applies_to: vector spaces
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _dimension_ of an object is the number of perpendicular directions you can move within that space.
      Think of the directions you can move in a space: along a line, you can only go back and forth but not side to side or up and down, so a line has one dimension.

    # The _dimension_ of a space is the number of independent directions you can move in it.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a space, such as a line, a plane, or the space around us, and coordinates that describe each point in it.
      The _dimension_ of the space is the number of coordinates needed to describe a point.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _dimension_ of the [vector](/dictionary/definitions/vector) space $V$ is the minimum number of vectors needed to span $V$, which is also the number of vectors in every [basis](/dictionary/definitions/vector-basis) of $V$.
---
Other notions of dimension include the dimension of a manifold, the [Hausdorff dimension](/dictionary/definitions/hausdorff-dimension) of a fractal, and the dimension of a physical quantity.
