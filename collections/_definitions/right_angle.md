---
title: Right angle
term: right angle
applies_to: angles
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _right angle_ is an angle that is a quarter of a full turn, like the corner of a square or of a sheet of paper.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _right angle_ is an angle that measures

      $$90^\circ = \frac{\pi}{2} \text{ radians}.$$
properties:
  - >-
    A right angle is formed by two rays or segments that share an endpoint; the term describes the angle, not the lines.
---
