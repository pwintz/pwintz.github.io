title: Left limit
term: left limit
applies_to: functions of a real variable
synonyms: [left-hand limit, limit from the left, '$\lim_{x \to a^{-}} f(x)$']
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a function $f$ and a number $a$, and let $x$ approach $a$ from below (from the left).
      The _left limit_ of $f$ at $a$, written $\lim_{x \to a^{-}} f(x)$, is the number that $f(x)$ approaches.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a function $f$ defined on an interval to the left of $a$.
      We write $\lim_{x \to a^{-}} f(x) = L$, and call $L$ the _left limit_ of $f$ at $a$, if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a $\delta > 0$ such that

      $$\abs{f(x) - L} < \varepsilon \quad \text{whenever } a - \delta < x < a.$$
