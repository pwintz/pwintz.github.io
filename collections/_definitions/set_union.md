---
title: Union of sets
term: union
applies_to: sets
synonyms: [$\cup$]
levels:
  # elementary:
  #   text: |-
  #     Think of two sets, like the toys in two boxes.
  #     The _union_ of the two sets is one set that contains everything from either set.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider two sets $A$ and $B$.
      The _union_ of $A$ and $B$, written $A \cup B$, is the set of every element that is in $A$, in $B$, or in both.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider two sets $A$ and $B$.
      The _union_ of sets $A$ and $B$ is 
      
      $$A \cup B = \lbrace x : x \in A \text{ or } x \in B \rbrace.$$

      More generally, the union of a family of sets $$(A_i)_{i \in I}$$ is the set of all $x$ with $x \in A_i$ for some $i \in I$.
