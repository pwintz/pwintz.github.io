title: Union of sets
term: union
applies_to: sets
synonyms: [$\cup$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _union_ of two sets is one set that contains everything from either set.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _union_ $A \cup B$ is the set of everything that is in $A$, in $B$, or in both.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _union_ of $A$ and $B$ is $A \cup B = \lbrace x : x \in A \text{ or } x \in B \rbrace$. More generally, the union of a family of sets $$(A_i)_{i \in I}$$ is the set of all $x$ with $x \in A_i$ for some $i \in I$.
