title: Neumann boundary condition
term: Neumann
applies_to: boundary value problems
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a differential equation on a region, such as the heat equation, whose solution must also satisfy a condition on the [boundary](/definitions/boundary) of the region.
      A _Neumann_ boundary condition specifies the flux through the boundary.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a partial differential equation for $u$ on a region $\Omega$ with [boundary](/definitions/boundary) $\partial \Omega$, where $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}$ is the derivative in the direction of the outward normal.
      A _Neumann_ boundary condition has the form $\frac{\partial u}{\partial n} = g$ on $\partial \Omega$.
