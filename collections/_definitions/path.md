    text: |-
      A _path_ is a way of moving continuously from one point to another, described by the position $\gamma(s)$ at each value of a parameter $s$ that goes from $0$ to $1$.
  undergraduate:
    context: topology
    text: |-
      Consider a topological space $X$.
      A _path_ in $X$ is a [continuous](/dictionary/definitions/continuous) function

      $$\gamma : [0, 1] \to X.$$

      The points $\gamma(0)$ and $\gamma(1)$ are the start and the end of the path.
properties:
  - >-
    Every path is a [curve](/dictionary/definitions/curve), parametrized by the interval $[0, 1]$.
  - >-
    A space is [path connected](/dictionary/definitions/path-connected) if every two points of the space are joined by a path.

In graph theory, a _path_ is a sequence of edges that joins a sequence of distinct vertices.

In physics and everyday speech, the "path" of a moving object usually means the set of points that the object passes through, which is the [orbit](/dictionary/definitions/orbit) in the language of dynamical systems.
