published: false
title: Outcome (probability)
term: outcome
applies_to: random experiments
synonyms: [elementary outcome, sample point]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of something whose result is up to chance, like rolling a die.
      An _outcome_ is one possible result, such as rolling a $4$.
  undergraduate:
    context: probability
    text: |-
      Consider a random experiment and its sample space $\Omega$, the set of all of its possible results.
      An _outcome_ of the experiment is an element $\omega$ of $\Omega$.
properties:
  - >-
    Exactly one outcome occurs each time the experiment is performed.
