title: Right limit
term: right limit
applies_to: functions of a real variable
synonyms: [right-hand limit, limit from the right, '$\lim_{x \to a^{+}} f(x)$']
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a function $f$ and a number $a$, and let $x$ approach $a$ from above (from the right).
      The _right limit_ of $f$ at $a$, written $\lim_{x \to a^{+}} f(x)$, is the number that $f(x)$ approaches.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a function $f$ defined on an interval to the right of $a$.
      We write $\lim_{x \to a^{+}} f(x) = L$, and call $L$ the _right limit_ of $f$ at $a$, if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a $\delta > 0$ such that $\lvert f(x) - L \rvert < \varepsilon$ whenever $a < x < a + \delta$.
