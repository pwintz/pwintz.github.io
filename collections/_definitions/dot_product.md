title: Dot product
term: dot product
applies_to: vectors in $\reals^n$
synonyms: [scalar product, $x \cdot y$]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _dot product_ of [vectors](/definitions/vector) in $\reals^n$ is $x \cdot y = x_1 y_1 + x_2 y_2 + \dots + x_n y_n$.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _dot product_ is the standard [inner product](/definitions/inner-product) on $\reals^n$, $x \cdot y = x^{\top} y$.
properties:
  - >-
    $x \cdot y = \lVert x \rVert \lVert y \rVert \cos \theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between $x$ and $y$. In particular, $x \cdot y = 0$ exactly when $x$ and $y$ are perpendicular (or one of them is zero).
