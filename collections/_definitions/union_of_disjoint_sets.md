---
title: Union of disjoint sets
term: disjoint union (of disjoint sets)
applies_to: sets
synonyms: [$A \sqcup B$ (in some texts)]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider two sets $A$ and $B$ that have no elements in common.
      The [union](/dictionary/definitions/set-union) $A \cup B$ is a _union of disjoint sets_.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider sets $A$ and $B$ such that $A \cap B = \emptyset$.
      A _union of disjoint sets_ is the ordinary [union](/dictionary/definitions/set-union) $A \cup B$ in this case.
      Writing $A \sqcup B$ (or $A \mathbin{\dot{\cup}} B$) for it asserts that $A$ and $B$ are disjoint.
---
