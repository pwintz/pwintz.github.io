title: Union of disjoint sets
term: disjoint union (of disjoint sets)
applies_to: sets
synonyms: [$A \sqcup B$ (in some texts)]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _union of disjoint sets_ is the [union](/definitions/set-union) of sets that have no elements in common.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _union of disjoint sets_ is the ordinary [union](/definitions/set-union) $A \cup B$ in the case where $A \cap B = \emptyset$. Writing $A \sqcup B$ (or $A \mathbin{\dot{\cup}} B$) for it asserts that $A$ and $B$ are disjoint.
