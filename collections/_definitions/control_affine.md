title: Control-affine system
term: control-affine
applies_to: control systems
synonyms: [input-affine, affine in the control]
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A system is _control-affine_ if it depends affinely on the input, but possibly nonlinearly on the state: $\dot{x} = f(x) + g(x) u$.
