title: Arithmetic mean
term: mean
applies_to: data
synonyms: [arithmetic mean, average]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _mean_ of a list of numbers is the sum of all the numbers divided by how many there are. 
      The mean of $2, 4, 9$ is $15 / 3 = 5$.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _mean_ of data $x_1, \dots, x_n$ is

      $$\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n x_i.$$
---
There are other means, such as the geometric mean $\sqrt[n]{x_1 x_2 \cdots x_n}$ and the harmonic mean. "Mean" alone usually means the arithmetic mean.
