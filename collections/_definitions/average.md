---
title: Average
term: average
applies_to: data
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a list of numbers, like the heights of the students in a class.
      An _average_ of the list is a single number that represents a typical value of it.
      Usually, "average" means the [arithmetic mean](/dictionary/definitions/arithmetic-mean).
---
In everyday use, "average" sometimes refers to the [median](/dictionary/definitions/median) or mode ("the average household").
In technical writing, say "mean" or "median" instead.
