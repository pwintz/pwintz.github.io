title: Dirichlet boundary condition
term: Dirichlet
applies_to: boundary value problems
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a differential equation on a region, whose solution must also satisfy a condition on the [boundary](/dictionary/definitions/boundary) of the region.
      A _Dirichlet_ boundary condition specifies the value of the solution on the boundary.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a partial differential equation for $u$ on a region $\Omega$ with [boundary](/dictionary/definitions/boundary) $\partial \Omega$.
      A _Dirichlet_ boundary condition has the form $u = g$ on $\partial \Omega$.
