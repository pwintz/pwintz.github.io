title: Cumulative distribution function
term: cumulative distribution function
applies_to: random variables
synonyms: [CDF, distribution function]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [random variable](/definitions/random-variable) $X$ with real values.
      The _cumulative distribution function_ of $X$ gives, for each number $x$, the [probability](/definitions/probability) that $X$ is at most $x$:

      $$F(x) = P(X \leq x).$$
properties:
  - >-
    Every cumulative distribution function is nondecreasing and right-continuous, with $F(x) \to 0$ as $x \to -\infty$ and $F(x) \to 1$ as $x \to \infty$.
  - >-
    Every [random variable](/definitions/random-variable) has a cumulative distribution function, even when it has neither a [probability mass function](/definitions/probability-mass-function) nor a [probability density](/definitions/probability-density).
