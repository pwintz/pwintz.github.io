title: Open ball
term: open ball
applies_to: sets of points
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An _open ball_ is a [ball](/definitions/ball) without its surface: all of the points at distance strictly less than $r$ from a center $c$.
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      The _open ball_ of radius $r$ around $c$ is $B(c, r) = \lbrace x : d(x, c) < r \rbrace$, the points at distance strictly less than $r$ from $c$. It does not include the [sphere](/definitions/sphere) $S(c, r)$.
