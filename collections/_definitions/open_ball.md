---
title: Open ball
term: open ball
applies_to: sets of points
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a center $c$ and a radius $r$.
      The _open ball_ with center $c$ and radius $r$ is a [ball](/dictionary/definitions/ball) without its surface: all of the points at distance strictly less than $r$ from $c$.
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a [metric space](/dictionary/definitions/metric-space) with [metric](/dictionary/definitions/metric) $d$, a center $c$, and a radius $r$.
      The _open ball_ of radius $r$ around $c$ is the set of points at distance strictly less than $r$ from $c$:

      $$B(c, r) = \setdef{x \suchthat d(x, c) < r}.$$

      It does not include the [sphere](/dictionary/definitions/sphere) $S(c, r)$.
