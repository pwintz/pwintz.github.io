title: Recurrent
term: recurrent
applies_to: points of a dynamical system
levels:
  graduate:
    context: dynamical systems
    text: |-
      A point $x$ is _recurrent_ if its trajectory returns arbitrarily close to $x$ infinitely often: for every neighborhood $U$ of $x$ and every $T$, there is $t > T$ with $\phi_t(x) \in U$.
properties:
  - >-
    Every point on a [periodic](/definitions/periodic) orbit is recurrent, but recurrent points need not be periodic.
  - >-
    The Poincaré recurrence theorem says that, for volume-preserving dynamics on a [bounded](/definitions/bounded) region, almost every point is recurrent.
