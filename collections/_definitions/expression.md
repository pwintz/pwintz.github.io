title: Expression
term: expression
applies_to: mathematical notation
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      An _expression_ is a combination of numbers, variables, and operations, with no equals sign or [inequality](/dictionary/definitions/inequality) sign.
  undergraduate:
    context: logic
    text: |-
      Consider a formal language of mathematical symbols.
      An _expression_ is a well-formed string of symbols, that is, a string built by the rules of the language.
      Expressions come in two kinds:

      1. a [term](/dictionary/definitions/term-logic), such as $x^2 + 1$, names an object and has a value, and
      2. a [formula](/dictionary/definitions/formula-logic), such as $x^2 \geq 0$, makes a claim and has a truth value.
---
The levels disagree on purpose: in school algebra, an expression has no equals or inequality sign, so $x^2 \geq 0$ is an inequality, not an expression; in logic, $x^2 \geq 0$ is an expression, namely a formula.
