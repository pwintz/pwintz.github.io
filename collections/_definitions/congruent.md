---
title: Congruent
term: congruent
applies_to: geometric figures and integers
synonyms: [$\cong$, "$\\equiv \\pmod{n}$"]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of two shapes cut out of paper.
      The shapes are _congruent_ if both shapes have the same form and the same size, so that one could be placed exactly on top of the other (possibly after flipping it over).
  high-school:
    context: geometry
    text: |-
      Consider two figures in the plane, and the rigid motions that move one of them: rotations, reflections, and translations.
      The figures are _congruent_ if such motions can move one exactly onto the other: the figures have the same shape and the same size.
---
In number theory, "congruent" has a different meaning: $a \equiv b \pmod{n}$ means $n$ divides $a - b$.
Both meanings are [equivalence](/dictionary/definitions/equivalence-relation) [relations](/dictionary/definitions/relation).
