title: Congruent
term: congruent
applies_to: geometric figures and integers
synonyms: [$\cong$, "$\\equiv \\pmod{n}$"]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Two shapes are _congruent_ if they have the same shape and the same size, so that one could be placed exactly on top of the other (possibly after flipping it over).
  high-school:
    context: geometry
    text: |-
      Two figures are _congruent_ if one can be moved onto the other exactly by rotations, reflections, and translations: same shape and same size.
---
In number theory, "congruent" has a different meaning: $a \equiv b \pmod{n}$ means $n$ divides $a - b$.
Both meanings are equivalence [relations](/definitions/relation).
