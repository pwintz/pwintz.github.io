title: Lyapunov stable equilibrium
term: stable
applies_to: equilibria
synonyms: [Lyapunov stable, stable in the sense of Lyapunov]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A resting state is _stable_ if a small push moves the system only a little away from it.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) is _stable_ if solutions that start close to it stay close to it.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      An [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) $x^{\ast}$ of $\dot{x} = f(x)$ is _stable_ if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $\delta > 0$ such that $\lVert x(0) - x^{\ast} \rVert < \delta$ implies $\lVert x(t) - x^{\ast} \rVert < \varepsilon$ for all $t \geq 0$.
---
Stability does not require solutions to converge to the [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium).
