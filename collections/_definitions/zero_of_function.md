title: Zero of a Function
term: zero (of a function)
applies_to: functions and equations
synonyms: [root of a function]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [function](/dictionary/definitions/function) $f$.
      A _zero_ of $f$ is an input $x$ in the [domain](/dictionary/definitions/domain) of $f$ such that

      $$f(x) = 0.$$
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a function $f : X \to Y$, where $Y$ contains a zero element, such as $\reals$, $\complexes$, or a [vector space](/dictionary/definitions/vector-space).
      A _zero_ of $f$ is a point $x \in X$ such that

      $$f(x) = 0.$$
properties:
  - |-
    The zeros of $f$ are the solutions of the equation $f(x) = 0$.
---
Strictly, an _equation_ such as $x^2 - 4 = 0$ has roots (solutions), while a _function_ such as $x \mapsto x^2 - 4$ has zeros.
In practice, both words are used for both.
"Root" is the usual word for [polynomials](/dictionary/definitions/polynomial), and "zero" is the usual word for other functions, such as the zeros of $\sin$ or the zeros of a [transfer function](/dictionary/definitions/transfer-function).
