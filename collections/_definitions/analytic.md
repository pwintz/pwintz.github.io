title: Analytic function
term: analytic
applies_to: functions
synonyms: [real analytic, "$C^{\\omega}$"]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A function is _analytic_ if, near every point, it equals a power series (a "polynomial with infinitely many terms").
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A function is _analytic_ if, near each point $a$ of its domain, it equals its Taylor series:

      $$f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!} (x - a)^n$$

      for all $x$ close to $a$.
