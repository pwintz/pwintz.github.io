title: Circle
term: circle
applies_to: sets of points
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a center point on a page and a fixed distance from it.
      A _circle_ is the round curve made of all the points at that distance from the center.
      A circle is only the curve, not the region inside it.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a center $c$ in the plane and a radius $r > 0$.
      The _circle_ with center $c$ and radius $r$ is the set of points in the plane at distance $r$ from $c$.
      It is a [sphere](/definitions/sphere) in $\reals^2$ (the $1$-sphere $S^1$).
---
The region inside a circle is a _disk_.
"Find the area of the circle" really means the area of the disk.
