title: Circle
term: circle
applies_to: sets of points
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _circle_ is a round curve, where every point is the same distance from the center. A circle is only the curve, not the region inside it.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _circle_ is the set of points in the plane at a fixed distance $r$ from a center $c$. It is a [sphere](/definitions/sphere) in $\reals^2$ (the $1$-sphere $S^1$).
---
The region inside a circle is a _disk_. "Find the area of the circle" really means the area of the disk.
