title: Unit of measurement
term: unit
applies_to: physical quantities
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of measuring the length of a table, in inches or in centimeters.
      A _unit_ is a standard amount used for measuring.
  high-school:
    context: physics
    text: |-
      Consider a physical quantity of a given dimension, such as a length.
      A _unit_ is a fixed quantity of that dimension, used as a reference.
      A measurement is a number times a unit.
