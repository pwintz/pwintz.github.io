published: false
title: Leading entry
term: leading entry
applies_to: rows of a matrix
synonyms: [leading coefficient]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a nonzero row of a [matrix](/definitions/matrix), read from left to right.
      The _leading entry_ of the row is its first nonzero entry.
properties:
  - |-
    A matrix is in echelon form when every zero row is at the bottom and each leading entry is in a column to the right of the leading entry in the row above it.
---
