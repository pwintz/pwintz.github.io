---
title: Natural numbers (starting at 1)
term: natural numbers (from 1)
applies_to: numbers
synonyms: [positive integers, counting numbers, "$\\nats$", "$\\nats_+$", "$\\mathbb{Z}_{> 0}$"]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _natural numbers_ are the counting numbers $1, 2, 3, 4, \dots$
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _natural numbers_ are the set $\setdef{1, 2, 3, \dots}$ of positive [integers](/definitions/integer), often written $\nats$, $$\mathbb{Z}_{> 0}$$, or $$\nats_{+}$$.
---
This convention is common in number theory and analysis.
