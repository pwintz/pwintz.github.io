title: Total Internal Reflection
term: total internal reflection
applies_to: waves
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) traveling in one material with [wave speed](/dictionary/definitions/wave-speed) $c_1$ that reaches a flat boundary with a second material with a larger wave speed $c_2$, such as light in water reaching the surface of the water.
      _Total internal reflection_ is the [reflection](/dictionary/definitions/reflection-wave) of all of the wave back into the first material, which occurs when the [angle](/dictionary/definitions/angle) of incidence $\theta$, measured from the [normal vector](/dictionary/definitions/normal-vector) of the boundary, is greater than the _critical angle_ $\theta_c$ such that

      $$\sin \theta_c = \frac{c_1}{c_2}.$$
properties:
  - >-
    Total internal reflection follows from Snell's law $\sin \theta_2 = (c_2 / c_1) \sin \theta_1$: when $\theta_1 > \theta_c$, no [angle](/dictionary/definitions/angle) $\theta_2$ satisfies Snell's law, so no [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) is [transmitted](/dictionary/definitions/transmission-wave).
  - >-
    Total internal reflection is impossible when the [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) enters a material with a smaller [wave speed](/dictionary/definitions/wave-speed).
  - >-
    During total internal reflection, the [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) reaches slightly into the second material as an _evanescent wave_, which decays exponentially with the distance from the boundary and carries no [energy](/dictionary/definitions/energy) away from the boundary.
  - >-
    Optical fibers carry light by total internal reflection at the boundary between the glass core and the cladding around the core.
  - >-
    The critical [angle](/dictionary/definitions/angle) for light going from water into air is about $49^{\circ}$, and the critical angle for sound going from air into water is about $13^{\circ}$, because sound travels faster in water than in air.
