title: Maximal solution (hybrid systems)
term: maximal (solution)
applies_to: solutions of hybrid systems
levels:
  graduate:
    text: |-
      A solution of a [hybrid system](/definitions/hybrid-system) is _maximal_ if it cannot be extended: no other solution agrees with it on its hybrid time domain and has a strictly larger domain.
properties:
  - >-
    A maximal solution can fail to be complete by escaping to infinity in finite time, or by reaching a point from which it can neither flow nor jump.
