  undergraduate:
    context: continuous-time linear systems
    text: |-
      The _Routh–Hurwitz_ criterion is a test, using a table built from the [coefficients](/definitions/coefficient) of a polynomial, for whether all of its roots have negative real parts. Applied to the characteristic polynomial, it decides stability of a continuous-time linear system without computing the roots.
