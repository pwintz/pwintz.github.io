title: Routh–Hurwitz criterion
term: Routh–Hurwitz
applies_to: polynomials
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a linear system, which is stable when all roots of a certain polynomial have negative real parts.
      The _Routh–Hurwitz_ criterion is a test that uses only the [coefficients](/definitions/coefficient) of the polynomial to tell whether all of its roots have negative real parts.
  undergraduate:
    context: continuous-time linear systems
    text: |-
      Consider a continuous-time linear system and its characteristic polynomial.
      The _Routh–Hurwitz_ criterion is a test, using a table built from the [coefficients](/definitions/coefficient) of the polynomial, for whether all of its roots have negative real parts.
      It decides stability of the system without computing the roots.
