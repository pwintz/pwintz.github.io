title: Regulation
term: regulation
applies_to: control problems
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      _Regulation_ is keeping a quantity at a target value.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      _Regulation_ is keeping a quantity at a constant desired value despite [disturbances](/definitions/disturbance), as a thermostat regulates temperature.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      _Regulation_ is the control problem of driving the output of a system to a constant [set point](/definitions/set-point) (often zero) and keeping it there, despite [disturbances](/definitions/disturbance).
---
In output regulation theory (Francis and Wonham), "regulation" is broader: it includes [tracking](/definitions/tracking) references and rejecting [disturbances](/definitions/disturbance) that are generated by a known exosystem.
