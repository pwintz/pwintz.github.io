title: Regulation
term: regulation
applies_to: control problems
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a quantity that you want to keep steady, like the temperature of a room.
      _Regulation_ is keeping the quantity at a target value.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a quantity that [disturbances](/definitions/disturbance) push away from its desired value, like the temperature of a room.
      _Regulation_ is keeping the quantity at a constant desired value despite the disturbances, as a thermostat regulates temperature.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a control system with an output and a constant [set point](/definitions/set-point), often zero.
      _Regulation_ is the control problem of driving the output to the set point and keeping it there, despite [disturbances](/definitions/disturbance).
---
In output regulation theory (Francis and Wonham), "regulation" is broader: it includes [tracking](/definitions/tracking) references and rejecting [disturbances](/definitions/disturbance) that are generated by a known exosystem.
