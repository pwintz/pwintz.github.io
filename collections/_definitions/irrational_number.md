---
title: Irrational number
term: irrational number
applies_to: numbers
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      An _irrational number_ is a number that cannot be written as a [fraction](/definitions/fraction) of [whole numbers](/definitions/whole-numbers) (or negative whole numbers).
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An _irrational number_ is a real number that cannot be written as a [fraction](/definitions/fraction) of [integers](/definitions/integer).
properties:
  - >-
    The decimal expansion of an irrational number neither ends nor repeats.
  - >-
    The irrational numbers are [uncountable](/definitions/uncountable), while the [rational numbers](/definitions/rational-number) are countable.
---
