---
title: Irrational number
term: irrational number
applies_to: numbers
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      An _irrational number_ is a number that cannot be written as a [fraction](/dictionary/definitions/fraction) of [whole numbers](/dictionary/definitions/whole-numbers) (or negative whole numbers).
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An _irrational number_ is a [real number](/dictionary/definitions/real-number) that cannot be written as a [fraction](/dictionary/definitions/fraction) of [integers](/dictionary/definitions/integer).
properties:
  - >-
    The decimal expansion of an irrational number neither ends nor repeats.
  - >-
    The irrational numbers are [uncountable](/dictionary/definitions/uncountable), while the [rational numbers](/dictionary/definitions/rational-number) are [countable](/dictionary/definitions/countable).
---
