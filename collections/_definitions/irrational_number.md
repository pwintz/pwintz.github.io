title: Irrational number
term: irrational number
applies_to: numbers
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      An _irrational number_ is a number that cannot be written as a [fraction](/definitions/fraction) of [whole numbers](/definitions/whole-numbers) (or the negative of one).
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An _irrational number_ is a [real number](/definitions/real-number) that is not rational.
properties:
  - >-
    The decimal expansion of an irrational number never ends and never repeats.
  - >-
    The irrational numbers are [uncountable](/definitions/uncountable), while the [rational numbers](/definitions/rational-number) are countable.
