published: false
title: Wave
term: wave
applies_to: media and fields
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _wave_ is a disturbance that travels from place to place, such as a ripple on a pond or a sound in the air, while the water or the air itself only moves back and forth.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a medium, such as air, water, or a stretched string.
      A _wave_ in the medium is a disturbance of the medium from rest that travels through the medium and carries [energy](/dictionary/definitions/energy) from place to place, while each part of the medium only moves back and forth about its resting position.
  undergraduate:
    context: linear waves in a uniform medium
    text: |-
      Consider a uniform medium in which a small disturbance $u(\vect{x}, t)$ from rest, such as the pressure of sound, satisfies the _wave equation_

      $$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \nabla^2 u,$$

      where $c > 0$ is the [wave speed](/dictionary/definitions/wave-speed) and $\nabla^2$ is the [Laplacian](/dictionary/definitions/laplacian).
      A _wave_ in the medium is a nonzero solution $u$ of the wave equation.
properties:
  - >-
    A wave carries [energy](/dictionary/definitions/energy) and [momentum](/dictionary/definitions/momentum) from place to place, but does not carry the medium along with the wave.
  - >-
    In a _transverse_ wave, such as a wave on a string, the medium moves perpendicular to the direction in which the wave travels.
    In a _longitudinal_ wave, such as sound in air, the medium moves back and forth along the direction in which the wave travels.
  - >-
    Light and other electromagnetic waves need no medium: the disturbance is in the electric and magnetic fields, which can travel through a vacuum.
  - >-
    (d'Alembert's formula.)
    In one dimension, every solution of the wave equation is the sum of a wave $f(x - c t)$ that travels to the right and a wave $g(x + c t)$ that travels to the left.
---
In more general settings, a _wave_ is a disturbance that travels at a finite speed, even when the disturbance does not satisfy the wave equation, such as a water wave in deep water or a shock wave.
