title: Closure of a set
term: closure
applies_to: sets
levels:
  high-school:
    context: subsets of $\reals^n$
    text: |-
      Consider a set $A$ of points on the number line, in the plane, or in space.
      The _closure_ of $A$ is $A$ together with all of its [boundary](/dictionary/definitions/boundary) points.
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a set $A$ in a [metric space](/dictionary/definitions/metric-space).
      The _closure_ $\overline{A}$ of $A$ is the set of limits of convergent [sequences](/dictionary/definitions/sequence) of points of $A$.
  graduate:
    context: topological spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a set $A$ in a topological space.
      The _closure_ $\overline{A}$ of $A$ is the [intersection](/dictionary/definitions/set-intersection) of all [closed sets](/dictionary/definitions/closed-set) that contain $A$.
properties:
  - >-
    The closure of $A$ is the smallest [closed set](/dictionary/definitions/closed-set) that contains $A$, and $A$ is closed if and only if $A = \overline{A}$.
  - >-
    The closure of $A$ is the [union](/dictionary/definitions/set-union) of the [interior](/dictionary/definitions/interior) and the boundary of $A$.
