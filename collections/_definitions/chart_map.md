title: Chart Map
term: chart map
applies_to: charts
synonyms: [coordinate map]
levels:
  graduate:
    context: differential geometry
    text: |-
      Consider a [chart](/dictionary/definitions/chart-manifold) $(U, \varphi)$ on a [manifold](/dictionary/definitions/manifold) $M$ of dimension $n$.
      The _chart map_ of $(U, \varphi)$ is the [homeomorphism](/dictionary/definitions/homeomorphism)

      $$\varphi : U \to \varphi(U) \subseteq \reals^n.$$
properties:
  - |-
    The entries of $\varphi(p)$ are the _coordinates_ of the point $p$ in the chart $(U, \varphi)$.
---
Many texts call the chart map itself a "chart" and leave its domain $U$ implicit.
