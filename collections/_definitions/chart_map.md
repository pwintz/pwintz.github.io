---
title: Chart Map
term: chart map
applies_to: manifolds
synonyms: [coordinate map]
levels:
  graduate:
    context: differential geometry
    text: |-
      Consider a [manifold](/dictionary/definitions/manifold) $M$ of [dimension](/dictionary/definitions/dimension-manifold) $n$ and an open [subset](/dictionary/definitions/subset) $U$ of $M$.
      A _chart map_ on $U$ is a [homeomorphism](/dictionary/definitions/homeomorphism) $\varphi$ from $U$ onto an open subset of $\reals^n$:

      $$\varphi : U \to \varphi(U) \subseteq \reals^n.$$
properties:
  - |-
    A chart map $\varphi$ on $U$ and its domain $U$ form a [chart](/dictionary/definitions/chart-manifold) $(U, \varphi)$ on $M$.
  - |-
    The entries of $\varphi(p)$ are the _coordinates_ of the point $p$ in the chart $(U, \varphi)$.
---
Many texts call the chart map itself a "chart" and leave its domain $U$ implicit.
