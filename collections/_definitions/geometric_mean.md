title: Geometric mean
term: geometric mean
applies_to: lists of positive numbers
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider positive numbers $x_1, \dots, x_n$, such as yearly growth factors.
      Their _geometric mean_ is the $n$th root of their product, $\sqrt[n]{x_1 x_2 \cdots x_n}$.
properties:
  - |-
    The geometric mean is at most the [arithmetic mean](/definitions/arithmetic-mean), with equality only when all of the numbers are equal (the AM–GM inequality).
  - |-
    The logarithm of the geometric mean is the arithmetic mean of the logarithms.
  - |-
    The geometric mean is the right average for growth factors and ratios: a quantity that grows by the factors $x_1, \dots, x_n$ ends where it would if it grew by their geometric mean each time.
