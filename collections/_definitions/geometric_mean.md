---
title: Geometric Mean
term: geometric mean
applies_to: lists of positive numbers
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider positive numbers $x_1, \dots, x_n$.
      The _geometric mean_ of $x_1, \dots, x_n$ is the $n$th root of the product $x_1 x_2 \cdots x_n$:

      $$\sqrt[n]{x_1 x_2 \cdots x_n}.$$
properties:
  - |-
    The geometric mean is at most the [arithmetic mean](/dictionary/definitions/arithmetic-mean), with equality only when all of the numbers are the same number (the AM–GM inequality).
  - |-
    The logarithm of the geometric mean is the [arithmetic mean](/dictionary/definitions/arithmetic-mean) of the logarithms.

  - |-
    The geometric mean is suitable for finding the average of growth factors and ratios: a quantity that grows by the factors $x_1, \dots, x_n$ ends where it would if it grew by their geometric mean each time.
---
