title: Homeomorphism
term: homeomorphism
applies_to: topological spaces
levels:
  undergraduate:
    context: topological spaces
    text: |-
      Consider topological spaces $X$ and $Y$.
      A _homeomorphism_ from $X$ to $Y$ is a [bijective](/definitions/bijective) function $f : X \to Y$ such that $f$ and $f^{-1}$ are both [continuous](/definitions/continuous).
properties:
  - >-
    Spaces related by a homeomorphism are called _homeomorphic_, and they have the same topological properties, such as [compactness](/definitions/compact) and [connectedness](/definitions/connected).
  - >-
    A [continuous](/definitions/continuous) bijection need not be a homeomorphism: $t \mapsto (\cos t, \sin t)$ from $[0, 2\pi)$ to the unit [circle](/definitions/circle) is a continuous bijection, but its inverse is not continuous at $(1, 0)$.
---
The popular saying that "a coffee cup is a donut" means that their surfaces are homeomorphic.
