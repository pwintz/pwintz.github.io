title: Difference
term: difference
applies_to: numbers and vectors
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _difference_ of two numbers is the result of subtracting one from the other: $7 - 3 = 4$, so the difference of $7$ and $3$ is $4$.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _difference_ of $a$ and $b$ is the result $a - b$ of subtraction. It depends on the order and can be negative: $3 - 7 = -4$.
