title: Admittance
term: admittance
applies_to: electric circuits
synonyms: ['$Y$']
levels:
  undergraduate:
    context: linear circuits
    text: |-
      Consider a linear electric circuit with two terminals and with nonzero [impedance](/dictionary/definitions/impedance) $Z(\omega)$ at angular [frequency](/dictionary/definitions/frequency-oscillation) $\omega$.
      The _admittance_ of the circuit at frequency $\omega$ is the reciprocal of the impedance:

      $$Y(\omega) = \frac{1}{Z(\omega)}.$$
properties:
  - |-
    Admittance is measured in siemens (S), where one siemens is one reciprocal ohm.
  - |-
    Admittances in parallel add, just as [impedances](/dictionary/definitions/impedance) in series add.
  - |-
    The real part of the admittance $Y = G + i B$ is the _conductance_ $G$, and the imaginary part is the _susceptance_ $B$.
