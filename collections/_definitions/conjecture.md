title: Conjecture
term: conjecture
applies_to: mathematical statements
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _conjecture_ is a mathematical statement that someone believes is true but that has not been proved.
properties:
  - >-
    A proved conjecture becomes a theorem, and a disproved conjecture becomes false, but a conjecture's name often stays, as with Fermat's Last Theorem (proved by Wiles) and the Poincaré conjecture (proved by Perelman).
