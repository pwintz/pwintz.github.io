title: Infinity
term: infinity
applies_to: limits and the extended real numbers
synonyms: [$\infty$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      _Infinity_ is the idea of something that goes on forever, bigger than any number. It is not itself a number.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      _Infinity_, written $\infty$, is a symbol for growing without bound. It is not a [real number](/definitions/real-number). "$x \to \infty$" means $x$ becomes larger than any given number.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      In the extended [real numbers](/definitions/real-number) $\reals \cup \lbrace -\infty, \infty \rbrace$, _infinity_ ($\infty$) is an element greater than every real number. Some arithmetic, such as $\infty - \infty$, is left undefined.
