title: Classical solution
term: classical solution
applies_to: differential equations
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _classical solution_ of a differential equation is a [differentiable](/definitions/differentiable) function that satisfies the equation at every time.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _classical solution_ of $\dot{x} = f(t, x)$ on an interval is a [continuously differentiable](/definitions/continuously-differentiable) function $x(t)$ with $\dot{x}(t) = f(t, x(t))$ for every $t$ in the interval.
properties:
  - >-
    If $f$ is [continuous](/definitions/continuous), classical solutions exist (Peano's theorem). If $f$ is also locally Lipschitz in $x$, they are unique.
