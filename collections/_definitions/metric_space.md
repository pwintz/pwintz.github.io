title: Metric space
term: metric space
applies_to: spaces
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _metric space_ is a set of points with a way of measuring the distance between any two of them.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _metric space_ is a set $X$ together with a [metric](/definitions/metric) $d$ on $X$. It has distances, but not necessarily addition, scaling, lengths of [vectors](/definitions/vector), or angles.
