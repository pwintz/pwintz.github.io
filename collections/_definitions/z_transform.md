title: Z-transform
term: Z-transform
applies_to: sequences
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _Z-transform_ turns a [sequence](/definitions/sequence) of numbers into a function, which turns linear difference equations (where each value depends on earlier values) into algebraic equations.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _Z-transform_ of a [sequence](/definitions/sequence) $x[0], x[1], x[2], \dots$ is $X(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x[n] z^{-n}$, for the complex numbers $z$ where the series converges. It is the discrete-time analog of the [Laplace transform](/definitions/laplace-transform).
