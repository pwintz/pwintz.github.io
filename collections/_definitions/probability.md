title: Probability
term: probability
applies_to: events
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _probability_ of something happening is a number from $0$ to $1$ that says how likely it is: $0$ means it cannot happen, and $1$ means it is certain.
  undergraduate:
    context: measure-theoretic probability
    text: |-
      Consider a probability space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, P)$.
      The _probability_ of an [event](/definitions/event) $A \in \mathcal{F}$ is the number $P(A)$ that the [probability measure](/definitions/probability-measure) $P$ assigns to it.
