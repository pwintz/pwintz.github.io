title: Symmetric relation
term: symmetric relation
applies_to: relations
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A [relation](/definitions/relation) is _symmetric_ if, whenever $a$ is related to $b$, $b$ is also related to $a$.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A [relation](/definitions/relation) $R$ is _symmetric_ if $x \mathrel{R} y$ implies $y \mathrel{R} x$. It is _antisymmetric_ if $x \mathrel{R} y$ and $y \mathrel{R} x$ imply $x = y$.
---
Antisymmetric does not mean "not symmetric." Equality is both symmetric and antisymmetric, and some [relations](/definitions/relation) are neither.
