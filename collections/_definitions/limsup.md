title: Limit superior
term: limit superior
applies_to: sequences
synonyms: [$\limsup$]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _limit superior_ of a [sequence](/definitions/sequence) is the largest value that its terms keep coming back close to forever.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _limit superior_ of a [sequence](/definitions/sequence) $(a_n)$ is $\limsup_{n \to \infty} a_n = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sup_{k \geq n} a_k$, the largest value that a subsequence of $(a_n)$ can converge to (allowing $\pm \infty$).
properties:
  - >-
    The limit superior always exists in the extended [real numbers](/definitions/real-number), even when the limit does not.
---
