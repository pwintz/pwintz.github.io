title: Laplacian
term: Laplacian
applies_to: scalar functions
synonyms: [$\Delta f$, $\nabla^2 f$]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a function of several variables, like the temperature at each point of a metal plate.
      The _Laplacian_ of the function measures how much its value at a point differs from the average of the values at nearby points.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a twice-differentiable function $f : \reals^n \to \reals$.
      The _Laplacian_ of $f$ is

      $$\Delta f = \nabla \cdot \nabla f = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1^2} + \dots + \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n^2},$$

      the [divergence](/definitions/divergence) of the [gradient](/definitions/gradient).
