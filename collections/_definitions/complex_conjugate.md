---
title: Complex Conjugate
term: complex conjugate
applies_to: complex numbers
synonyms: [conjugate of a complex number, '$\overline{z}$', '$z^{\ast}$']
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a complex number $z = a + b i$, where $a$ and $b$ are [real numbers](/dictionary/definitions/real-number) and $i = \sqrt{-1}$ is the [imaginary unit](/dictionary/definitions/imaginary-unit).
      The _complex conjugate_ of $z$ is the complex number with the sign of the imaginary part flipped:

      $$\conj{z} = a - b i.$$
properties:
  - |-
    In the complex plane, $\conj{z}$ is the [reflection](/dictionary/definitions/reflection-geometry) of $z$ across the real axis.
  - |-
    The product of a complex number $z$ and its conjugate is the square of the absolute value of $z$: $z \conj{z} = a^2 + b^2 = \abs{z}^2$.
  - |-
    A complex number $z$ is real if and only if $\conj{z} = z$.
  - |-
    Conjugation respects addition and multiplication: $\conjOf{z + w} = \conj{z} + \conj{w}$ and $\conjOf{z w} = \conj{z} \, \conj{w}$.
---
Most mathematics texts write the complex conjugate of $z$ as $\overline{z}$, and physics texts often write it as $z^{\ast}$.
Readers can change the notation used throughout this dictionary in the [notation settings](/settings/notation/).
