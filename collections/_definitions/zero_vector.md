published: false
title: Zero vector
term: zero vector
applies_to: vector spaces
synonyms: ["$\\mathbf{0}$"]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _zero vector_ is the arrow with length zero. It does not point in any direction.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _zero vector_ is the [vector](/definitions/vector) whose components are all zero.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _zero vector_ of a [vector](/definitions/vector) space $V$ is its additive identity: the vector $\mathbf{0}$ with $v + \mathbf{0} = v$ for every $v \in V$. In a space of functions, it is the function that is $0$ everywhere.
properties:
  - >-
    The zero vector is the only [vector](/definitions/vector) with length zero, and it has no direction.
