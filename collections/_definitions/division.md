title: Division
term: division
applies_to: numbers
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      _Division_ is splitting an amount into equal groups. $12 \div 3 = 4$ because $12$ split into $3$ equal groups gives $4$ in each group.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      _Division_ of $a$ by $b$ (for $b \neq 0$), written $a \div b$, gives the number $c$ with $b c = a$.
