---
title: Wave Speed
term: wave speed
applies_to: waves
synonyms: ['$c$', '$v$']
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Consider a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) such as an ocean wave or sound wave. 
      The _wave speed_ measures how fast the peaks of the wave move from place to place.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) traveling through a medium.
      The _wave speed_ is the distance that a peak of the wave travels per unit time.
properties:
  - |-
    The wave speed $c$, the [frequency](/dictionary/definitions/frequency-oscillation) $f$, and the [wavelength](/dictionary/definitions/wavelength) $\lambda$ of a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) are related by 
    
    $$c = f \lambda.$$
  - |-
    For small disturbances, the wave speed depends on the medium, such as its density and stiffness, but not on the [amplitude](/dictionary/definitions/amplitude) of the [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave).
  - |-
    When the wave speed depends on the [frequency](/dictionary/definitions/frequency-oscillation), which is called [dispersion](/dictionary/definitions/dispersion-wave), a peak of a single-frequency wave travels at the _phase speed_ $\omega / k$, and a group of [waves](/dictionary/definitions/wave) with nearby frequencies travels at the _group speed_ $\diff{\omega} / \diff{k}$, which carries the [energy](/dictionary/definitions/energy).
  - |-
    A change in wave speed from one medium to another causes [refraction](/dictionary/definitions/refraction).
---
The speed of sound is about $343$ m/s in air at $20^{\circ}$C and about $1480$ m/s in water.
The speed of light in a vacuum is $c_0 = 299{,}792{,}458$ m/s, exactly.
