title: Hausdorff space
term: Hausdorff
applies_to: topological spaces
synonyms: [T₂]
levels:
  graduate:
    context: topology
    text: |-
      Consider a topological space $X$, and two distinct points in it.
      The space $X$ is _Hausdorff_ if every two distinct points have disjoint [neighborhoods](/dictionary/definitions/neighborhood).
properties:
  - >-
    In a Hausdorff space, limits of [sequences](/dictionary/definitions/sequence) are unique.
  - >-
    Every [metric space](/dictionary/definitions/metric-space) is Hausdorff.
