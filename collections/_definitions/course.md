---
title: Course
term: course
applies_to: vehicles
synonyms: [track, course over ground]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _course_ of a ship, airplane, or car is the compass direction that it moves, such as north or east.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a vehicle that moves on or above the surface of the earth.
      The _course_ of the vehicle is the [angle](/dictionary/definitions/angular-measure) from north to the direction that the vehicle moves, measured clockwise and written as a number $\chi$ such that

      $$0^\circ \le \chi < 360^\circ.$$
    caveat: >-
      In navigation, "course" sometimes means the direction that the navigator plans to move, and "track" means the direction that the vehicle actually moves.
properties:
  - |-
    The course of a vehicle is the direction of the horizontal part of its velocity.
  - |-
    A vehicle that is not moving does not have a course.
  - |-
    The [heading](/dictionary/definitions/heading) of a vehicle is the direction that it points, while its course is the direction that it moves.
    A crosswind or a current makes the course differ from the heading.
---
