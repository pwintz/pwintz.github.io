title: Point
term: point
applies_to: geometric spaces
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _point_ is an exact location, drawn as a dot.
  undergraduate:
    context: affine spaces
    text: |-
      A _point_ is an element of an affine space. Subtracting two points gives a [vector](/definitions/vector) (the [displacement](/definitions/displacement) between them), and adding a vector to a point gives a point, but two points cannot be added.
