title: Volume
term: volume
applies_to: solids and regions
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a solid object, like a box or a ball.
      The _volume_ of the solid is the amount of space it takes up, measured in cubic units.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a region $R \subseteq \reals^n$.
      The _volume_ of $R$ is its three-dimensional (or $n$-dimensional) size, given by $\int_R 1 \mathrm{d}x$.
