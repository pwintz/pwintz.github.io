title: Fraction
term: fraction
applies_to: numbers and notation
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _fraction_ is a number written as $\frac{a}{b}$ that means $a$ parts of a whole that is split into $b$ equal parts.
    caveat: >-
      Fractions can be larger than one whole, such as $\frac{5}{4}$, and the numerator and denominator need not be [whole numbers](/definitions/whole-numbers).
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _fraction_ is an expression of the form $\frac{a}{b}$, with numerator $a$ and denominator $b \neq 0$, that denotes $a$ divided by $b$.
---
A fraction is a way of _writing_ a number. Different fractions, such as $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{2}{4}$, can denote the same number.
