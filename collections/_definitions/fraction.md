      $$\frac{a}{b}$$

  undergraduate:

      The _fraction_ $a / b$ is the element of $F$ defined by

      $$\frac{a}{b} = a b^{-1},$$

      where $b^{-1}$ is the multiplicative [inverse](/dictionary/definitions/inverse-element) of $b$.

A fraction is a way of _writing_ a number.
Different fractions, such as $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{2}{4}$, can denote the same number.

When written like $a / b$, the slash between the numerator $a$ and the denominator $b$ is called a _solidus_ or _forward slash_ (it is leaning forward, in the direction of text).

The line between $a$ and $b$ in the expression $\frac{a}{b}$ is called a _fraction bar_.
