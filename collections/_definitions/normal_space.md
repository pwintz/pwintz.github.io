title: Normal space
term: normal
applies_to: topological spaces
synonyms: [T₄]
levels:
  graduate:
    context: topology
    text: |-
      Consider a [T₁](/definitions/t1-space) topological space, and two disjoint closed sets in it.
      The space is _normal_ if any two disjoint closed sets have disjoint open neighborhoods.
