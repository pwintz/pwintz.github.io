title: Discrete distribution
term: discrete distribution
applies_to: probability distributions
synonyms: [discrete random variable]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [random variable](/dictionary/definitions/random-variable) $X$.
      The [distribution](/dictionary/definitions/probability-distribution) of $X$ is _discrete_ if $X$ takes values in a list of separate values, such as the integers, and each value in the list has a positive [probability](/dictionary/definitions/probability).
  undergraduate:
    context: measure-theoretic probability
    text: |-
      Consider a [random variable](/dictionary/definitions/random-variable) $X$.
      The [distribution](/dictionary/definitions/probability-distribution) of $X$ is _discrete_ if there is a [countable](/dictionary/definitions/countable) set $S \subseteq \reals$ such that

      $$P(X \in S) = 1.$$
properties:
  - >-
    A discrete distribution is described by its [probability mass function](/dictionary/definitions/probability-mass-function) $p(x) = P(X = x)$, and its [cumulative distribution function](/dictionary/definitions/cumulative-distribution-function) is a staircase.
