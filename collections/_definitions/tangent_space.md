title: Tangent space
term: tangent space
applies_to: smooth manifolds
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _tangent space_ of a curve or surface at a point is the set of directions you can move along it at that point: the directions along the tangent line of a curve, or the tangent plane of a surface.
  undergraduate:
    context: smooth surfaces in $\reals^n$
    text: |-
      The _tangent space_ $T_x M$ of a [smooth](/definitions/smooth) surface $M$ at $x$ is the set of velocities $\gamma^{\prime}(0)$ of smooth curves $\gamma$ in $M$ with $\gamma(0) = x$.
properties:
  - >-
    The tangent space is a [vector](/definitions/vector) space whose dimension equals the dimension of $M$.
