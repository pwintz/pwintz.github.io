title: Polynomial
term: polynomial
applies_to: expressions and algebraic objects
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _polynomial_ in $x$ is an [expression](/definitions/expression) of the form $a_n x^n + \dots + a_1 x + a_0$, built from numbers and the variable $x$ using addition, subtraction, and multiplication only.
  undergraduate:
    context: algebra
    text: |-
      Consider a ring $R$, such as $\reals$ or $\mathbb{Z}$, and a variable $x$.
      A _polynomial_ in $x$ with [coefficients](/definitions/coefficient) in $R$ is a formal sum

      $$a_0 + a_1 x + \dots + a_n x^n$$

      with $a_0, \dots, a_n \in R$.
      Two polynomials are equal exactly when their coefficients are equal.
properties:
  - |-
    The _degree_ of a nonzero polynomial is the largest $n$ with $a_n \neq 0$.
  - |-
    A nonzero polynomial with coefficients in a field has at most as many [roots](/definitions/root-of-function) as its degree.
