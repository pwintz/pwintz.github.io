title: Skew-symmetric matrix
term: skew-symmetric
applies_to: square matrices
synonyms: [antisymmetric]
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a square [matrix](/dictionary/definitions/matrix) $A$.
      The matrix $A$ is _skew-symmetric_ if $A$ equals the negative of its [transpose](/dictionary/definitions/transpose):

      $$A^{\top} = -A.$$
properties:
  - >-
    The diagonal entries of a skew-symmetric matrix are $0$, because each diagonal entry $a_{ii}$ of a skew-symmetric matrix $A = [a_{ij}]$ satisfies $a_{ii} = -a_{ii}$.
  - >-
    Every square matrix is the sum of a [symmetric](/dictionary/definitions/symmetric-matrix) part $\frac{1}{2}(A + A^{\top})$ and a skew-symmetric part $\frac{1}{2}(A - A^{\top})$.
