---
title: Stabilization
term: stabilization
applies_to: control problems
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a system that does not stay at a desired resting state by itself, like a broom balanced on your hand.
      _Stabilization_ is designing a controller that makes the system settle at that resting state and stay near it.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a control system with an [equilibrium](/dictionary/definitions/equilibrium) (or a set) that is to be made stable.
      _Stabilization_ is the control problem of finding a [feedback](/dictionary/definitions/feedback) law that makes the equilibrium of the closed-loop system [asymptotically stable](/dictionary/definitions/asymptotically-stable).
---
