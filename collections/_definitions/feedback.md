title: Feedback
term: feedback
applies_to: control systems
synonyms: [feedback control]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      _Feedback_ is when a system watches its own results and adjusts.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      _Feedback_ is using information about a system's output to decide its input.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      _Feedback_ is the use of measurements of a system's output (or state) to determine its input, as in $u = k(y)$.
properties:
  - >-
    Feedback can stabilize unstable systems and reduce sensitivity to [disturbances](/definitions/disturbance) and model errors, but too much loop gain can destabilize a stable system.
