title: Total derivative (along a path)
term: total derivative (along a path)
applies_to: functions along paths
synonyms: [material derivative (in fluid mechanics)]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      When $f$ depends on time both directly and through another changing quantity $x(t)$, its _total derivative_ is its rate of change along the way, combining both effects by the chain rule.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      For a function $f(t, x)$ evaluated along a path $x(t)$, the _total derivative_ is $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t} f(t, x(t)) = \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} + \nabla_x f \cdot \dot{x}$. It accounts for the change in $f$ both from its explicit dependence on $t$ and through $x(t)$.
---
Another common meaning is the [linear map](/definitions/linear-map) $Df(x)$: see [total derivative (linear map)](/definitions/total-derivative-linear-map) and [the meanings of "total derivative"](/distinctions/total-derivative).
