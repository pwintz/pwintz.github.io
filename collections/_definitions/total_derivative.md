---
title: Total derivative (along a path)
term: total derivative (along a path)
applies_to: functions along paths
synonyms: [material derivative (in fluid mechanics)]
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      For a function $f(t, x)$ evaluated along a path $x(t)$, the _total derivative_ is
      
      $$\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t} f(t, x(t)) = \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} + \nabla_x f \cdot \dot{x}.$$
---

The total derivative accounts for the change in $f$ both from its explicit dependence on $t$ and through $x(t)$.


Another common meaning of "total derivative" is the [linear map](/definitions/linear-map) $Df(x)$: see [total derivative (linear map)](/definitions/total-derivative-linear-map) and [the meanings of "total derivative"](/distinctions/total-derivative).
