title: Nyquist stability criterion
term: Nyquist
applies_to: feedback loops
levels:
  undergraduate:
    context: linear feedback systems
    text: |-
      The _Nyquist_ stability criterion is a test for the stability of a closed [feedback](/definitions/feedback) loop based on how many times the plot of the open-loop frequency response $L(i \omega)$ encircles the point $-1$, counted against the number of unstable open-loop [poles](/definitions/pole).
properties:
  - >-
    The Bode stability criterion (positive gain and [phase margins](/definitions/phase-margin)) is a special case that applies when the open-loop system is stable and its frequency response crosses the critical gain and phase only once.
