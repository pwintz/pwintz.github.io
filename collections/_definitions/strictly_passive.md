title: Strictly passive system
term: strictly passive
applies_to: input-output systems
levels:
  graduate:
    context: nonlinear control
    text: |-
      A system is _strictly passive_ if $u^{\top} y \geq \dot{V} + \psi(x)$ for some [positive definite](/definitions/positive-definite-function) function $\psi$ of the state: energy is dissipated whenever the state is not at the origin.
