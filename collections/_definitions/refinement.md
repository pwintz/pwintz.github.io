title: Refinement
term: refinement
applies_to: partitions and covers
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a partition that splits something into pieces, and another partition of the same thing.
      The second partition is a _refinement_ of the first if it splits the first one's pieces into smaller pieces.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider two partitions (or covers) $P$ and $P^{\prime}$ of the same set.
      The partition $P^{\prime}$ is a _refinement_ of $P$ if every block of $P^{\prime}$ is contained in some block of $P$, so $P^{\prime}$ splits the set more finely.
      For partitions of an interval, $P^{\prime}$ refines $P$ if every point of $P$ is also a point of $P^{\prime}$.
