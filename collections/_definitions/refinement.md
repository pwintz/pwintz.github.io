title: Refinement
term: refinement
applies_to: partitions and covers
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _refinement_ of a partition is a partition that splits its pieces into smaller pieces.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A partition (or cover) $P^{\prime}$ is a _refinement_ of $P$ if every block of $P^{\prime}$ is contained in some block of $P$, so $P^{\prime}$ splits the set more finely. For partitions of an interval, $P^{\prime}$ refines $P$ if every point of $P$ is also a point of $P^{\prime}$.
