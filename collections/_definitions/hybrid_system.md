title: Hybrid system
term: hybrid system
applies_to: dynamical systems
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _hybrid system_ is a dynamical system whose state can both change continuously (flow) and change instantaneously (jump).
  graduate:
    context: "the framework of Goebel, Sanfelice, and Teel"
    text: |-
      A _hybrid system_ is given by $\dot{x} \in F(x)$ for $x \in C$ and $x^{+} \in G(x)$ for $x \in D$, where $C$ is the flow set, $F$ is the flow map, $D$ is the jump set, and $G$ is the jump map.
