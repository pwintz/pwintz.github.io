title: Uniform distribution
term: uniform distribution
applies_to: random variables
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a random experiment with [outcomes](/definitions/outcome) in an interval $[a, b]$ (or in a finite set).
      The outcome has a _uniform distribution_ if all outcomes are equally likely: for an interval, the [probability](/definitions/probability) of landing in a subinterval is proportional to its length,

      $$P(c \leq X \leq d) = \frac{d - c}{b - a} \quad \text{for } a \leq c \leq d \leq b.$$
