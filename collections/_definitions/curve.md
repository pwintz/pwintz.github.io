---
title: Curve
term: curve
applies_to: geometric objects
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _curve_ is the path traced by the tip of a pencil that moves across a page without lifting off.
      
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _curve_ is a one-dimensional shape in the plane or in space.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _curve_ is a [continuous](/dictionary/definitions/continuous) map $\gamma : I \to \reals^n$ from an interval $I$ (a parametrized curve), or the [range](/dictionary/definitions/range-image) of such a map.
---
"Curve" is used for both the map $\gamma$ and its range.
The same range can be traced by many different parametrizations.
