title: Complete solution (hybrid systems)
term: complete (solution)
applies_to: solutions of hybrid systems
levels:
  graduate:
    text: |-
      A solution of a [hybrid system](/definitions/hybrid-system) is _complete_ if its hybrid time domain is unbounded: it continues forever, through unbounded flow time, infinitely many jumps, or both.
properties:
  - >-
    Every complete solution is maximal.
