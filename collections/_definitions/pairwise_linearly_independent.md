---
title: Pairwise linearly independent vectors
term: pairwise linearly independent
applies_to: lists of vectors
synonyms: [pairwise non-parallel]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A list of vectors is _pairwise linearly independent_ if every two vectors in the list point along different lines through the origin.
  undergraduate:
    context: vector spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a list $v_1, \dots, v_k$ of vectors in a vector space.
      The list is _pairwise linearly independent_ if, for every two different indices $i$ and $j$, the two vectors $v_i$ and $v_j$ are [linearly independent](/definitions/linearly-independent).
properties:
  - >-
    Every [linearly independent](/definitions/linearly-independent) list is pairwise linearly independent, but a pairwise linearly independent list can be linearly dependent: $(1, 0)$, $(0, 1)$, and $(1, 1)$ are pairwise linearly independent, and $(1, 1) = (1, 0) + (0, 1)$.
  - >-
    For lists of two vectors, pairwise linear independence and linear independence are the same.
---
