title: Symmetry (of a shape)
term: symmetry (of a shape)
applies_to: shapes and objects
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A shape has _symmetry_ if it looks the same after a flip, a turn, or a slide. A square looks the same after a quarter turn.
  graduate:
    context: group theory
    text: |-
      A _symmetry_ of an object is a transformation of the surrounding space (such as an isometry) that maps the object onto itself.
properties:
  - >-
    The symmetries of an object form a [group](/definitions/group) under composition, its symmetry group. For example, a square has $8$ symmetries.
