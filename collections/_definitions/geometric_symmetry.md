title: Symmetry (of a shape)
term: symmetry (of a shape)
applies_to: shapes and objects
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a shape that you flip over, turn, or slide.
      The shape has _symmetry_ if it looks the same afterward.
      A square looks the same after a quarter turn.
  graduate:
    context: group theory
    text: |-
      Consider an object in a space, and the transformations of the space, such as isometries.
      A _symmetry_ of the object is a transformation of the surrounding space that maps the object onto itself.
properties:
  - >-
    The symmetries of an object form a [group](/definitions/group) under composition, its symmetry group.
    For example, a square has $8$ symmetries.
