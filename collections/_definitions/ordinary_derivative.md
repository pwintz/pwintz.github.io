---
title: Ordinary derivative
term: ordinary derivative
applies_to: functions of one variable
synonyms: ["$f^{\\prime}$", "$\\frac{\\mathrm{d}f}{\\mathrm{d}x}$"]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a function $f$ of one variable.
      The _derivative_ of $f$ is

      $$f^{\prime}(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}:$$

      the slope of the graph of $f$ at $x$, or the rate at which $f$ changes.
---
