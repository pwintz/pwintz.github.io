title: One-sided derivative
term: one-sided derivative
applies_to: functions of one variable
synonyms: [left derivative, right derivative]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The right _one-sided derivative_ of $f$ at $a$ is $\lim_{h \to 0^{+}} \frac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h}$, and the left one-sided derivative uses $h \to 0^{-}$ instead.
properties:
  - >-
    A function is [differentiable](/definitions/differentiable) at $a$ exactly when both one-sided derivatives exist and are equal. For $\lvert x \rvert$ at $0$, they are $1$ and $-1$.
