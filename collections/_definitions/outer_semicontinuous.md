title: Outer semicontinuous set-valued map
term: outer semicontinuous
applies_to: set-valued maps
levels:
  graduate:
    context: set-valued analysis (Rockafellar and Wets)
    text: |-
      A set-valued map $F : X \rightrightarrows Y$ is _outer semicontinuous_ at $x$ if $\limsup_{y \to x} F(y) \subseteq F(x)$: whenever $y_n \to x$, $z_n \in F(y_n)$, and $z_n \to z$, then $z \in F(x)$.
properties:
  - >-
    $F$ is outer semicontinuous everywhere if and only if its graph is closed.
