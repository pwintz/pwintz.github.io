title: Circle criterion
term: circle criterion
applies_to: "Lur'e systems"
levels:
  graduate:
    context: absolute stability
    text: |-
      The _circle criterion_ is a frequency-domain test for the absolute stability of a linear system in [feedback](/definitions/feedback) with any memoryless nonlinearity in a given sector $[k_1, k_2]$.
properties:
  - >-
    The circle criterion generalizes the [Nyquist](/definitions/nyquist-criterion) criterion, with the point $-1$ replaced by a disk determined by $k_1$ and $k_2$.
