title: Circle criterion
term: circle criterion
applies_to: "Lur'e systems"
levels:
  graduate:
    context: absolute stability
    text: |-
      Consider a linear system in [feedback](/dictionary/definitions/feedback) with a memoryless nonlinearity that is only known to lie in a sector $[k_1, k_2]$.
      The _circle criterion_ is a frequency-domain test for the absolute stability of such a feedback system, for every nonlinearity in the sector.
properties:
  - >-
    The circle criterion generalizes the [Nyquist](/dictionary/definitions/nyquist-criterion) criterion, with the point $-1$ replaced by a disk determined by $k_1$ and $k_2$.
