levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a quantity that you can calculate from other quantities, like the area of a rectangle from its sides.
      A _formula_ is a rule, written with symbols, for calculating one quantity from others.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a quantity that depends on others, like the area of a [circle](/definitions/circle) on its radius.
      A _formula_ is an equation that expresses one quantity in terms of others.

In logic, a "[formula](/definitions/formula-logic)" is any well-formed statement (which may be true or false), such as $\forall x (x \geq 0)$.
That is a different meaning.
