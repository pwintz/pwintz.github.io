title: Full rank
term: full rank
applies_to: matrices
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      An $m \times n$ [matrix](/definitions/matrix) has _full rank_ if its [rank](/definitions/matrix-rank) is $\min(m, n)$, the largest possible. It has _full column rank_ if its rank is $n$ (independent columns) and _full row rank_ if its rank is $m$ (independent rows).
