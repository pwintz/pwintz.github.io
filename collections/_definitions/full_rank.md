title: Full rank
term: full rank
applies_to: matrices
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider an $m \times n$ [matrix](/definitions/matrix), whose [rank](/definitions/matrix-rank) is at most $\min(m, n)$.
      The matrix has _full rank_ if its rank is $\min(m, n)$, the largest possible.
      It has _full column rank_ if its rank is $n$ (independent columns) and _full row rank_ if its rank is $m$ (independent rows).
