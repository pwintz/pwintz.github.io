title: Inner product
term: inner product
applies_to: vector spaces
synonyms: ["$\\langle x, y \\rangle$"]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An _inner product_ is a way of multiplying two [vectors](/definitions/vector) to get a number, generalizing the [dot product](/definitions/dot-product), that gives a way to measure lengths and angles.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a real [vector](/definitions/vector) space and a [bilinear form](/definitions/bilinear-form) $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ on it.
      The form is an _inner product_ if it is symmetric ($\langle x, y \rangle = \langle y, x \rangle$) and [positive definite](/definitions/positive-definite-function) ($\langle x, x \rangle > 0$ for $x \neq 0$).
      For complex vector spaces, symmetry is replaced by conjugate symmetry.
