title: Consistent numerical scheme
term: consistent
applies_to: numerical schemes
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A numerical method for a differential equation is _consistent_ if each of its steps approximates the equation better and better as the step size shrinks.
  undergraduate:
    context: numerical analysis
    text: |-
      A numerical scheme is _consistent_ if its local truncation error tends to zero as the step sizes tend to zero: the exact solution nearly satisfies the discrete equations.
