title: Plot
term: plot
applies_to: visualizations
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a grid, with one quantity across and another up the side.
      A _plot_ is a picture made by placing dots or lines on the grid to show how one quantity changes with the other.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _plot_ is a drawing that shows how one quantity varies with another by placing marks at positions given by coordinates.
