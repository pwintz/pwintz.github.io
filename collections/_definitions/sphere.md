title: Sphere
term: sphere
applies_to: sets of points
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _sphere_ is the surface of a [ball](/definitions/ball). A sphere is hollow: it does not include the inside.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _sphere_ is the set of all points in space at a fixed distance $r$ (the radius) from a point $c$ (the center).
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      In a [metric space](/definitions/metric-space) with [metric](/definitions/metric) $d$, the _sphere_ of radius $r$ around $c$ is $S(c, r) = \lbrace x : d(x, c) = r \rbrace$. In $\reals^{n+1}$ with the Euclidean metric, the unit sphere is written $S^n$; it is $n$-dimensional.
---
The dimension convention is a common source of confusion: the ordinary sphere in $\reals^3$ is the $2$-sphere $S^2$, because its surface is two-dimensional.
