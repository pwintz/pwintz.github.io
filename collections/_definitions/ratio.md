title: Ratio
term: ratio
applies_to: pairs of quantities
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _ratio_ is a comparison of two amounts, written $a : b$.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _ratio_ $a : b$ compares two (or more) quantities by division. Two ratios $a : b$ and $c : d$ are equal if $a / b = c / d$. Ratios of more than two quantities are also used.
