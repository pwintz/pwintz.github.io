A chart does not need coordinates: a pie chart has none.
In differential geometry, a ["chart"](/dictionary/definitions/chart-manifold) is something else entirely: a [coordinate map](/dictionary/definitions/chart-map) on part of a [manifold](/dictionary/definitions/manifold).
