title: Sequence
term: sequence
applies_to: collections
synonyms: [list, tuple]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _sequence_ is a list of things in a particular order.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _sequence_ is an ordered list of terms $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ Both order and repetition matter: $(1, 2)$, $(2, 1)$, and $(1, 1, 2)$ are different sequences.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _sequence_ is a function from $\mathbb{N}$ (an infinite sequence) or from $\lbrace 1, \dots, n \rbrace$ (a finite sequence, or $n$-tuple) to a set.
