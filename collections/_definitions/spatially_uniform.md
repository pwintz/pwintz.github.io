---
title: Uniform (in space)
term: uniform (in space)
applies_to: fields, materials, and densities
synonyms: [homogeneous]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a quantity that varies over a region, such as the density of a material or the strength of an electric field.
      The quantity is _uniform_ if it has the same value at every point of the region.
---
