title: Standard deviation
term: standard deviation
applies_to: random variables and data
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider data, or a [random variable](/definitions/random-variable).
      The _standard deviation_ $\sigma$ is the square root of the [variance](/definitions/variance), and it measures how far values typically are from the mean, in the same units as the data:

      $$\sigma = \sqrt{\operatorname{Var}}.$$
