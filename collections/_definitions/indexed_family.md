title: Indexed family
term: indexed family
applies_to: collections
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      An _indexed family_ is a function $i \mapsto x_i$ from an index set $I$ to a set, written $$(x_i)_{i \in I}$$. Unlike a set, an indexed family can repeat elements. A [sequence](/definitions/sequence) is an indexed family with $I = \mathbb{N}$.
