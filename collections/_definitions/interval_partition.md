title: Partition of an interval
term: partition (of an interval)
applies_to: intervals
synonyms: [mesh, grid]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _partition_ of an interval is a list of cut points that split it into smaller intervals, as in the rectangles of a Riemann sum.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _partition_ of an interval $[a, b]$ is a finite [sequence](/definitions/sequence) of points $a = x_0 < x_1 < \dots < x_n = b$ that divides $[a, b]$ into subintervals $[x_{k-1}, x_k]$, as in a Riemann sum.
---
Despite the name, this is not a partition of $[a, b]$ in the set sense: neighboring subintervals share an endpoint.
