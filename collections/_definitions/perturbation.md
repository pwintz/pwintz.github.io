title: Perturbation
term: perturbation
applies_to: systems and equations
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a system, with its equations, parameters, and starting state.
      A _perturbation_ is a small change to the system, its parameters, or its starting state.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a system $\dot{x} = f(x)$, with its parameters and initial state.
      A _perturbation_ is a (usually small) change to the system's equations, parameters, or initial state, such as $\dot{x} = f(x) + g(t, x)$ with $g$ small.
      A perturbation need not be an input signal.
