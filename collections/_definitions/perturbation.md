title: Perturbation
term: perturbation
applies_to: systems and equations
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _perturbation_ is a small change to a system, its parameters, or its starting state.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _perturbation_ is a (usually small) change to a system's equations, parameters, or initial state, such as $\dot{x} = f(x) + g(t, x)$ with $g$ small. A perturbation need not be an input signal.
