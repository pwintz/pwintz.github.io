---
title: Tracking
term: tracking
applies_to: control problems
levels:
  # elementary:
  #   text: |-
  #     _Tracking_ is making something follow a moving target.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      _Tracking_ is making a quantity follow a changing target, as a driver steers a car to follow a road.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      In control theory, _tracking_ is a control problem with the goal of making the output $y(t)$ follow a time-varying reference $r(t)$, so that the tracking error $e(t) = r(t) - y(t)$ converges to zero (or stays small).
properties:
  - >-
    Tracking a reference can be posed as stabilizing the origin of the error dynamics.
---
