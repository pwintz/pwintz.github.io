title: Convergent numerical scheme
term: convergent (scheme)
applies_to: numerical schemes
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A numerical method for a differential equation is _convergent_ if its answer approaches the exact solution as the step size shrinks.
  undergraduate:
    context: numerical analysis
    text: |-
      A numerical scheme is _convergent_ if its numerical solution approaches the exact solution as the step sizes tend to zero.
