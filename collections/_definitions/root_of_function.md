title: Root (zero) of a function
term: root
applies_to: functions and equations
synonyms: [zero of a function]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _root_ of a function $f$ is a number $x$ with $f(x) = 0$. A root of an equation is a solution of it.
---
Strictly, an _equation_ such as $x^2 - 4 = 0$ has roots (solutions), while a _function_ such as $x \mapsto x^2 - 4$ has zeros. In practice, both words are used for both.
