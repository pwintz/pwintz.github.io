title: Inner product space
term: inner product space
applies_to: spaces
synonyms: [pre-Hilbert space]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An _inner product space_ is a [vector](/dictionary/definitions/vector) space with a way of measuring lengths and angles.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      An _inner product space_ is a [vector](/dictionary/definitions/vector) space together with an [inner product](/dictionary/definitions/inner-product).
properties:
  - >-
    Every inner product space is a [normed space](/dictionary/definitions/normed-space) with $\norm{x} = \sqrt{\ip{x}{x}}$.
  - >-
    The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality holds: $\abs{\ip{x}{y}} \leq \norm{x} \norm{y}$.
