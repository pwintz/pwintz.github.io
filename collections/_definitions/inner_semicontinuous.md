title: Inner semicontinuous set-valued map
term: inner semicontinuous
applies_to: set-valued maps
levels:
  graduate:
    context: set-valued analysis (Rockafellar and Wets)
    text: |-
      A set-valued map $F : X \rightrightarrows Y$ is _inner semicontinuous_ at $x$ if $F(x) \subseteq \liminf_{y \to x} F(y)$: for every $z \in F(x)$ and every [sequence](/definitions/sequence) $y_n \to x$, there are points $z_n \in F(y_n)$ (for $n$ large enough) with $z_n \to z$.
