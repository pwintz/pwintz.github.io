title: Equal
term: equal
applies_to: any objects
synonyms: [$=$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of two amounts, like the number of apples in two baskets.
      The amounts are _equal_ if the amounts have the same value.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider two mathematical objects $a$ and $b$.
      The statement $a = b$ means that $a$ and $b$ are _equal_: $a$ and $b$ are the same object.
properties:
  - >-
    Two sets are equal if and only if they have the same elements.
    Two functions are equal if and only if they have the same domain, [codomain](/definitions/codomain), and output at every input.
