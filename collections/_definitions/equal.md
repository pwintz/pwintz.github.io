---
published: false
title: Equal
term: equal
applies_to: any objects
synonyms: [$=$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Two quantities are _equal_ if they have the same value.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      $a = b$ means that $a$ and $b$ are _equal_: they are the same object.
properties:
  - >-
    Two sets are equal if and only if they have the same elements. Two functions are equal if and only if they have the same domain, [codomain](/definitions/codomain), and output at every input.
---
