title: Edge of a polygon or polyhedron
term: edge (of a polygon or polyhedron)
applies_to: polygons and polyhedra
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      An _edge_ is a straight side of a flat shape, or a line where two faces of a solid meet. A cube has $12$ edges.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An _edge_ of a polygon is a line segment between two adjacent vertices (one of its sides). An _edge_ of a polyhedron is a segment where two of its faces meet.
