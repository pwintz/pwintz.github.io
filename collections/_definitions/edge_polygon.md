---
title: Edge of a polygon or polyhedron
term: edge (of a polygon or polyhedron)
applies_to: polygons and polyhedra
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A polygon is a 2D shape with straight sides that form a loop. Each side is called an _edge_ of the polygon. 
    # Think of a flat shape with straight sides, or a solid with flat faces, like a box.
    # An _edge_ is a straight side of the flat shape, or a line where two faces of the solid meet.
    # A cube has $12$ edges.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      In 2D, an _edge_ of the polygon is a line segment between two adjacent vertices (one of its sides).
      In 3D, an _edge_ of the polyhedron is a segment where two of its faces meet, such as the 12 line segments separating the faces of a cube.
---
