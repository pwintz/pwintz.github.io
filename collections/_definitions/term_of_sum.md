title: Term (of a sum)
term: term
applies_to: sums
synonyms: [summand]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      In a sum, each number being added is a _term_.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _term_ of a sum is one of the parts that are added together.
---
"Term" also means an element of a [sequence](/definitions/sequence) (the third term of $1, 4, 9, \dots$ is $9$) and, in logic, an expression that denotes an object.
