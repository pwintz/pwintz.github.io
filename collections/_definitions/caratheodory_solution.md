title: Carathéodory solution
term: Carathéodory solution
applies_to: differential equations
levels:
  graduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a differential equation $\dot{x} = f(t, x)$ whose right-hand side may be discontinuous in $t$.
      A _Carathéodory solution_ of the equation is an [absolutely continuous](/definitions/absolutely-continuous) function $x(t)$ such that $\dot{x}(t) = f(t, x(t))$ for almost every $t$.
properties:
  - >-
    Carathéodory solutions exist when $f$ is measurable in $t$, [continuous](/definitions/continuous) in $x$, and suitably [bounded](/definitions/bounded), which covers systems driven by discontinuous inputs $u(t)$.
