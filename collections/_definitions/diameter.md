title: Diameter
term: diameter
applies_to: sets
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _diameter_ of a [circle](/definitions/circle) is the distance across it, through its center.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _diameter_ of a [circle](/definitions/circle) (or [sphere](/definitions/sphere)) is the length of a line segment through its center with both endpoints on it.
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      The _diameter_ of a set $A$ is $\operatorname{diam}(A) = \sup \lbrace d(x, y) : x, y \in A \rbrace$, the largest distance between two points of $A$.
properties:
  - >-
    The diameter of a [circle](/definitions/circle) or [sphere](/definitions/sphere) is twice its radius. In a general [metric space](/definitions/metric-space), a [ball](/definitions/ball) of radius $r$ has diameter at most $2r$, and possibly less.
