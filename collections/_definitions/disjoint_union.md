title: Disjoint union
term: disjoint union (tagged)
applies_to: sets
synonyms: [tagged union, coproduct of sets, $\bigsqcup$]
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _disjoint union_ is a construction that combines sets while keeping their elements apart, even if the sets overlap. The disjoint union of $A$ and $B$ is $A \sqcup B = (\lbrace 1 \rbrace \times A) \cup (\lbrace 2 \rbrace \times B)$, so each element is tagged with the set it came from.
  graduate:
    context: category theory
    text: |-
      The _disjoint union_ is the coproduct in the category of sets: a set $A \sqcup B$ with injections $A \to A \sqcup B$ and $B \to A \sqcup B$ such that any pair of functions $A \to C$ and $B \to C$ factors uniquely through it.
