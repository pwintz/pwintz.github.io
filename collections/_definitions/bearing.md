---
title: Bearing
term: bearing
applies_to: vehicles and objects
synonyms: [compass bearing]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _bearing_ of an object from a ship, airplane, or car is the compass direction from the vehicle to the object, such as north or east.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a vehicle on or above the surface of the earth and an object at a different place.
      The _bearing_ of the object from the vehicle is the [angular measure](/dictionary/definitions/angular-measure) of the angle from north to the direction of the line from the vehicle to the object, measured clockwise and written as a number $\beta$ such that

      $$0^\circ \le \beta < 360^\circ.$$

      For example, an object due east of the vehicle has a bearing of $90^\circ$.
properties:
  - |-
    The bearing is measured the same way as the [heading](/dictionary/definitions/heading) and the [course](/dictionary/definitions/course), but it describes the direction to an object, not the direction the vehicle points or moves.
  - |-
    The bearing depends on the positions of the vehicle and the object, but not on the heading or the course of the vehicle.
  - |-
    The _relative bearing_ of an object is its bearing minus the heading of the vehicle, so it tells where the object is relative to the nose of the vehicle.
    For example, an object due east of a vehicle that points north has a bearing of $90^\circ$ and a relative bearing of $90^\circ$, but an object due east of a vehicle that points east has a bearing of $90^\circ$ and a relative bearing of $0^\circ$.
---
