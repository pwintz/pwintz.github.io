title: Distance
term: distance
applies_to: pairs of points
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _distance_ between two things is how far apart they are. A distance is never negative.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      On the number line, the _distance_ between $a$ and $b$ is $\lvert a - b \rvert$. In the plane, the distance between $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is $\sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2 + (y_1 - y_2)^2}$.
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      The _distance_ between points $x$ and $y$ of a [metric space](/definitions/metric-space) is the value $d(x, y)$ of its [metric](/definitions/metric) $d$.
