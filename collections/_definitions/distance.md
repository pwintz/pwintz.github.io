title: Distance
term: distance
applies_to: pairs of points
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of two things, like two houses on a street.
      The _distance_ between them is how far apart they are.
      No distance is negative.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider two points, either on the number line or in the plane.
      On the number line, the _distance_ between $a$ and $b$ is $\lvert a - b \rvert$.
      In the plane, the distance between $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is $\sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2 + (y_1 - y_2)^2}$.
  undergraduate:
    context: metric spaces
    text: |-
      Consider a [metric space](/definitions/metric-space) with [metric](/definitions/metric) $d$, and points $x$ and $y$ in it.
      The _distance_ between $x$ and $y$ is the value $d(x, y)$.
