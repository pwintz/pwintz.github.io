title: "Lyapunov's direct method"
term: Lyapunov (direct)
applies_to: nonlinear systems
synonyms: ["Lyapunov's second method"]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      _Lyapunov's direct method_ shows that a system settles at an [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) by finding an energy-like quantity that always decreases along its solutions, without solving the equations.
  undergraduate:
    context: nonlinear systems
    text: |-
      _Lyapunov's direct method_ shows that an [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) is [asymptotically stable](/definitions/asymptotically-stable) by finding a [positive definite](/definitions/positive-definite-function) function $V$ (a [Lyapunov function](/definitions/lyapunov-function)) whose derivative $\dot{V} = \nabla V \cdot f$ along solutions is negative definite. It requires no solutions of the system.
