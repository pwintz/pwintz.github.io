title: Polynomial equation
term: polynomial equation
applies_to: equations
synonyms: [algebraic equation]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [polynomial](/definitions/polynomial) $p$.
      A _polynomial equation_ is an [equation](/definitions/equation) of the form $p(x) = 0$, or one that can be rearranged into that form.
properties:
  - |-
    The solutions of $p(x) = 0$ are the [roots](/definitions/root-of-function) of $p$.
  - |-
    Polynomial equations of degree at most $4$ can be solved with formulas that use radicals, but those of degree $5$ or more cannot, in general (the Abel–Ruffini theorem).
