---
title: Manifold
term: manifold
applies_to: topological spaces
synonyms: [topological manifold]
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a positive [integer](/dictionary/definitions/integer) $n$.
      A _manifold_ of dimension $n$ is a set $M$ such that each point of $M$ has a [neighborhood](/dictionary/definitions/neighborhood) that can be bent and stretched, without tearing or gluing, into an [open ball](/dictionary/definitions/open-ball) in $\reals^n$.
  graduate:
    context: topology
    text: |-
      Consider a nonnegative [integer](/dictionary/definitions/integer) $n$.
      A _manifold_ of dimension $n$ is a [topological space](/dictionary/definitions/topological-space) $M$ such that

      1. $M$ is [Hausdorff](/dictionary/definitions/hausdorff-space),
      2. $M$ is [second countable](/dictionary/definitions/second-countable), and
      3. every point of $M$ has an [open neighborhood](/dictionary/definitions/open-neighborhood) that is [homeomorphic](/dictionary/definitions/homeomorphism) to an open [subset](/dictionary/definitions/subset) of $\reals^n$.
properties:
  - |-
    A manifold of dimension $n$ has an [atlas](/dictionary/definitions/atlas) of [charts](/dictionary/definitions/chart-manifold) to $\reals^n$.
---
