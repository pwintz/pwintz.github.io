title: Isomorphic
term: isomorphic
applies_to: mathematical structures
synonyms: [$\cong$]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Two structures are _isomorphic_ if they are the same except for the names of their elements.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Two structures ([groups](/definitions/group), [vector](/definitions/vector) spaces, graphs, and so on) are _isomorphic_ if there is a bijection between them that preserves the structure, and whose inverse also preserves the structure. Isomorphic structures are indistinguishable by their structure alone.
