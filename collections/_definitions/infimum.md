title: Infimum
term: infimum
applies_to: ordered sets
synonyms: [greatest lower bound, $\inf$]
levels:
  high-school:
    context: sets of real numbers
    text: |-
      The _infimum_ of a set is the largest number that is less than or equal to every element of the set.
  undergraduate:
    context: partially ordered sets
    text: |-
      The _infimum_ of $S$ is a [lower bound](/definitions/lower-bound) $m$ of $S$ such that $m^{\prime} \leq m$ for every lower bound $m^{\prime}$ of $S$.
properties:
  - >-
    Every nonempty set of [real numbers](/definitions/real-number) that is [bounded](/definitions/bounded) below has an infimum. This is the completeness of $\reals$, and it fails in $\mathbb{Q}$.
  - >-
    If the infimum of $S$ belongs to $S$, it is the minimum of $S$.
