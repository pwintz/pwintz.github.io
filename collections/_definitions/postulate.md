title: Postulate

applies_to: mathematical theories
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _postulate_ is a statement accepted without proof as a starting point, especially in geometry, as in Euclid's postulates.
properties:
  - >-
    In modern mathematics, "postulate" and [axiom](/definitions/axiom) are synonyms.
    Euclid used "postulate" for geometric assumptions and "common notion" for general ones, such as "things equal to the same thing are equal to each other."
