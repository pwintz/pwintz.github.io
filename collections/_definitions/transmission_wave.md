published: false
title: Transmission of a Wave
term: transmission (of a wave)
applies_to: waves
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      _Transmission_ is the passing of a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave), such as light or sound, through a surface into another material, such as light passing through a window.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) traveling through one material that reaches the boundary with a second material.
      _Transmission_ is the passing of part or all of the wave across the boundary into the second material.
      The wave that passes into the second material is called the _transmitted wave_.
properties:
  - |-
    At a boundary that absorbs no [energy](/dictionary/definitions/energy), the energy of the incident [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) equals the energy of the [reflected](/dictionary/definitions/reflection-wave) wave plus the energy of the transmitted wave.
  - |-
    The transmitted [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) changes direction by [refraction](/dictionary/definitions/refraction) when the second material has a different [wave speed](/dictionary/definitions/wave-speed).
  - |-
    The fraction of the [amplitude](/dictionary/definitions/amplitude) that is transmitted is the [transmission coefficient](/dictionary/definitions/transmission-coefficient).
---
In communications, _transmission_ means sending a signal, such as a radio broadcast, from a transmitter to a receiver.
