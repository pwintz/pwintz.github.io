---
title: Relation
term: relation
applies_to: sets
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _relation_ is a rule that determines whether whether two objects are related.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider sets $X$ and $Y$ and their product $X \times Y$.
      A binary _relation_ between $X$ and $Y$ is a [subset](/definitions/subset) $R \subseteq X \times Y$.
      For $x \in X$ and $y \in Y$, the statement $x \mathrel{R} y$ ("$x$ is related to $y$") is true if $(x, y) \in R$ and false if $(x, y) \notin R$.
