title: Lower semicontinuous set-valued map
term: lower semicontinuous
applies_to: set-valued maps
synonyms: [lower hemicontinuous]
levels:
  graduate:
    context: set-valued analysis (Berge)
    text: |-
      A set-valued map $F : X \rightrightarrows Y$ is _lower semicontinuous_ at $x$ if for every open set $U$ that intersects $F(x)$, there is a neighborhood $V$ of $x$ such that $F(y)$ intersects $U$ for all $y \in V$.
