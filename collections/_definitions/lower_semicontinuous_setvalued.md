title: Lower semicontinuous set-valued map
term: lower semicontinuous
applies_to: set-valued maps
synonyms: [lower hemicontinuous]
levels:
  graduate:
    context: set-valued analysis (Berge)
    text: |-
      Consider a [set-valued map](/dictionary/definitions/set-valued-map) $F : X \rightrightarrows Y$ and a point $x$.
      The map $F$ is _lower semicontinuous_ at $x$ if for every [open set](/dictionary/definitions/open-set) $U$ that intersects $F(x)$, there is a [neighborhood](/dictionary/definitions/neighborhood) $V$ of $x$ such that $F(y)$ intersects $U$ for all $y \in V$.
