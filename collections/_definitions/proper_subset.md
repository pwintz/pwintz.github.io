title: Proper subset
term: proper subset
applies_to: pairs of sets
synonyms: [$\subsetneq$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A set $A$ is a _proper subset_ of a set $B$ if everything in $A$ is also in $B$, and $B$ has at least one thing that $A$ does not.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      $A$ is a _proper subset_ of $B$ if $A \subseteq B$ and $A \neq B$, so $B$ has at least one element that is not in $A$.
