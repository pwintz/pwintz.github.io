---
title: Proper subset
term: proper subset
applies_to: pairs of sets
synonyms: [$\subsetneq$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of two sets, $A$ and $B$, where everything in $A$ is also in $B$.
      The set $A$ is a _proper subset_ of $B$ if $B$ has at least one thing that $A$ does not.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider two sets $A$ and $B$ such that $A \subset B$.
      The set $A$ is a _proper subset_ of $B$ if $A \neq B$, so $B$ has at least one element that is not in $A$.
