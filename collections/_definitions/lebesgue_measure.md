levels:

    text: |-
      The _Lebesgue measure_ assigns a size to (measurable) [subsets](/definitions/subset) of $\reals^n$, extending length ($n = 1$), area ($n = 2$), and volume ($n = 3$) to complicated sets. More generally, a _measure_ is a countably additive function from a collection of sets to $[0, \infty]$.
