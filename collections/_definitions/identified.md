title: Identified
term: identified
applies_to: any objects
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider two objects that are not literally equal, but correspond to each other through a natural bijection.
      The objects are _identified_ when we agree to treat them as the same.
  graduate:
    context: topology
    text: |-
      Consider some points of a topological space that we want to treat as one point.
      To _identify_ those points is to pass to a quotient space in which they become a single point.
