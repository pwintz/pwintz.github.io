title: Identified
term: identified
applies_to: any objects
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Two objects that are not literally equal are _identified_ when we agree to treat them as the same, usually via a natural bijection.
  graduate:
    context: topology
    text: |-
      To _identify_ points of a space is to pass to a quotient space in which they become a single point.
