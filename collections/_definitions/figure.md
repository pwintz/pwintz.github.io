levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a book or an article with pictures in it.
      A _figure_ is a numbered picture in the book or article, with a caption that explains it.
  high-school:
    context: documents
    text: |-
      Consider a document, such as a report or a paper, that contains illustrations.
      A _figure_ is a numbered, captioned element of the document that contains an image, plot, diagram, or other illustration, as in "see Figure 3."
---
In geometry, a "figure" is a shape, such as a triangle.
That is a different meaning.
