---
title: Bernoulli distribution
term: Bernoulli distribution
applies_to: random variables
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a random experiment that either succeeds or fails, such as a coin flip that succeeds if the coin lands heads.
      A [random variable](/dictionary/definitions/random-variable) $X$ that equals $1$ for success and $0$ for failure has a _Bernoulli distribution_ with success [probability](/dictionary/definitions/probability) $p$ if

      $$P(X = 1) = p \quad \text{and} \quad P(X = 0) = 1 - p.$$
properties:
  - >-
    A Bernoulli [random variable](/dictionary/definitions/random-variable) with success [probability](/dictionary/definitions/probability) $p$ has [mean](/dictionary/definitions/expected-value) $p$ and [variance](/dictionary/definitions/variance) $p (1 - p)$.
