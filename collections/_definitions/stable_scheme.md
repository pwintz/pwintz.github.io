title: Stable numerical scheme
term: stable (scheme)
applies_to: numerical schemes
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A numerical method for a differential equation is _stable_ if small errors, such as rounding errors, do not grow out of control as it takes more steps.
  undergraduate:
    context: numerical analysis
    text: |-
      A numerical scheme is _stable_ if errors introduced at one step (from rounding or from the data) are not amplified without bound as the computation proceeds, uniformly as the step sizes tend to zero (over a fixed time interval).
---
This is different from the stability of an [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) of a dynamical system, though the two are related.
