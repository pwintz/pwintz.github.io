---
title: Prepend
term: prepend
applies_to: lists
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      To _prepend_ something to a list is to add it at the beginning.
  undergraduate:
    context: collections and data structures
    text: |-
      To _prepend_ $x$ to a list $(a_1, \dots, a_n)$ is to form the list $(x, a_1, \dots, a_n)$, with $x$ added at the beginning.
---
Compare [append](/definitions/append), which adds an element at the end.
