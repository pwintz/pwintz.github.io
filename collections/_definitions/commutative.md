---
title: Commutative operation
term: commutative
applies_to: binary operations
synonyms: [commutativity]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A way of combining two numbers is _commutative_ if the order of the numbers doesn't matter, such as $2 + 3 = 3 + 2$.
  undergraduate:
    context: abstract algebra
    text: |-
      Consider a set $S$ with a [binary operation](/dictionary/definitions/binary-operation) $\ast$.
      The operation $\ast$ is _commutative_ if

      $$a \ast b = b \ast a \quad \text{for all } a, b \in S.$$
properties:
  - >-
    Addition and multiplication of [real numbers](/dictionary/definitions/real-number) are commutative.
  - >-
    Subtraction, division, [composition](/dictionary/definitions/function-composition) of functions, and multiplication of matrices are not commutative: for example, $5 - 3 = 2$, but $3 - 5 = -2$.
  - >-
    A [group](/dictionary/definitions/group) whose operation is commutative is called _abelian_.
---
