---
title: Equivalence relation
term: equivalence relation
applies_to: relations
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a set $X$ and a [relation](/definitions/relation) $\sim$ on $X$.
      The relation $\sim$ is an _equivalence relation_ if, for all $a, b, c \in X$,

      1. $a \sim a$ ([reflexive](/definitions/reflexive-relation)),
      2. $a \sim b$ implies $b \sim a$ ([symmetric](/definitions/symmetric-relation)), and
      3. $a \sim b$ and $b \sim c$ imply $a \sim c$ ([transitive](/definitions/transitive-relation)).
properties:
  - >-
    An equivalence relation splits $X$ into disjoint _equivalence classes_ $[a] = \setdef{b \in X \suchthat b \sim a}$, which form a [partition](/definitions/set-partition) of $X$, and every partition comes from an equivalence relation in this way.
