title: Convergent sequence
term: convergent
applies_to: sequences
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A list of numbers that goes on forever is _convergent_ if its numbers get closer and closer to one particular number.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A [sequence](/definitions/sequence) is _convergent_ if its terms approach a single finite number $L$, its limit.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A [sequence](/definitions/sequence) $(a_n)$ in a [metric space](/definitions/metric-space) is _convergent_, and _converges_ to $L$, if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $N$ such that $d(a_n, L) < \varepsilon$ for all $n \geq N$.
