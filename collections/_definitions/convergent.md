title: Convergent sequence
term: convergent
applies_to: sequences
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a list of numbers that goes on forever.
      The list is _convergent_ if its numbers get closer and closer to one particular number.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a [sequence](/definitions/sequence) of numbers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ The sequence is _convergent_ if its terms approach a single finite number $L$, its limit.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a [sequence](/definitions/sequence) $(a_n)$ in a [metric space](/definitions/metric-space) with [metric](/definitions/metric) $d$.
      The sequence is _convergent_, and _converges_ to $L$, if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $N$ such that

      $$d(a_n, L) < \varepsilon \quad \text{for all } n \geq N.$$
