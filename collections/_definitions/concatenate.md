title: Concatenate
term: concatenate
applies_to: lists and strings
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of two lists, one written after the other.
      To _concatenate_ the two lists is to put the second list right after the first, making one longer list.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider two lists (or strings), $a = (a_1, \dots, a_m)$ and $b = (b_1, \dots, b_n)$.
      To _concatenate_ $a$ and $b$ is to join $a$ and $b$ end to end, giving $(a_1, \dots, a_m, b_1, \dots, b_n)$.
properties:
  - >-
    Concatenation is associative but not commutative, and, unlike [union](/definitions/set-union), it keeps repeated elements.
