title: Concatenate
term: concatenate
applies_to: lists and strings
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      To _concatenate_ two lists is to put the second list right after the first, making one longer list.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      To _concatenate_ two lists (or strings) is to join them end to end. The concatenation of $(a_1, \dots, a_m)$ and $(b_1, \dots, b_n)$ is $(a_1, \dots, a_m, b_1, \dots, b_n)$.
properties:
  - >-
    Concatenation is associative but not commutative, and, unlike [union](/definitions/set-union), it keeps repeated elements.
