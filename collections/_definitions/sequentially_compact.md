title: Sequentially compact
term: sequentially compact
applies_to: topological spaces
levels:
  graduate:
    context: topological spaces
    text: |-
      A space is _sequentially compact_ if every [sequence](/definitions/sequence) in it has a convergent subsequence.
properties:
  - >-
    For [metric spaces](/definitions/metric-space), sequential compactness is equivalent to compactness. For general topological spaces, neither implies the other.
