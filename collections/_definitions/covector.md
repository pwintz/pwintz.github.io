---
title: Covector
term: covector
applies_to: vector spaces
synonyms: [linear functional, dual vector, one-form]
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      A _covector_ is a rule that takes a [vector](/definitions/vector) and returns a number, in a way that respects adding vectors and scaling them.
      In coordinates, it is written as a row of numbers, which multiplies a column vector.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a [vector](/definitions/vector) space $V$ and its scalars.
      A _covector_ on $V$ is a [linear map](/definitions/linear-map) from $V$ to its scalars; an element of the dual space $V^{\ast}$.
      In coordinates, covectors are often written as row vectors, which act on column vectors by [matrix](/definitions/matrix) multiplication.
  graduate:
    context: differential geometry
    text: |-
      Consider a manifold $M$ and a point $x$ of $M$, with its cotangent space $T_x^{\ast} M$.
      A _covector_ at $x$ is an element of $T_x^{\ast} M$.
      The [differential](/definitions/differential) $\mathrm{d}f$ of a function is a covector field (a one-form).
---
