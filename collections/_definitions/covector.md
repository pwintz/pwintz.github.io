title: Covector
term: covector
applies_to: vector spaces
synonyms: [linear functional, dual vector, one-form]
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      A _covector_ is a [linear map](/definitions/linear-map) from a [vector](/definitions/vector) space $V$ to its scalars; an element of the dual space $V^{\ast}$. In coordinates, covectors are often written as row vectors, which act on column vectors by [matrix](/definitions/matrix) multiplication.
  graduate:
    context: differential geometry
    text: |-
      A _covector_ at a point $x$ of a manifold $M$ is an element of the cotangent space $T_x^{\ast} M$. The [differential](/definitions/differential) $\mathrm{d}f$ of a function is a covector field (a one-form).
