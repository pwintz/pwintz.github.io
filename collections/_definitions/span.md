title: Span
term: span
applies_to: sets of vectors
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a list of [vectors](/definitions/vector) $v_1, \dots, v_k$.
      The _span_ of the vectors is the set of all vectors that can be built from them by stretching and adding.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider vectors $v_1, \dots, v_k$ in a vector space $V$.
      The _span_ of the vectors is the set of their linear combinations:

      $$\operatorname{span}(v_1, \dots, v_k) = \setdef{c_1 v_1 + \dots + c_k v_k \suchthat c_1, \dots, c_k \text{ scalars}}.$$

      The vectors _span_ $V$ if their span is all of $V$.
properties:
  - >-
    The span of any vectors is a subspace, the smallest subspace that contains them.
