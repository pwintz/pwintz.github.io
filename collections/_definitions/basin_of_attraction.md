title: Basin of attraction
term: basin of attraction
applies_to: attractors
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _basin of attraction_ of an [attractor](/definitions/attractor) is the set of starting states from which the system eventually settles into it.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The _basin of attraction_ of an [attractor](/definitions/attractor) $A$ is the set of all initial states whose solutions converge to $A$.
---
For an [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium), the basin of attraction and the [region of attraction](/definitions/region-of-attraction) are the same set. "Basin" is more common in dynamical systems, and "region" in control.
