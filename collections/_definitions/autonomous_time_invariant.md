title: Autonomous system (time-invariant)
term: autonomous (time-invariant)
applies_to: dynamical systems
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      In one convention, a system is _autonomous_ if the rules for how it changes do not depend on the time on the clock.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      In one convention, a system is _autonomous_ if its equations do not depend explicitly on time, as in $\dot{x} = f(x)$.
---
This convention is common in the theory of ordinary differential equations. Compare [autonomous (no input)](/definitions/autonomous-no-input).
