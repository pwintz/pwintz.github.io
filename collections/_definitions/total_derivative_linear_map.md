term: total derivative (linear map)

levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      In multivariable calculus, the _total derivative_ of $f : \reals^n \to \reals^m$ at $x$ is the [linear map](/definitions/linear-map) $Df(x)$ that best approximates $f(x + h) - f(x)$ for small $h$. Its [matrix](/definitions/matrix) is the [Jacobian](/definitions/jacobian).
---
This is the same concept as the [differential](/definitions/differential). See [the meanings of "total derivative"](/distinctions/total-derivative).
