---
title: Total derivative (linear map)
term: total derivative (linear map)
applies_to: functions of several variables
synonyms: [differential, Fréchet derivative, $Df(x)$]
levels:
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      In multivariable calculus, the _total derivative_ of $f : \reals^n \to \reals^m$ at $x$ is the [linear map](/dictionary/definitions/linear-map) $Df(x)$ that best approximates $f(x + h) - f(x)$ for small $h$. 
---
The [matrix](/dictionary/definitions/matrix) of a total derivative is the [Jacobian](/dictionary/definitions/jacobian) matrix.

This is the same concept as the [differential](/dictionary/definitions/differential). See [the meanings of "total derivative"](/dictionary/distinctions/total-derivative).
