title: Standing Wave
term: standing wave
applies_to: waves
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      A _standing wave_ is a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) that vibrates in place instead of moving along, such as the vibration of a plucked guitar string.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a medium, such as a string held at both ends, in which a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) oscillates.
      The wave is a _standing wave_ if the shape of the wave stays in place and only grows and shrinks over time, with _nodes_, at which the medium does not move, and _antinodes_, at which the medium oscillates with the largest [amplitude](/dictionary/definitions/amplitude).
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      Consider a [wave](/dictionary/definitions/wave) $u(x, t)$ in a medium.
      The wave $u$ is a _standing wave_ if $u$ has the form

      $$u(x, t) = X(x) \cos(\omega t + \phi)$$

      for a function $X$ of position and constants $\omega$ and $\phi$.
properties:
  - |-
    The sum of two [waves](/dictionary/definitions/wave) with the same [amplitude](/dictionary/definitions/amplitude) and [frequency](/dictionary/definitions/frequency-oscillation) that travel in opposite directions is a standing wave:

    $$A \cos(k x - \omega t) + A \cos(k x + \omega t) = 2 A \cos(k x) \cos(\omega t).$$
  - |-
    Neighboring nodes of a sinusoidal standing wave are half a [wavelength](/dictionary/definitions/wavelength) apart.
  - |-
    A string of length $L$ that is held at both ends has standing waves only with the [wavelengths](/dictionary/definitions/wavelength) $2 L / n$ for [positive integers](/dictionary/definitions/natural-numbers-from-one) $n$, which set the pitches of the string.
