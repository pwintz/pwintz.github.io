title: Edge of a graph
term: edge (of a graph)
applies_to: graphs (networks)
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      An _edge_ of a network is a line connecting two of its dots.
  undergraduate:
    context: graph theory
    text: |-
      An _edge_ of a graph is an element of its edge set. In a simple undirected graph, an edge is an unordered pair $\lbrace u, v \rbrace$ of distinct vertices.
---
A graph edge has no position or length. Drawing it as a line segment is only a convenience.
