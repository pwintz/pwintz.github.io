---
title: Zeno solution
term: Zeno (solution)
applies_to: solutions of hybrid systems
levels:
  graduate:
    text: |-
      A solution of a [hybrid system](/definitions/hybrid-system) is _Zeno_ if it has infinitely many jumps while its ordinary time stays [bounded](/definitions/bounded).
---

In a Zeno solution, the time between jumps approaches zero, meaning the system jumps infinitely often jumps infinitely often in a finite amount of time.

Conversely, a solution is not necessarily Zeno if the time between jumps. 
For example, if the time between jumps is given by the harmonic sequence

$$1,~1/2,~1/3,~1/4,~1/5,~\dots$$

the ordinary time approaches infinity because the harmonic sequence diverges:

$$
1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/4 + 1/5 + \dots = \infty
$$
