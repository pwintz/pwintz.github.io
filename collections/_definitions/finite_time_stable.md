title: Finite-time stable equilibrium
term: finite-time stable
applies_to: equilibria
levels:
  high-school:
    text: |-
      An [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) is _finite-time stable_ if solutions that start near it stay near it and actually reach it in a finite amount of time, rather than only getting closer and closer.
  graduate:
    text: |-
      An [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) is _finite-time stable_ if it is stable and every solution starting near it reaches it exactly, at a finite time that may depend on the initial state, and stays there.
properties:
  - >-
    Finite-time stability requires a [vector](/definitions/vector) field that is not [Lipschitz continuous](/definitions/lipschitz-continuous) at the [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium), because solutions of a Lipschitz system cannot reach an equilibrium in finite time.
---
If the settling time is [bounded](/definitions/bounded) independently of the initial state, the [equilibrium](/definitions/equilibrium) is _fixed-time_ stable.
