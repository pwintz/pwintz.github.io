title: Area
term: area
applies_to: regions and surfaces
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a rectangle drawn on paper, with a base and a height.
      The _area_ of the rectangle is the product of its base times its height.

    # The _area_ of other shapes is the amount of flat surface it covers, measured in square units.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider a region in the plane, or a surface in space.
      Its _area_ is its two-dimensional size.
      The area of a surface in space, such as a [sphere](/definitions/sphere), is called its _surface area_.
