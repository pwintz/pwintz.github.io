---
title: Zero (the number)
term: zero
applies_to: numbers
synonyms: [$0$]
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Consider a box that could contain some items. If there are no items in the box, then number of items is _zero_.
  undergraduate:
    text: |-
      The number zero is the additive identity of real and complex numbers.
properties:
  - >-
    $a + 0 = a$ and $a \cdot 0 = 0$ for every number $a$. Division by $0$ is undefined.
---
