---
title: Displacement
term: displacement
applies_to: positions
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      Think of a moving object, like a [ball](/dictionary/definitions/ball) rolling across a floor.
      The _displacement_ of the object is how far, and in which direction, it ended up from where it started.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      Consider an object that moved from a starting point $P$ to an ending point $Q$.
      The _displacement_ of the object is its change in position, as a [vector](/dictionary/definitions/vector) from $P$ to $Q$.
properties:
  - >-
    The length of the displacement is the [straight-line distance](/dictionary/definitions/euclidean-distance) between the start and the end, which is at most the distance traveled along the path.
---
