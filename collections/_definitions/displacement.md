title: Displacement
term: displacement
applies_to: positions
levels:
  elementary:
    text: |-
      The _displacement_ of a moving object is how far, and in which direction, it ended up from where it started.
  high-school:
    text: |-
      The _displacement_ of a moving object is its change in position, as a [vector](/definitions/vector) from the starting point to the ending point.
properties:
  - >-
    The length of the displacement is the straight-line distance between the start and the end, which is at most the distance traveled along the path.
